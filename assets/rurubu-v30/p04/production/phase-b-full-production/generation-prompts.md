# P04 Phase B ImageGen prompts

Mode: built-in ImageGen. Every accepted source was generated as opaque RGB on an extraction-only violet field, then normalized to an exact flat `#6C00A8` matte before production RGBA extraction.

## Common production contract

- Japanese travel-wedding magazine / bright Rurubu-like printed sticker and cutout rendering.
- High saturation: hot pink, vivid blue/cyan, yellow, green, white and dark navy.
- Strong dark-navy outline, white/yellow keyline and controlled dimensional shadow.
- Entire canvas outside the isolated asset uses only extraction matte `#6C00A8`.
- No checkerboard, transparent-background simulation, purple artwork, watercolor, painterly botanical, aged parchment, sepia, retro poster, Canva icon pack, SaaS illustration, logo or watermark.
- Preserve all intended white/yellow keylines, navy outlines, text, flowers, leaves, ribbon/paper and object interiors as opaque artwork.

## Accepted asset prompts

### P04_LOCATION_LABEL_OKINAWA

Create one compact irregular white travel-ticket sticker. Render exactly `OKINAWA` on line 1 and `沖縄` on line 2 in vivid blue. Integrate a small hot-pink hibiscus and tropical greenery. Keep the asset horizontal, centered and isolated with generous matte margin.

### P04_LOCATION_LABEL_KOREA

Create one compact irregular white travel-ticket sticker. Render exactly `KOREA` on line 1 and `韓国` on line 2 in hot pink. Integrate one hot-pink heart and a small bright blossom. Keep the asset horizontal, centered and isolated with generous matte margin.

### P04_LOCATION_LABEL_HAWAII

Create one compact irregular white travel-ticket sticker. Render exactly `HAWAII` on line 1 and `ハワイ` on line 2 in fresh green. Integrate one white-yellow plumeria and two green leaves. Keep the asset horizontal, centered and isolated with generous matte margin.

### P04_TRAVEL_ROUTE_CONNECTOR

Create one restrained editorial travel-route ornament: a vivid sky-blue dashed curving flight path with two gentle loops, ending in a small glossy blue airplane at far right and one tiny hot-pink heart. Use a white keyline and navy edge. No text, nodes, pins, boxes, map, arrows, flowchart or roadmap treatment. Keep it wide and shallow.

### P04_BOTTOM_CLOSURE_ECOLOGY

Create one coherent lower-left tropical closure ecology integrating a circular blue postal stamp reading exactly `MEMORIES` and `AROUND THE WORLD`, a small white island chapel with vivid-blue roof, hot-pink hibiscus, white-yellow plumeria, glossy tropical leaves, palms, turquoise sea, cyan wave, beach and low green island horizon. Keep it bright, crisp, printed and cutout-like; no scattered clipart and no watercolor chapel.

### P04_MESSAGE_PANEL — accepted V2

Create one wide horizontal 4:3 warm-white paper note vessel, much wider than tall. Use clean softly torn edges, small blue dotted corner accents, two short yellow marker underline strokes near lower-left and one small hot-pink heart outside lower-right. Leave the central 80% blank for native Japanese text. Absolutely no letters, numbers, symbols, faux handwriting or placeholder copy.

V1 was rejected before placement because it was portrait-oriented and did not fit the Visual Master message quiet zone. It remains only as raw iteration evidence.

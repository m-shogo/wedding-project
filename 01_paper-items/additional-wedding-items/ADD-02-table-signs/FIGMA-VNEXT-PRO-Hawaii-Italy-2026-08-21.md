## What was learned

`VERIFIED_LOCAL` within ADD-02:

- family resemblance can be carried by **confidence, scale, strong destination color behavior and movement**, while layout geometry remains materially different by country;
- forcing the same edge/spine/sun construction onto a second destination immediately starts to feel templated, even when the palette changes;
- long-copy QA must include **decoration-to-copy collision**, not only root overflow. Hawaii A passed containment before the lagoon sweep was moved, but the screenshot still showed a real visual collision.

Do not transfer Hawaii/Italy colors, exact shapes, coordinates or motifs to other items.

## Why another premise is required

Fresh Spain review continues to show that Current is not sellable enough at the reopened visual gate:

- isolated `TILE A–E` rectangles read as UI/content modules rather than courtyard/ceramic material;
- the huge cobalt/saffron vertical rail has too much mass for its reader-facing/physical job;
- the overall skeleton is `header → sparse field → rail → lower module`, which reads like a system/layout exercise;
- placeholder copy is not the cause.

The next direction therefore must not be another tile arrangement, arch, vertical rail, or layered paper module.

## External professional/source grounding

Only neutral destination/lifestyle principles are consumed; no tourism logo or campaign layout is copied.

Official Spain tourism sources reviewed before choosing the premise:

- `https://www.spain.info/en/top/tardeo-spain/` — `tardeo` frames late afternoon as a social period where the rhythm of the city changes and people share food, drinks and conversation;
- `https://www.spain.info/en/world-tapas-day/` — tapas are described as sharing, conversation and connection, not merely food styling;
- `https://www.spain.info/en/top/spain-happy-lifestyle/` — outdoor life, sunlight, walking and lively streets are recurring Spanish lifestyle cues;
- `https://www.spain.info/en/40-anniversary/` — official tourism identity explicitly associates yellow with sunlight/joy and red with passion; these are treated only as high-level color semantics, not as permission to reproduce the Sol de Miró mark or its composition.

The item-specific translation is: **Spain should feel like the social lift of late afternoon before evening, not a collection of tourist symbols.**

## Pre-Figma method-switch experiments

Three materially different pre-Figma composition hypotheses were tested as blank-origin proxies. None used Current layout geometry.

### A — SUNSET WAVE FIELD — `REJECTED_PRE_FIGMA`

A large warm/cool flowing fixed-art field was tested as an atmosphere-first direction.

Why rejected:

- the waves became the first read;
- the result could belong to almost any sunny destination;
- it repeated the generic bright-shape grammar already rejected elsewhere in the suite;
- destination specificity did not rise enough to justify the fixed-art mass.

### B — TERRACE AWNING SHADOW — `REJECTED_PRE_FIGMA`

A physically connected awning/cast-shadow fixed-art direction was tested to express outdoor Spanish social life.

Why rejected:

- the cast shadow consumed too much of the copy-safe field;
- the canopy became a literal restaurant/café illustration rather than an editorial table sign;
- the fixed art again dominated the semantic copy;
- it risked looking like themed restaurant branding rather than wedding stationery.

### C — TARDEO TYPE SHEET — `SELECTED_PRE_FIGMA`

The strongest pre-Figma direction removes decorative fixed art from the initial build and makes **native typography, table number, open paper and late-afternoon color tension** the art direction.

The central premise is not “Spain = red/yellow icons.” It is **a late-afternoon editorial sheet whose energy comes from scale, asymmetry and open conversation space**.

This is a deliberate method switch from both Current and the rejected fixed-art experiments.

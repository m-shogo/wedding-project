## Family-scale result

### What improved

The top V3 row no longer reads as one rounded-shape system with color changes.

The strongest diversity gains come from:

- SPAIN: red/cream courtyard-color skeleton with lower irregular floor rhythm;
- TAIWAN: diagonal island-weave skeleton with open lower copy field;
- FRANCE: type-dominant broadside with offset press sheet / lower butter field.

These three prove that destination/material specificity can coexist with materially different mass distribution.

### What still fails the aspiration bar

HAWAII, JAPAN and ITALY remain too structurally related at family scale. Although their material vocabularies differ, they still converge on strong edge-field / upper-title compositions and feel more muted than the current production row.

The current production family is visually more immediately celebratory, but it also makes the known repeated large-circle / rounded-bar / capsule grammar obvious when viewed together.

The V3 family has improved editorial specificity and reduced obvious AI-template geometry, but the six-anchor set is **not yet uniformly stronger** on excitement, warmth and family rhythm.

## Decision

`CURRENT_PRODUCTION_RETAINED`.

Do not bulk-roll V3 to all eleven signs and do not promote the three strongest V3 anchors in isolation yet.

The next high-value work is not a seventh destination by default. First repair at least one of the weaker V3 anchors (HAWAII/JAPAN/ITALY) from a new blank-frame skeleton so the method proves it can improve both destination specificity **and** celebration energy across different concepts.

After that, rebuild the same-scale family board and judge whether the top row has a convincing rhythm of theme + variation rather than six unrelated posters or a new template.

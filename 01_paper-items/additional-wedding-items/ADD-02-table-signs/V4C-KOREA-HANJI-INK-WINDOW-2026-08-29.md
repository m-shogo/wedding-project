## V4C art direction — HANJI INK WINDOW

The candidate uses:

- deep ink upper field and warm hanji-like paper lower field;
- an offset physical paper-window cue with indigo wash and sparse lattice;
- layered organic ink-brush strokes rather than broad diagonal vector planes;
- translucent irregular hanji scraps / patch forms in muted mineral colors;
- one sparse red brush interruption, not a badge or seal;
- no fake Hangul, no flag imitation, no taegeuk imitation, no K-pop/neon motif, and no generic UI/card system.

The first V4C screenshot exposed that the initial polygon window plus circular mineral forms read too much like a geometric badge. That treatment was removed in the same run.

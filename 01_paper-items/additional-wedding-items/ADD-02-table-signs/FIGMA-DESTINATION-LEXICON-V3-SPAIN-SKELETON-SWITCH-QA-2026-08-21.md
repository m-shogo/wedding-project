## Why a new method was required

A same-scale HAWAII/JAPAN/ITALY V3 review showed that material diversity alone could still converge on one layout skeleton, especially a strong upper destination title plus edge-field composition. The next experiment therefore had to change both:

1. the place/material vocabulary;
2. the mass distribution / layout skeleton;

while explicitly recovering stronger celebration energy.

The current production Spain sign was not used as an authoring source. Only verified facts/constraints were carried: 1000×1480, SPAIN / スペイン, TABLE 04, native theme headline/description roles and 2026.10.24.

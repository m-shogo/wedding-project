## V5 fixed-art concept

`DAYLIGHT SWEEP` uses one large off-frame warm daylight sweep at the top and one quieter dark/cyan terminal movement at the lower-right. It intentionally avoids:

- route/map lines;
- event-by-event nodes;
- repeated cards/modules;
- fake airline credentials;
- badges, stamps, pill labels, barcode, plane icons;
- decorative English copy.

The left edge remains a simple physical/editorial binding field rather than a web-navigation rail. The large cream center stays open for native chronology.

## Expected improvement

Compared with previous rejected route/fold studies, V5 should:

1. increase emotional energy at thumbnail scale without becoming a dashboard;
2. leave chronology to typography rather than an infographic;
3. keep one strong gesture instead of several unrelated travel motifs;
4. retain credible printed-program negative space at actual size.

## Regression risks / rejection conditions

Reject V5 if Figma assembly shows any of the following:

- the daylight sweep reads as a generic event-poster wave;
- fixed art competes with the Japanese headline or event times;
- the left binding field reads like an app sidebar;
- the lower-right movement feels like decorative filler rather than a terminal/closing counterweight;
- long-copy forces text into the warm sweep or dark terminal field;
- the whole-item result is more exciting but less clearly a timetable/program.

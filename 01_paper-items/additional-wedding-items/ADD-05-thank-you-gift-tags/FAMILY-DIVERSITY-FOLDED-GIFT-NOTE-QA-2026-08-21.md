## Visible family-scale issue

The existing `WAVE SOUVENIR` is individually strong, but its dominant grammar is an oversized navy organic wave plus coral sweep plus lagoon circle. - FOLDED GIFT NOTE: strongest physical-object distinction. It reads as a tiny wrapped note attached to the gift rather than another poster-like colored field. Selected for full family test.

## Why another clean-room pass was necessary

The previous selected `WAVE SOUVENIR` was individually strong, but its dominant navy organic wave + coral sweep + lagoon circle repeated the suite-level large-rounded-shape grammar captured by the promoted `FAMILY_SCALE_TEMPLATE_REPETITION` rule.

The first family-diversity replacement, `FOLDED GIFT NOTE`, was intentionally not promoted. Whole-item review showed that although it improved family diversity, its small inset paper read too quiet/minimal for the current travel / celebration / pop brief and still required a real-gift attachment judgment.

Rather than polishing that V2 again, this run performed a method switch and created three new blank-frame V3 directions from confirmed facts only.

## Blank-frame V3 directions

Created without copying existing production or the prior family-diversity candidate:

- `30:2 / GIFT WRAP BAND` — vertical wrapping-paper band with centered message lane.
- `30:10 / CLIPPED LUGGAGE LABEL` — strong top cap / side index physical-tag grammar.
- `30:23 / RIBBON FOLD` — gift ribbon crossing warm paper and a dark return field.

Council critique:

- GIFT WRAP BAND: physical and distinct, but too retail-packaging / restrained for the wedding suite.
- CLIPPED LUGGAGE LABEL: readable, but the top/side/footer segmentation started to resemble a system label rather than a celebratory keepsake.
- RIBBON FOLD: strongest balance of gift-specific physical meaning, readable copy, bold color, asymmetry and family diversity without airline cosplay or generic tropical decoration.

`RIBBON FOLD` was selected and rebuilt as fresh full-size production candidates rather than duplicating the study.

## Comparison decision

After the V3 candidate was mature, the retained `WAVE SOUVENIR` and prior `FOLDED GIFT NOTE` were compared only as completion references.

Decision: promote `RIBBON FOLD`.

Reasons:

1. preserves the stronger pop/color energy of the reopened brief better than the quiet inset-paper V2;
2. removes the repeated oversized rounded-wave/orb grammar of the previous selected design;
3. uses an item-specific physical metaphor — gift ribbon / folded wrapping paper — rather than a generic travel poster treatment;
4. retains immediate message readability at both tag sizes;
5. keeps punch geometry and all confirmed copy editable and production-separated.

## Current visual direction

Emotional brief: `育ててもらった時間を、これからの暮らしへ持っていく。`

A later family-scale audit found a higher-order suite repetition: after abstract circles/capsules were removed, several unrelated Current items had independently converged on otherwise-successful letter/envelope/paper-insert metaphors. The previous RETURN LETTER HOME remained attractive in isolation, but ADD-16 had a weaker functional claim to the letter metaphor than items whose primary action is actually writing/sending a note.

The selected direction treats ADD-16 as a tactile `HOME TEXTILE MAT` keepsake instead:

- dark forest selvage as the main physical anchor;
- warm oat paper field;
- rust + saffron short weave bands;
- one thread-like vertical rule;
- Japanese-first `育ててくれた時間を、これからの力に。`;
- open native message/signature roles on the front;
- open writing surface with functional rules on the reverse;
- no envelope flap, paper-insert construction, giant circle/capsule, fake airline credential, barcode, route UI, tropical clip-art or generated family/person imagery.

The metaphor is intentionally tied to `home / upbringing / keepsake / carrying something into the next life stage`, not reused because another paper-object style worked elsewhere.

All message/date/signature/guide roles remain native editable text.

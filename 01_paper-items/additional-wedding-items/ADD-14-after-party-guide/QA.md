## Current art direction

`VELVET LETTER` treats the second-party invitation as a late-night note slipped from a velvet sleeve rather than another flight/afterglow system.

A6 uses a plum outer sleeve, pale warm letter paper, sky-blue paper edge, apricot date fold and restrained sage RSVP support. Japanese `もう少しだけ、一緒に。` is the first read. Venue/address/access, reception/start/end, fee/contact, notice and RSVP remain native semantic placeholders.

A5 is an independent reflow, not a scaled A6. It preserves the letter/sleeve material idea while using a wider editorial hierarchy appropriate to `840×592`.

This direction replaces the prior cream/deep-ocean + coral-circle + mint-sweep vocabulary, which remained individually strong but contributed to suite-level motif repetition.

## Visible problem on the retained Current

Retained `VELVET LETTER` remained structurally healthy, but live whole-item/actual-size screenshots exposed a new visual-quality defect under the reopened professional gate:

- A6/A5 relied on one large pale letter field inside a plum sleeve;
- the lower half became visually under-authored at thumbnail scale;
- the visible sage RSVP rectangle read like a web/form button rather than print/event language;
- the result was tasteful but too quiet for the current `travel / pop / fun / excitement` brief and for a second-party invitation whose job is to raise energy after the reception.

This is not a correctness failure. It is a renewed art-direction judgment: if presented today as a fresh proposal, the retained Current would no longer be the strongest option.

## Professional reference learning applied

A bounded nightlife/event-poster research pass was used only as neutral design learning, not as visual copying.

Transferable principles applied:

- event communication should capture the *energy and mood of the night*, rather than defaulting to the same nightlife clichés;
- typographic weight, repetition, strict grids and one deliberately distorted/offset element can create physical poster energy without UI containers;
- a successful event poster should feel worth keeping, not merely informational.

No external artwork, identity, layout, palette or protected brand asset was copied.

## New art direction — MIDNIGHT ZINE

Emotional brief:

`披露宴が終わっても、もう少し遊びたい。その夜だけの小さなイベントポスターを手渡す。`

First read:

`まだ帰らない、夜のつづきへ。`

Visual system:

- cobalt left editorial column carrying meaningful `AFTER PARTY` identity;
- large Japanese display headline as the emotional carrier;
- warm-cream information field;
- hot-pink top print edge and yellow terminal edge as high-energy print marks;
- dark ink footer containing date and RSVP semantic role as typography, not a button;
- no QR box, rounded RSVP button, fake airline data, barcode, gate/class, stock nightlife imagery, gradient or shadow.

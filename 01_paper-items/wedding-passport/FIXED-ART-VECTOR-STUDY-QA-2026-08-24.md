## New professional design learning used

The research step was tightened with current high-quality primary design sources:

- Pentagram Books: book design must orchestrate typography, layout, printing and binding into a storytelling experience; the printed book has authority and endurance beyond a one-frame poster.
- Pentagram Travel + Leisure: strong travel editorial design opens layouts, foregrounds imagery/visual atmosphere, and keeps information hierarchy clear rather than simulating density with containers.
- Pentagram United Airlines: one strong travel gesture can carry emotional flight energy when it is used boldly rather than multiplied into travel clichés.
- Pentagram Impala: everyday travel vernacular can generate excitement, but literal ticket/signage language must remain appropriate to the artifact rather than becoming fake administrative UI.

Sources:
- https://www.pentagram.com/book-design
- https://www.pentagram.com/work/travel-leisure
- https://www.pentagram.com/work/united-airlines/story
- https://www.pentagram.com/work/impala/story

Transferable conclusion for this item: **increase travel energy through one dominant spatial gesture and publication rhythm while protecting the cover's keepsake-booklet reading.** Do not solve anticipation by adding more planes, stamps, badges or fake transport fields.

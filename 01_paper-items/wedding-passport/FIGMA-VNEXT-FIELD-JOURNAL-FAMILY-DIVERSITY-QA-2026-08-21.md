## Why the Passport reopened again

The previous professional vNext remained individually strong, but a later family-scale audit verified the cross-item failure fingerprint `FAMILY_SCALE_TEMPLATE_REPETITION`: deep navy + warm cream + coral/mint/yellow together with oversized circles/capsule sweeps had accumulated across unrelated wedding artifacts.

The Passport reproduced that same family-level risk. This did not invalidate its earlier long-copy/structure proof; it reopened only the family-fit/art-direction ceiling.

The retained prior current was inspected only for the family-level repetition diagnosis and final mature comparison. No previous visual node, layout, ornament, crop, palette construction, generated asset or component was used as the construction source for the new candidate.

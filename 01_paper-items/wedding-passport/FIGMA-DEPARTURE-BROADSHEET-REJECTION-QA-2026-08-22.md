## Why a new clean-room study was justified

A fresh live screenshot of the retained Current still reads coherently as a stitched field journal, but it is intentionally restrained. Under the current professional reset brief — travel / flight / Hawaii warmth / pop / excitement — it was worth testing whether a materially bolder typographic travel direction could raise emotional energy without falling back into the previously rejected rounded-sun/capsule family grammar.

The new direction was authored from a blank page. It did not duplicate or reuse old Current nodes, layout groups, decoration, crop, assets, or generated imagery. Only verified non-visual requirements were re-entered: `1480×2100`, passport/keepsake role, confirmed `2026.10.24`, `YOKOHAMA`, couple-name role, issue-information role, and editable native copy.

## External professional hypothesis consumed

Two travel-brand case studies informed the hypothesis, not the literal layout:

- Pentagram / United Airlines: a genuine identity element can become bigger, bolder and more expressive, including sweeping beyond normal bounds, instead of accumulating many small aviation clichés. `https://www.pentagram.com/work/united-airlines`
- Pentagram / Impala: everyday travel vernacular can carry anticipation and excitement when it remains bold, colourful and ownable rather than tech/admin dominated. `https://www.pentagram.com/work/impala/story`

Hypothesis tested: **increase emotional travel energy through one strong factual/typographic gesture rather than adding fake flight data, stamps, badges, barcodes or generic tropical imagery.**

## Screenshot QA

### Whole / thumbnail

Result: `MIXED / REJECT`.

The new front is clearly more energetic than the retained FIELD JOURNAL and the oversized `10.24` creates a stronger first glance. However, the composition reads primarily as a contemporary event/travel poster rather than a passport/keepsake object. The travel energy is present, but item specificity is weaker.

The back remains readable but similarly behaves as an event broadside. The relationship between the cream story sheet, date cluster and large closing line is strong enough as a poster, but not strong enough to justify replacing a deliberately book/keepsake-specific Current.

### Reading scale

Result: `PASS TYPOGRAPHY / REJECT ARTIFACT IDENTITY`.

Japanese hierarchy is clear and native. No airport-admin UI or fake credential language appears. The design nevertheless loses too much of the physical Passport identity that made the retained Current distinct from Boarding Pass, signs and other flat wedding collateral.

### Actual-size/detail

Front was reviewed at an `1800 px` long-edge render from the native `1480×2100` canvas. Type remains legible and stable; the rejection is not a rendering defect.

A visible secondary weakness also appeared: the small English footer `OUR JOURNEY STARTS HERE` becomes low-value decorative filler at actual scale. Because the entire direction was rejected, this was not cosmetically repaired and promoted; the study is retained as rejection evidence instead.

## Learning

`VERIFIED_LOCAL → CROSS_ITEM_CANDIDATE`:

**Travel-energy uplift must not erase artifact-role specificity.** A stronger colour/typographic travel vernacular can raise excitement while still being the wrong promotion if the result reads as a generic event/travel poster rather than the specific physical object the item is meant to be.

Transfer the decision method, not this palette/layout. A future receiving item should compare `emotional energy gain` and `artifact-role specificity` as separate criteria rather than letting one substitute for the other.

## Shared art direction

Editorial mood:
- premium Japanese Hawaii travel special
- bright but not childish
- resort warmth, water, natural materials, editorial detail
- realistic photography rather than fantasy advertising
- coherent set with controlled variation in time of day and camera distance

Palette guidance:
- warm white / ivory
- sand beige
- deep ocean blue
- clear aqua
- coral or hibiscus pink accent
- sunshine yellow only as a micro accent

Avoid:
- visible brands and logos
- readable baked-in text
- generic tropical clip-art look
- excessive neon saturation
- impossible architecture or water
- identical cinematic orange/teal grading on every image
- generated people presented as the actual couple

- required text-safe zone exists
- lighting and saturation fit the set
- image adds a distinct editorial role rather than duplicating another image
- identifiable dummy person cannot be mistaken for the real couple

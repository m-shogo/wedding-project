## Visible problem tested

After FU `1209:2` became Best Outer, the remaining cream masthead/photo boundary was tested as a possible false-header defect. The hypothesis was that moving the dominant travel-object image upward could create a more continuous magazine field.

## Result

The photo intrusion compressed the strongest Japanese masthead hierarchy instead of improving continuity. `ふたり旅。` / subhead / `01` became crowded around the photo boundary and the cover lost the clearer `横浜 → issue line → 01` reading order that FU had established.

This is useful negative evidence against applying RSL-009 mechanically. A false-header repair is only beneficial when the image extension does not consume necessary headline breathing room or force editorial anchors into collision/noise.

## Learning

Do not classify every cream/photo boundary as a defect. Before extending a dominant image behind/into a masthead, test whether the boundary is actually breaking editorial continuity or instead providing required Japanese headline breathing room and hierarchy. A successful subtraction/field-extension principle still needs a binding/hierarchy-function check in the receiving composition.

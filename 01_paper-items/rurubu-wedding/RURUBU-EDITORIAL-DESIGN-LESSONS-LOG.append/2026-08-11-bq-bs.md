# 2026-08-11 — BQ/BS lessons companion

This append-only companion avoids replacing the large authority log with an incomplete readback. It records lessons from the verified BQ/BR/BS comparison for later consolidation into `RURUBU-EDITORIAL-DESIGN-LESSONS-LOG.md`.

## LESSON-CANDIDATE — Unequal Q&A importance can turn a form into an editorial profile

**Context:** V5 BQ inside clean-room `783:282`.

**Observed failure or opportunity:** Even after cards were removed, equal visual weight across Q1/Q2/Q3 made the lower profile page read like a questionnaire interface.

**General editorial principle:** When the factual content allows it, one answer can become a pull quote while the remaining Q&A becomes secondary reading. Editorial hierarchy is not the same as equal data presentation.

**Design change:** Existing Q1 answer was enlarged into a pull quote with one narrow magenta rule; Q2/Q3 stayed compact; common-point content became a smaller angled tape.

**Verification evidence:** whole-spread + actual-size left-page review; `53` native text, `6` IMAGE fills, `0` same-parent text intersections; accepted image hashes and fold preserved.

**Status:** TESTED.

## LESSON-CANDIDATE — Photo-role correctness outranks sharpness

**Context:** rejected BR outer `785:2`.

**Observed failure or opportunity:** A sharper verified coast photograph immediately improved surface quality compared with the soft Yokohama hero, but changed the semantic destination signal.

**General editorial principle:** A technically sharper or prettier photograph must be rejected if it fails the editorial role. Asset quality includes semantic fit, not only resolution and aesthetics.

**Verification evidence:** whole-spread screenshot comparison; BR rejected despite improved sharpness.

**Status:** TESTED.

## LESSON-CANDIDATE — Bounding a weak raster is mitigation, not completion

**Context:** BS outer `787:2`.

**Observed failure or opportunity:** Full-page stretching amplified the soft hero raster. Bounding it to `793.7 × 820` reduced visible interpolation damage, but actual-size softness remained.

**General editorial principle:** Layout can reduce the damage caused by a weak source, but cannot convert it into a passed dominant image. Raster gate and layout gate remain independent.

**Design change:** Correct Yokohama hero bounded to 820px height, article matter overlaps its lower edge, feature-01 paper compacted, print footer restored.

**Verification evidence:** whole-spread and actual-size front review; `39` native text, `7` IMAGE fills, `0` text intersections; fold preserved.

**Status:** TESTED.

## LESSON-CANDIDATE — Clean-room mastheads should carry genre energy without reproducing protected marks

**Context:** BS cover.

**Observed failure or opportunity:** A clean-room candidate does not need to reproduce the legacy publication-style image masthead to feel like a Japanese travel magazine.

**General editorial principle:** Use original native typography, destination hierarchy, photo dominance, issue/date devices, and editorial rhythm to evoke genre. Do not depend on copied publication logos.

**Design change:** clean-room masthead changed to original native text `旅するWEDDING` while the legacy image masthead stayed hidden in the duplicate.

**Verification evidence:** actual-size front screenshot retained magazine recognition and editability.

**Status:** TESTED.

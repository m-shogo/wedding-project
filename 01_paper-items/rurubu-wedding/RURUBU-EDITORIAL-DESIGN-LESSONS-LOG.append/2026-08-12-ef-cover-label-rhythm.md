# EF — cover label rhythm / photo-first hierarchy — 2026-08-12

## Lesson

A travel-magazine cover can be visually dense without becoming card-dense. The strongest hierarchy came from one dominant destination photo, one intentionally tilted support photo, one full-bleed lower photo, oversized native Japanese display type, and only two flat editorial accents.

## What improved

- Magenta top rule + native masthead created a stronger issue identity without imitating a protected logo.
- A compact yellow `保存版` marker gave the cover a real magazine issue cue without becoming sticker clutter.
- Feature 01 was allowed to sit directly on the hero; Feature 02 used a single yellow caption band; Feature 03 used only a narrow yellow rule over the lower photo.
- Actual-size QA exposed that dark navy Feature 01 type was insufficient over the darker pier. Switching the existing native title to white solved the defect without adding a UI-like panel.

## Reusable rule

When the cover still feels like a poster or landing page, do not solve it with more modules. First increase scale contrast, crop/overlap, and native headline rhythm. Introduce color fields only when they have a specific editorial job: issue marker, caption band, or reading rule.

`collision = 0` is necessary but not sufficient. Rendered contrast on the real image crop must pass at actual size.

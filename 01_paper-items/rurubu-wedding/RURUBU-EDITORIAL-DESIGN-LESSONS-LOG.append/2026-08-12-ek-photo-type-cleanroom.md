# EK — photo/type clean-room lesson

## Reusable lesson

For a Rurubu-like wedding travel cover, a small number of strong editorial anchors outperform decorative UI labels. If the composition already has a dominant destination image, large Japanese headline, and overlapping support photography, an extra badge or broad colored caption card can make the page feel like an app or brochure rather than print editorial.

Use subtraction first: remove the badge/card, keep native text, then express hierarchy through photo scale, crop, overlap, direct-on-photo copy, and very small issue metadata.

## What EK proved

- Removing the vertical 保存版 badge improved thumbnail readability without losing issue identity.
- Enlarging the 02 photograph and tightening its caption increased asymmetric travel-magazine energy.
- A large 01 number plus direct white headline can carry feature hierarchy without a card container.
- Lower full-bleed photography can carry 03 directly with only a thin editorial rule.
- Collision checks are necessary but insufficient unless paired with actual-size render review; EK caught and repaired a 01 number/title collision before promotion.
- Bounded safe-area checks should include inherited footer microtype, even when that microtype looks harmless in a whole-spread screenshot.

# EH — vertical issue strap / solid-ink back-cover type — 2026-08-12

## Lesson

When a photo-led cover still feels digitally assembled, the remaining problem may be editorial furniture rather than photo count. Replacing a square issue badge with a narrow page-edge strap and increasing one support photo's boundary overlap produced a stronger travel-magazine rhythm without adding modules.

## What improved

- `保存版` became a slim vertical yellow edge strap instead of a square badge.
- The tilted Feature 02 image was enlarged and pushed across the hero/lower-street boundary, increasing asymmetric energy.
- Feature 01 and Feature 03 remained direct-on-photo native typography rather than acquiring background cards.
- Back-cover display shadows were removed. A first solid-navy headline was too weak over the floral zone and was rejected; solid magenta passed actual-size review without restoring a shadow.
- Structure QA found one Feature 03 text-box collision that was not visually obvious at thumbnail scale; it was repaired before promotion.

## Reusable rule

Use print furniture before UI furniture: edge straps, rules, caption bands and direct-on-photo type should do a specific editorial job. Do not add a box simply to make hierarchy easier. For text on photography, local tonal-zone contrast at actual size decides the ink color.

Visual QA and structural QA are complementary. `collision = 0` does not prove good contrast, and a good screenshot does not prove the text boxes are non-overlapping.

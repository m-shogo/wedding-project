# Rurubu editorial lesson — EX ratio-correct collage

A photo-led travel-magazine cover does not improve merely because the dominant image gets larger. If the source was prepared for a wide editorial role, stretching it into a tall full-page field can make the page look more synthetic at actual print size even when the thumbnail looks energetic.

EW proved the failure mode: a full-page proxy strengthened composition at 500px but exposed blockiness at 794×1123, so it was not promoted. EX instead returned that image to a wide, ratio-plausible field and made the exact-Q60 Yokohama derivative the destination-specific postcard. The lower street image supplies full-bleed depth; a smaller support photo overlaps the field rather than becoming a uniform card.

The actual-size pass also proved that collision=0 is not sufficient visual QA. White `ふたり旅。` on cream was technically valid but visually weak, so an existing thin rule was repurposed as one sharp magenta print strap. A support-photo z-order defect was repaired without adding a container.

Reusable rule: **source fidelity before scale**. When an accepted photo begins to look artificial after enlargement, restore a plausible crop/aspect ratio and rebuild hierarchy with native typography, overlap, and varied photo scale before requesting another generated asset.

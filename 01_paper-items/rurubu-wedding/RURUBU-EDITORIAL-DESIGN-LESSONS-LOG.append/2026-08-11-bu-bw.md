# 2026-08-11 — Rurubu BU/BW reusable editorial lessons

Scope: Rurubu WEDDING only.

## Lesson 1 — Q&A syntax should not scale with a pull quote
When an existing factual answer becomes display typography, enlarging the literal `A` / `B` prefix with the quote preserves form grammar and weakens editorial voice. Keep the semantic answer native/editable, but separate the answer marker into microtype. BU `790:2` proved this with Q1-B: the 25 px sentence now stands alone while a 12 px `B` remains as provenance-oriented microtype. Whole-spread and actual-size review showed a more natural magazine quotation without changing the factual answer.

Next application: promote interview answers selectively, and scale the story—not the questionnaire syntax.

## Lesson 2 — A photographic pause can be stronger than another cover line
BS had a second mid-photo message between the dominant destination headline and the feature cluster. BV `790:284` removed `寄り道メモ / 寄り道したくなる、横浜へ。` only in a safe duplicate. The resulting path became more publication-like because the photograph gets visual breathing room before the 01/02/03 article cluster.

Next application: before adding cover copy, test whether subtraction improves the destination-photo hierarchy. Do not fill every quiet photographic area with text merely because space exists.

## Lesson 3 — Pasted-paper fields should hug editorial content, not behave like cards
Feature 01 still carried a large rectangular cream field after other UI-like elements were removed. BW `790:467` compressed that paper while preserving a dominant `01`, a large Japanese title, and unequal overlapping photo stories 02/03. Structure QA remained at zero same-parent text intersections.

Next application: a print-paper motif is useful when it behaves like a clipped editorial fragment. If it creates broad unused internal space, it starts reading like a web card and should be compressed or removed.

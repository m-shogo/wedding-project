# 2026-08-11 — CY photo-led profile lesson

## Reusable lesson
When a profile spread has a credible dominant photograph and a genuine text-safe zone, treat the photograph as the page field instead of stacking a separate heading band above a photo block. A page-edge crop plus a materially smaller overlapping secondary portrait creates a stronger Japanese travel-magazine silhouette than two similarly weighted portrait modules.

Keep final typography native and editable. Put the Japanese feature headline directly into the verified text-safe area, then use unequal scale for names, metadata, one dominant pull quote, and compact secondary questions. Subtract redundant rules/cards before adding any new decoration.

## Failure / repair lesson
Always screenshot after changing photo scale or layer order. In CY, the first photo-led experiment put the headline behind the enlarged image even though the node geometry was correct. Bring overlay text above the photo, then rerun actual-size and structure QA. Also remove legacy rules that become redundant after hierarchy changes rather than letting old decoration survive invisibly into the new grammar.

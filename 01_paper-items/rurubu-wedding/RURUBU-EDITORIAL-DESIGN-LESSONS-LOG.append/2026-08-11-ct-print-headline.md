# Rurubu editorial lessons — CT print headline

Date: 2026-08-11

- **Outline is not the default answer for a busy travel photograph.** In this cover, removing the white+navy outline and splitting the native Japanese display into navy `横浜` plus white `ふたり旅。` on one flat magenta field improved the whole-cover and thumbnail silhouette.
- **A color bar should carry hierarchy, not decorate empty space.** The magenta field survives because it is attached directly to the primary cover line; adding additional bars around unrelated text would weaken the result.
- **Repair local contrast locally.** Feature 02 needed a narrow dark ink strip behind its caption; it did not need a new card, global dark overlay, shadow system, or replacement of the photo module.
- **Whole-spread screenshots do not prove production geometry.** CT looked visually sound while its provisional fold guide had shifted vertically. The structural readback caught the regression and the guide was restored before acceptance.
- **A better cover composition does not cure a bad hero raster.** CT is the strongest outer comparator only for composition/typography. Until the verified Q60 derivative is actually placed and visually checked, V5-01 remains open and dominant-photo pass remains `2/3`.

Next use: exact Q60 placement should be tested on a rollback-safe CT-derived target, then the headline/photo relationship must be re-judged at thumbnail, reading, and actual-size scales rather than assuming the stronger raster automatically preserves the winning crop.

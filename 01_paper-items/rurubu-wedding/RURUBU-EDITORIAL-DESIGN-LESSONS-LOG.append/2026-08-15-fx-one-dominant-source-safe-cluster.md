# 2026-08-15 — FX: one dominant can carry the page

## Lesson

A magazine page does not need every subsection to own a second hero photograph. If an upper section already supplies enough dominant photographic mass, a lower cluster can become more credible by respecting each source's intrinsic scale and using typography, asymmetry and varied image roles for energy.

FO's lower Memory 01 looked strong at a glance but used a `352×368` street source at `680×410`. FX keeps the upper `1356×560 → 793.7×492` History photo as the page dominant and reduces the lower street role to `350×365`, while preserving two support images at source-safe sizes.

- Memory 02 skyline `1214:268`: `240×220 → 200×220`;

## Negative evidence

## Transfer boundary

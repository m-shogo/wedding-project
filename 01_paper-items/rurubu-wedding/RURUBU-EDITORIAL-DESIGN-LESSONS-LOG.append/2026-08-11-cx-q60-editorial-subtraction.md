# 2026-08-11 — CX editorial subtraction / Q60 transport lesson

## Reusable lesson
When a profile spread has one strong pull quote and several smaller Q&A items, do not give every question a full colored rule or equal module geometry. That recreates a form/dashboard silhouette. Keep one dominant article moment, then treat secondary questions as compact marginal notes with unequal number scale and tighter text grouping.

## Evidence
CX `854:187` removed the Q2/Q3 full-width rules and enlarged only their native issue numbers. Whole-spread and actual-size QA improved the editorial hierarchy while preserving all factual text, fold geometry, and production image hashes. Structure QA ended at zero same-parent text intersections after a 2 px common-point repair.

## Asset lesson
An official Figma upload target is not a placed asset. If the byte POST fails before commit, status remains `NOT PLACED / NOT VISUALLY VERIFIED`. Do not compensate for a weak dominant raster with more decoration; switch to another safe visual target and keep the asset gate open.

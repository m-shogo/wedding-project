## CANDIDATE LESSON — Break hero-plus-index semantics with photo ownership and one asymmetric paper field

**Context:** V5 outer BD `756:2` → BF `761:2`.

**Observed failure or opportunity:** BD had removed much legacy card geometry, but its lower three-feature treatment still read as a tidy index attached to a hero. The page was cleaner without yet becoming unmistakably photo-driven travel editorial.

**General editorial principle:** Removing rounded cards does not by itself remove interface semantics. When the cover still reads `hero + navigation`, first test more photo ownership, unequal story scale, and one deliberately shaped paper field that overlaps the photo/copy system.

**Process change:** Work on a rollback-safe duplicate; inspect z-order as part of visual QA; perform actual-size Japanese type review; run text intersection QA after visual repairs.

**Verified design change:** BF expands the temporary hero to `774 × 1102`, uses one warm asymmetric paper field, keeps 01 dominant, places 02 on a cyan-rule lower-left article line, places 03 in a compact yellow print callout, and overlaps unequal coast/old-town teaser photos across the seam. Initial z-order and cramped-story regressions were detected and repaired before adoption.

**Evidence:** BF `761:2`, front `761:131`; native text `39`; IMAGE fills `8`; same-parent text intersections `0`; fold `761:176 = 2 × 1122.5`; all eight known image hashes preserved. Detailed run: `learning-runs/2026-08-11-v5-bf-bg-cleanroom-editorial-qa.md`.

## CANDIDATE LESSON — Let portrait collage establish profile hierarchy before Q&A structure

**Context:** V5 inside BE `758:2` → BG `763:2`.

**Observed failure or opportunity:** BE's right page was strongly editorial, but the left still resembled a profile form because the portraits and three questions carried relatively even structural weight.

**General editorial principle:** For magazine-profile pages, let the portrait relationship establish the page first. Questions can then use unequal columns/stacking and short editorial rules instead of equal containers.

**Process change:** Preserve identity-safe verified photos and native facts; promote existing semantic text rather than duplicating it; use actual-size screenshots plus programmatic same-parent intersection QA.

**Verified design change:** BG expands and overlaps the two identity-safe portraits, makes Q1 the larger left story and stacks Q2/Q3 at right with magenta/cyan/yellow rules. A duplicate shared-interest pull quote was caught visually and deleted, and a SHOGO name/metadata intersection was caught structurally and repaired.

**Evidence:** BG `763:2`, left `763:3`, inherited right `763:126`; native text `53`; IMAGE fills `6`; same-parent text intersections `0`; fold `763:276 = 2 × 1122.5`; all six known image hashes preserved. Detailed run: `learning-runs/2026-08-11-v5-bf-bg-cleanroom-editorial-qa.md`.

**Status:** TESTED AS V5 COMPARATOR; not promoted to Current or global project rule.

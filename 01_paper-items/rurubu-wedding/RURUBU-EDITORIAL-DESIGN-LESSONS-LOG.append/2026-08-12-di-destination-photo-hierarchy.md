# 2026-08-12 — DI: destination photography must own the travel-mag silhouette

## Lesson

A wedding-travel cover can be technically cleaner yet still fail the genre if its largest image reads as props styling. At thumbnail scale, a Japanese travel-information cover needs an unmistakable photographic destination/travel-memory mass before secondary wedding cues.

When the exact destination asset is temporarily blocked, the safe fallback is **not** to enlarge a low-quality proxy. Use a verified high-quality travel-memory image as the dominant editorial atmosphere, label it so it cannot be mistaken for the exact destination, and retain the exact-destination proxy only as a small factual inset until its lifecycle is complete.

Subtraction still comes first: remove UI-like masthead fields; let native Japanese type sit directly on photography; use one asymmetric paper field and varied type scale rather than modular cards. If the lower page becomes lifestyle-empty after subtraction, restore a compact native-text feature before adding a new container.

DI `925:2` demonstrated this: coast travel-memory dominant, native `横浜 / ふたり旅。`, direct-on-photo 02, small Yokohama inset, giant 01, compact 03. Thumbnail/whole/actual-size QA passed after fixing one text-box collision; structure ended at 39 native texts, 7 visible images, 0 same-parent text intersections.

## Reuse rule

For future Rurubu/V6 concepts: judge the cover in grayscale-like silhouette terms first — what is the biggest visual mass, and does it say “travel information magazine” before reading copy? If not, changing badges or spacing is insufficient. Change the dominant-photo role and scale relationship first.

# DM photo-cluster lesson

Date: 2026-08-12
Scope: RURUBU WEDDING only

## Problem

Even a full-bleed travel photograph can still feel like a poster when the lower hierarchy is built from one large information field and too few photographic interruptions.

## Tested rule

Before adding decoration, reduce panel mass and let unequal photography create the page rhythm. Use one dominant photograph, then a smaller factual destination anchor and at least one differently scaled/rotated support photograph. Keep Japanese headline typography native and dominant.

## Result

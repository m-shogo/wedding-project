# 2026-08-15 — FU role-sized source fidelity

## Lesson

**Source fidelity is a macro-composition constraint, not a final asset check.**

FL proved that a composition can pass thumbnail, hierarchy and structure QA while depending on an intrinsically small raster for too much printed area. The `352×368` street source had been expanded to `515×822.5`, turning an otherwise useful support photograph into the dominant print surface.

When this happens, do not preserve the macro-layout simply because it has accumulated polish. Reassign the weak source to a bounded role and rebuild the hierarchy around a source whose intrinsic dimensions and crop plausibly support the intended printed area.

## Evidence

- rejected first repair FT `1208:2`: higher-resolution Yokohama waterfront as dominant improved source fidelity but repeated the same city image across front/back and weakened lower-page closure;
- adopted FU `1209:2`: high-resolution `944×608` travel-object source became `793.7×512` dominant, low-resolution street became `226×304` support, dining remained `444×286`, exact Q60 skyline remained `230×211` from intrinsic `240×220`;
- 500 px whole-item PASS;
- 1000 px reading spread PASS;
- actual-size front 794×1123 PASS;
- actual-size back ≈798×1123 PASS;
- final absolute text intersections `0` and 18 px safe-area risks `0`.

## Important nuance

This is not a rule that flat-lay photography is better than destination photography. FT demonstrated that a higher-resolution photograph can still be editorially wrong when repetition and page closure regress. The transferable decision order is:

1. inspect intrinsic source fidelity against intended printed role;
2. if the source cannot sustain the role, change the role or composition;
3. then evaluate semantic coherence, repetition, binding and page closure;
4. only then polish crop and typography.

Exact imagery, Japanese type, palette, overlap geometry and Rurubu-like editorial grammar remain Rurubu-specific.

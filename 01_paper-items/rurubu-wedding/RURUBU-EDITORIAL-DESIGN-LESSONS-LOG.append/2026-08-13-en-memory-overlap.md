# Rurubu editorial lesson — EN dominant memory field

A travel-magazine page can still feel grid-like even when card chrome has been removed. If a dominant photograph and two support photographs align as a clean side column, the geometry itself can recreate UI rhythm.

EN `1098:2` proved a stronger pattern: expand the lead photo until it becomes the page field, then treat the support photographs as unequal editorial insets. Preserve accepted photo hashes and factual copy; change hierarchy before generating more assets.

One flat square-corner cream caption paper was justified only after direct-on-photo contrast failed. It has no shadow, rounding or decorative effect.

The run also exposed a QA lesson: same-parent text intersection returned zero while destination copy still overlapped a folio belonging to another parent. Actual-size screenshot review caught it. Final QA therefore uses absolute text geometry across nested parents near page edges.

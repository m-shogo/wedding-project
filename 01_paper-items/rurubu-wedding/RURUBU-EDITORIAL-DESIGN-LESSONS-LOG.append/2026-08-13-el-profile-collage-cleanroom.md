# EL — profile collage clean-room lesson

## Reusable lesson

On a Japanese travel-magazine-style profile spread, two equal portrait rectangles plus evenly separated metadata can still feel like brochure/UI composition even when the photography is good. A stronger pattern is `dominant portrait + smaller overlapping portrait + direct-on-photo display type`, with metadata returning to the paper field below.

Use subtraction rather than adding decoration. Preserve native text and verified image fills, create hierarchy through unequal scale and overlap, then validate the rendered title at actual size; geometry checks alone cannot catch a muddy photo/text boundary.

## What EL proved

- Increasing one profile photograph to dominant scale improved thumbnail recognition and reduced equal-grid rhythm.
- A smaller bride portrait with a restrained white print edge and slight rotation produced collage energy without sticker/card overload.
- The first title placement crossing image and cream was visually weak even though the structure was valid; it was rejected.
- Moving the native Japanese title and subline directly onto the dominant image in white gave a clearer editorial anchor without adding a panel, gradient, or shadow.
- Existing identity-safe hashes for groom and bride remained unchanged.

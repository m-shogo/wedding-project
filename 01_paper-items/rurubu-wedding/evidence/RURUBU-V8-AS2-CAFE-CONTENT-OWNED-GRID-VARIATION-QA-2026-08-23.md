## Fresh professional research

This run deliberately moved away from recent photo-selection references and tested a book/grid question.

### Walker Art Center — *The Making of the Lifelike Catalog*

The catalogue designer describes a **master grid with large content-specific variations**: essay, plates and object lessons use substantially different grids because the material has different editorial jobs. The useful principle is not to imitate those layouts, but to distinguish publication coherence from mechanical repetition.

### MoMA — Irma Boom, *SHV Think Book 1996–1896*

MoMA documents Boom's treatment of typography, layout, materials, printing, page edges and nonlinear navigation as one book architecture. The useful Rurubu implication is that a book's identity can live in a system of relationships and physical/reading behavior rather than in one repeated positional template.

## Observed live defect in AS `2261:2`

AS was structurally sound and already had a legitimate quiet text-led role. However, the daytime Cafe sequence placed three semantically different sensory fragments on essentially one repeated left axis:

- `2261:8 / カップの音。` — x54 y410
- `2261:24 / 窓の光。` — x54 y510
- `2261:25 / 次の店を決める会話。` — x54 y585

At thumbnail scale this made the left page read as a stack of equal-position text modules. The quiet space therefore risked becoming a stylistic signal — `book design = big empty field` — instead of pacing generated by the content.

## Root-cause hypothesis

`ROOT_CAUSE_HYPOTHESIS`:

> The three fragments have different semantic scale: two are quick object/light observations, while the third is a broader social memory. Keeping all three on the same positional axis over-equalizes their editorial roles and turns restraint into a template.

The correction must remain bounded by two safeguards:

1. do not invent asymmetry merely to make the page look designed;
2. do not add a fake/schematic image merely to fill quiet space.

## Anti-AI / professional-authenticity gate

AS2 specifically reduces one AI/template signal: **uniform spacing/positioning applied to semantically different beats**.

It does not introduce:

- repeated equal cards/modules;
- rounded pills/badges;
- generic gradients/shadows;
- decorative English;
- fake tropical or luxury motifs;
- arbitrary tape/sticker/rotation randomness;
- a fake image slot;
- schema/internal-process microcopy.

The main remaining risk is that restraint can still be over-read as luxury-by-whitespace. Future comparison must judge the full V8 sequence, not AS2 in isolation.

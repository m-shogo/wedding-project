## Professional research input

New/deeper research in this pass focused on interview/profile editorial structure rather than another photo/crop or numbering pass.

- Society of Publication Designers / Anna Alexander on *Interview*: the magazine's editorial art was a portrait, and the ambition was a portrait meaningful on its own rather than generic supporting imagery. https://www.spd.org/first-love/2018/2/anna-alexander-director-of-photography-at-wired
- magCulture / *The Gentlewoman* #27: the team describes a flatplan with an intentional arc and repeatedly re-evaluates the balance of people/features; portrait/text balance changes according to the feature rather than a fixed module. https://magculture.com/blogs/journal/the-gentlewoman-27
- magCulture / Penny Martin: editorial decisions begin from the interviewee/real life rather than repeating magazine forms by habit. https://magculture.com/blogs/journal/at-work-with-penny-martin-the-gentlewoman

Rurubu-specific hypothesis: in an unanswered real-couple Q&A, generic food/dinner structural dummies should not act as the dominant editorial art merely because they make the page look complete. If verified shared-couple imagery exists, use only role-correct human evidence and let the questions themselves carry the page structure.

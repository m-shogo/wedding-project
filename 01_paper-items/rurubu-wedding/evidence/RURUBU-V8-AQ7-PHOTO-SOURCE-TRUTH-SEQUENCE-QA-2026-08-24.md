## New professional research used

This run rotated to whole-book editing/sequencing rather than repeating pagination, number, or local-grid research. Aperture material on Stuart Smith and photobook production treats editing and sequencing as core design work: images are selected for the sequence, weak images are removed, and pacing/physical page-turn affect meaning. Aperture educational guidance also recommends variation in distance/composition and calm versus energetic sections. SPD's Rev Journal case study establishes rhythm and pace across the full publication before refining individual treatments.

Rurubu hypothesis: when photography is not source-verified, do not keep it merely to avoid whitespace. First protect documentary truth; then use existing grounded content and page rhythm to create a defensible closing beat until legitimate photography exists.

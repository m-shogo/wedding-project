### K8 / Profile+Q&A — `PERSONALITY DRIFT`

K8 is source-truth-safe and editorially coherent, but at thumbnail scale it reads closer to restrained editorial/book design than to a high-energy Japanese travel-information magazine. The correct response is not to restore false photos; typography, information architecture and eventually role-correct place imagery must carry the energy.

### H11 / Cafe+Table — `PERSONALITY DRIFT / PHOTO BLOCKED`

The spread has a stronger photo-led opening than K8/G10, but the current Cafe/Table sources remain structural dummies. The complete Hawaii wedding pool has already been audited and provides zero role-correct food/table/interior images, so further styling of those dummies cannot solve the core issue.

### C6E / 1DAY — `UTILITY STRONG / ENERGY MODERATE`

Grounded planning data gives this spread real travel-guide value, but the current typographic treatment remains much quieter than the declared V7 direction. Unlike K8, C6E already has useful concrete planning information, so a future re-arm should amplify scanning and journey momentum rather than add decorative modules.

## Root-cause hypothesis

A sequence of individually successful local corrections — removing unjustified numbers, generic microcopy, unverified photos, redundant dividers and template furniture — improved truth and reduced AI/template signals. However, these correct local subtractions were not always followed by a **publication-level personality check**.

The cumulative effect is that V7 has partially converged toward the restrained V8 school it was meant to oppose. This is not evidence that prior truth gates were wrong. It indicates a missing second step:

> after subtraction removes false structure, the page still needs a positive editorial idea strong enough for its intended publication personality.

Do **not** undo source-truth, event-truth, pagination or anti-AI gates just to restore density.

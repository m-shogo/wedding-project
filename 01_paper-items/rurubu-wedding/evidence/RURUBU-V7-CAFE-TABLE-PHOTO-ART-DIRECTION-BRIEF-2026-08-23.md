## Why this brief exists

V7 Cafe / Table has structurally valid page hierarchy, but its current images are explicitly structural photo dummies. The visual bottleneck is no longer primitive layout polish; it is whether final role-specific imagery creates appetite and Hawaii destination truth without AI-stock cliches.

## New research used

- dancyu editor Hiroo Ueno describes asking photographers to prioritize making food look genuinely desirable rather than merely stylish; he specifically favors the eater's viewpoint and natural/back lighting for appetite.
- dancyu art-direction interviews emphasize that the editorial policy comes first and that page construction should serve how the editor wants the material communicated.
- TRANSIT editor Naonori Kato and art director Fumikazu Ohara describe travel publishing as seeing a place without preset assumptions and transmitting it through photographs and words.
- There There's TRANSIT redesign describes several standard grids coordinating margins/text while allowing different editorial situations rather than one repeated template.

These are observations, not project rules.

## Rurubu-specific hypothesis

For V7 food/travel photography, `stylish` is not the primary photo job. The image should first create `食べたい` and `そこに行きたい`, while showing believable table/shop/street evidence of Hawaii. A generic resort sunset or luxury flat-lay fails even if visually polished.

## Current slot roles

### Cafe dominant

- current structural slot: approximately `465×565`, portrait-oriented
- editorial role: communicate one cafe break as an experience, not a product cutout
- preferred visual character: 35–50 mm equivalent; seated/eater eye level; natural side/back light; visible food texture and local environmental trace
- no final readable generated signage required

### Table dominant

- current structural slot: approximately `705×460`, landscape
- editorial role: shared meal first, then hands/plates/room/local atmosphere
- faces are not required; generated recognizable people must not represent the actual couple/family/friends/dog
- avoid wide-angle room distortion

## Comparison plan

Generate at least three materially different candidates per main role:

A. appetite / food-first
B. partial human presence / sharing-first
C. place / shop-environment-first

Compare in the exact slot, not as isolated images. Critique each through art direction, editorial usefulness, page-sequence role, typography/crop relationship, photo authenticity and print survival.

## Explicit negatives

- sterile top-down flat-lay as the only visual idea
- luxury-resort stock mood
- excessive hibiscus/palm/tiki shorthand
- fantasy sunset as proof of Hawaii
- plastic food / diffusion gloss
- malformed hands/faces
- generated readable signage as focal point
- generic tropical props with no editorial owner
- oversaturation / crushed shadows / over-HDR

## Current slot roles

### Cafe dominant

- current structural slot: approximately `465×565`, portrait-oriented
- editorial role: communicate one cafe break as an experience, not a product cutout
- preferred visual character: 35–50 mm equivalent; seated/eater eye level; natural side/back light; visible food texture and local environmental trace
- local evidence may include table wear, service objects, window/sidewalk/shop context, ordinary use traces, or other believable place cues; do not force stereotyped tropical props
- no final readable generated signage required

### Table dominant

- current structural slot: approximately `705×460`, landscape
- editorial role: shared meal first, then hands/plates/room/local atmosphere
- faces are not required; generated recognizable people must not represent the actual couple/family/friends/dog
- avoid wide-angle room distortion

Additional local-truth questions for each candidate:

- Does this image imply a believable Hawai‘i place without relying on a postcard cliché?
- Is the local evidence actually visible at reading and actual-size scale, or only described in the prompt?
- Would the image still feel truthful if hibiscus/palm/sunset shorthand were removed?
- Does the image feel observed/used rather than staged as generic luxury travel stock?

- fantasy sunset as proof of Hawaii
- plastic food / diffusion gloss
- malformed hands/faces
- generated readable signage as focal point
- generic tropical props with no editorial owner
- oversaturation / crushed shadows / over-HDR

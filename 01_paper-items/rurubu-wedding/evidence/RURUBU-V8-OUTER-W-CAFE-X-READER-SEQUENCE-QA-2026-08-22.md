## Fresh professional research that changed the decision

### D&DEPARTMENT / d design travel

Fresh review of official `d design travel` editorial policy reinforced two useful hypotheses without copying its visual language:

1. place-character should be discovered from real regional meaning rather than added as trend/decorative skin;
2. editorial writing should be in the editor's own truthful words, and photography should avoid exaggerating the subject merely for spectacle.

Applied to V8 Outer: functional navigation should sound like this publication's reader-facing voice rather than internal English section taxonomy.

### IDEA / book design as editorial structure

Fresh IDEA material on Takeo Nakano frames book design as not only graphic treatment but editorial design from the book's fundamental structure, including how the book reaches its reader.

Applied to Cafe/Table: related semantic nouns should not be spatially scattered merely to simulate art direction. They need a deliberate reading relationship.

These are project hypotheses tested locally, not copied layouts or permanent rules.

## New professional research used

This run deliberately used new references rather than reusing the recent JLREQ / book-design sources.

1. Masahiro Shintani interview (Ama Town): editorial expression should serve the person/content rather than the designer's self-expression; he describes choosing typographic character from the person being represented.
2. POPEYE 50th interview archive: the publication treats individual answers/voices as editorial material, not merely form fields.
3. BRUTUS editorial interview: the entry should remain readable while deeper material becomes richer; design is responsible for making dense specialist content approachable.

Rurubu hypothesis: when a Q&A page contains real answer text plus unanswered/short prompt-only items, the real human voice should carry more visual authority than a repeated numbered-question module system.

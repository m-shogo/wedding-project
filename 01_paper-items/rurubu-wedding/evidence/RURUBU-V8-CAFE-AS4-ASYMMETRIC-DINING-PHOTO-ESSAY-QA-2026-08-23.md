## New professional research used

This run deliberately rotated away from the recent divider/grid/time-map references and studied photo editing and publication pacing.

- Eye Magazine's profile of Gail Bichler describes an editorial process where the photo edit is reviewed early and design responds to selected imagery rather than treating photography as late decoration.
- Eye Magazine's 2025 review `Change the pace` emphasizes that repeated visual presentation creates monotony and that editorial rhythm benefits from meaningful variation.
- Eye Magazine's writing on photographic sequencing describes sequence as a narrative decision: location, atmosphere and time are changed by how images are selected and ordered.

Rurubu-specific hypothesis: **a restrained Cafe/Table spread can remain book-like while admitting one role-owned food/place photograph, but the photograph must behave as an editorial beat rather than a centered banner module.** The purpose is sensory/place evidence and spread pacing, not generic visual filling.

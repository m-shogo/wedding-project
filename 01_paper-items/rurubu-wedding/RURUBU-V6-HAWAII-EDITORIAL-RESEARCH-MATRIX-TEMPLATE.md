## Extraction rules

For every adopted principle, record:
1. the observed visual mechanism
2. why it supports travel-magazine authenticity
3. why it supports the wedding content
4. where it will appear in V6
5. how it remains original

Forbidden research outputs:
- “looks stylish” without a mechanism
- collecting many images without comparison
- copying the exact cover-line positions of one issue
- using reference photography as final material without rights confirmation
- adopting a trend because it is popular

## Required synthesis

After the matrix is populated, produce these decisions before Figma production:

### Cover system
- dominant-photo model
- logo/date relationship
- cover-line count and hierarchy
- small-photo treatment
- Hawaii/resort signal without cliché overload

### Inside system
- profile asymmetry
- Q&A/article treatment
- history/timeline structure
- memory-spot hierarchy
- map/route integration

### Back-cover system
- emotional closing image
- Friends & Family rhythm
- route/closing-copy treatment
- quieter density than the front cover

### Typography system
- display
- section
- body
- caption
- micro/folio
- Japanese line-break and punctuation review method

### Image-generation system
For each semantic role define before generation:
- target aspect ratio
- target composition
- text-safe zone
- focal subject
- prohibited artifacts
- whether people are absent, distant, back-view, or real-photo-only

## V6 candidate comparison

- `V6-A`

Do not make B merely a color variation.

- Hawaii/resort authenticity

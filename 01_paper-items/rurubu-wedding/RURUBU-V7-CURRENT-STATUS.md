New professional print-production research was deliberately rotated away from recent content-truth/photo-sequence topics. Adobe InDesign's current trapping/overprint guidance explains that press/paper tolerances can create misregistration between process colors, that trapping/overprinting behavior is press-dependent, and that small black text is commonly overprinted to reduce registration problems. Reverse/knockout text does not receive the same default black-text protection, so screen legibility alone is not sufficient press evidence.

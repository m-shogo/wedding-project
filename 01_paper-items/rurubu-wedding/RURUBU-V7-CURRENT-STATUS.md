## Latest verified improvement — grounded specific copy no longer acts as a caption for an unverified photo

### New/deeper professional research

This run rotated to visual-journalism caption/context integrity rather than repeating grid, pagination, section-number, or Japanese-wrap research.

Associated Press visual standards explicitly avoid generic/file/library imagery being presented so it could be mistaken for imagery photographed for the specific story, and require accurate visual context. NPPA caption guidance likewise requires captions to accurately describe what is actually visible and provide the necessary context.

Rurubu-specific hypothesis: even when native copy is factually grounded, placing it directly as a caption under an unverified structural photo dummy can transfer factual authority from the words to the image. Source truth therefore includes the semantic relationship between copy and photography, not only whether each element is individually labeled correctly in Figma.

This pass deliberately sought a contradictory viewpoint rather than reusing the recent subtraction rules.

- The Guardian redesign treats readability and navigability as core publication-design requirements and organizes navigation around reader use rather than internal structure.
- Neville Brody's magazine-typography discussion argues that a system should remain when its practical reason remains valid; numbers are not sacred as a form, but a publication still needs genuine finding/navigation devices where the reader job exists.

# Heading and Explanatory Line Can Replace a Q&A Tape

Date: 2026-08-06
Status: `VERIFIED FOR V5 / NOT YET PROJECT_RULE`

## Source

Live Rurubu V5 inside-left profile page, nodes `77:339`, `77:340`, and the native `3 QUESTIONS` heading `77:308` with its explanatory line.

## Hypothesis

When a section heading and adjacent explanatory copy already establish a Q&A module, an additional color-bar tape that restates the same function may add sticker/UI density without improving navigation or comprehension.

## Result

Hiding the yellow tape and its repeated label preserved the `3 QUESTIONS` heading, explanatory line, all three question modules, shared-interests section, Travel Note, native text, semantic structure, image fills, fold guide, and rollback frames. Whole-item, reading-scale, and actual-size/structure review found no empty gap, collision, clipping, text reflow, crop regression, or hierarchy loss.

## Failure / limit

This is not a blanket rejection of section tapes. A tape can remain when it supplies unique navigation, necessary contrast, or semantic information not already carried by the heading and explanatory text.

## Adopted use

For Rurubu V5, test subtraction when a tape directly restates the adjacent heading or explanatory line. Preserve the stronger native editorial device and verify the result at whole-item, page, and actual-size scales.

## Next application

Do not let decorative subtraction outrank unresolved image provenance and quality gates. Audit further labels only for demonstrable semantic duplication.

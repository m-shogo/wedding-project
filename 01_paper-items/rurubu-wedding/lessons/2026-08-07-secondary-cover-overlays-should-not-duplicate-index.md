# Secondary cover overlays should not duplicate a complete contents index

Date: 2026-08-07
Stage: `VERIFIED_FOR_V5 / PROJECT_RULE_NOT_YET_PROMOTED`

## Source

Live Rurubu WEDDING V5 cover candidate `77:18`, especially the hero headline clusters `77:196–77:201` and the complete feature index `77:209–77:232`.

## Hypothesis

When a cover already has one clear primary promise and a complete secondary contents index, additional topic bars over the hero photograph may repeat navigation rather than add meaning. Removing the duplicated bars should improve photo authority and hierarchy without losing content.

## Result

Verified. The blue and yellow secondary headline clusters were hidden while the primary pink promise and all six feature-index entries remained. Whole-item, reading-scale, and actual-size/structure QA passed without crop, geometry, text, semantic-node, fold-guide, or rollback regression.

## Failure / limit

This is not evidence that all secondary cover lines are unnecessary. Keep them when they add unique context, urgency, category, or navigation that does not exist elsewhere. Do not remove the only clear cover promise.

## Adopted status

`ADOPTED_FOR_RURUBU_V5_CURRENT`

## Next application

On future cover reviews, map every photo overlay to unique information before styling it. If an overlay merely repeats a complete contents index, test subtraction first and compare at thumbnail, reading, and actual-size scales.

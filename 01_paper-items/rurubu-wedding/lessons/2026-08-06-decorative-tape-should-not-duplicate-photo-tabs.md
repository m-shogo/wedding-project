# Decorative Tape Should Not Duplicate Photo Tabs

Date: 2026-08-06
Status: `VERIFIED FOR V5 / NOT YET PROJECT_RULE`

## Source

Live Rurubu V5 back cover, Friends & Family nodes `77:92–77:95`, `77:104`, and `77:105`.

## Hypothesis

When a photo tab already supplies identification, color contrast, and attachment-like geometry, a second translucent tape strip behind it may add visual noise without adding meaning.

## Result

Hiding the two tape strips preserved the Friends tabs, native text, photographs, captions, reading order, fold guide, image hashes, semantic structure, and rollback nodes. Whole-item and reading-scale review showed a cleaner two-photo module without an empty gap or loss of navigation.

## Failure / limit

This is not a blanket rule against tape or scrapbook devices. Tape may remain useful when it is the only attachment cue, organizes an otherwise ambiguous overlap, or contributes intentionally to the item concept. It should be rejected when it merely shadows an already complete label.

## Adopted use

For Rurubu V5, test removing secondary tape layers before adding or restyling photo labels. Retain the native-text locator and verify all three scales.

## Next application

Audit remaining sticker, tape, badge, and shadow combinations for semantic duplication. Do not remove unique content or necessary contrast devices.

# Timeline and Page Heading Can Replace a Photo Kicker

Date: 2026-08-06
Status: `VERIFIED FOR V5 / NOT YET PROJECT_RULE`

## Source

Live Rurubu V5 inside-right history page, nodes `77:466`, `77:467`, the `OUR HISTORY` heading, chronological timeline, and dominant history image `77:422`.

## Hypothesis

When a page heading and a complete chronological device already establish section meaning, an additional color-bar kicker placed over the lead photograph may duplicate semantics and weaken the photograph without improving navigation.

## Result

Hiding the yellow kicker and its repeated label preserved the history heading, timeline, dates, event labels, dominant photograph, caption, memory-spots section, native text, image hashes, fold guide, semantic structure, and rollback frames. Whole-item, reading-scale, and actual-size/structure review found no empty gap, collision, clipping, text reflow, crop regression, or hierarchy loss.

## Failure / limit

This is not a blanket rejection of photo kickers. A kicker can remain when it adds unique editorial context, required contrast, or navigation that is not already conveyed by the heading, chronology, caption, or adjacent module structure.

## Adopted use

For Rurubu V5, test subtraction when a photo kicker restates the page heading or timeline. Preserve the dominant photograph and the more direct native editorial devices, then verify all three scales.

## Next application

Audit remaining labels only for demonstrable semantic duplication. Do not let decorative subtraction outrank unresolved image provenance and quality gates.

## Why W / BT / BS replaced V / BR / BQ

V/BR/BQ had already removed much of the old card/dashboard grammar and passed structure QA, but the system still read too beige, sober and template-like at 500px whole-item scale.

W/BT/BS test a stricter editorial rule:

- use existing photography as the page mass;
- keep all factual/variable copy native;
- keep photographs replaceable;
- add only a small number of high-contrast flat labels/rules tied to real editorial roles;
- do not rebuild a repeated card system;
- do not add generic gradients/shadows;
- do not adopt generated section decoration merely because it exists.

The result is materially more legible as a travel-information magazine at thumbnail scale while preserving editability.

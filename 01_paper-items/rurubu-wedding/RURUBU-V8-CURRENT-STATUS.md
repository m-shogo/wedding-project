## Latest professional learning

### RSL-206 — functional index vs reading gateway

Fresh references:

- Eye on Design / AIGA, *Five Beautiful Book Covers and the Stories Behind Them*: cover design can be the culmination of the internal concept, logic and systems of a book.
- Designing With Type, *Typographic Book Cover*: hierarchy should be tested through logic, content and clarity within the fixed format rather than through arbitrary decoration.

Rurubu hypothesis: when a back-cover index has a genuine reader entry point, equal visual weighting can make correct navigation feel generic. Before adding decoration, test whether the navigation hierarchy can reflect how the publication is actually entered and read.

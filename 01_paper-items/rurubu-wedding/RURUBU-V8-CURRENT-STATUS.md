## Latest professional learning

### RSL-211 — facing-spread unity must come from semantic role contrast, not arbitrary geometric choreography

Fresh references used as hypotheses, not copied styles:

- MoMA / Irma Boom `SHV Think Book`: typography, layout, materials, printing and reader journey operate as one book system.
- D&AD / Donna Payne on Book Design: page-turn experience and physicality can be integral to why a design belongs specifically to the book medium.
- Designing With Type / Double-Page Spread: publication design coordinates display/body type, heads, captions, folios, illustrations and grid decisions across the spread.

Rurubu hypothesis: a facing spread should feel unified through complementary editorial jobs and content-owned scale. Do not manufacture unity by scattering/staggering text unless reading order, time, location, image subject, fold, caption relationship or another semantic/physical role owns the movement.

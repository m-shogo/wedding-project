## Professional references shaping V8

- JAGDA 2026 selected `here and there Vol. 1 – Vol. 13.5` by Kazunari Hattori as Book & Editorial Design. This supports testing the publication as a book system and sequence, not a set of isolated magazine modules.
- Pentagram / Sight and Sound demonstrates that strong editorial identity can use visible grids, typography and deliberate pace contrast while changing image treatment by spread.
- W3C JLReq / Japanese Script Resources remain the typography/line-composition technical baseline.

V8 hypothesis: professional warmth can come from sequence, typography, indexing, folios, text rhythm and controlled image fields—not from adding more cards or decoration. The risk is becoming so restrained that a travel publication loses desire and energy.

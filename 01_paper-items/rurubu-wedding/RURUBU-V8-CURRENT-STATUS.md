## Latest verified design improvement — 1DAY AT3

A common-scale comparison of V6 JA `2141:2`, V7 C6 `2316:2` and V8 AT `2264:2` showed that AT had the strongest restraint and easiest book-like reading, but at thumbnail scale its zero-image composition became more abstract than place-specific. The next test therefore targeted the relation between **time, page space and place evidence**, not decorative polish.

Fresh professional research moved to Kohei Sugiura's book-design practice through Musashino Art University Museum & Library's `Design Cosmos` archive. The useful observation was not a surface style: Sugiura treated the book as a time/space medium and repeatedly made relationships legible through spatial organization. The Rurubu-specific hypothesis was that a model-course spread can let temporal relationships affect page rhythm, but must not turn reader-facing itinerary data into a self-conscious design diagram.

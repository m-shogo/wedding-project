## Latest verified improvement — contents navigation without unverified ordinal structure

### New/deeper professional research

This run rotated to table-of-contents and book-navigation practice. Professional editorial examples treat a contents page/list as an entry and navigation device rather than decorative proof of “bookness”. Other book structures deliberately omit conventional chapter divisions or move a table of contents when those conventions do not help the reading experience.

Rurubu-specific hypothesis: V8 can keep a useful semantic overview of the book while withholding ordinal prefixes until final section order is authoritative. The goal is not to remove contents navigation, but to separate navigation meaning from unverified sequence metadata.

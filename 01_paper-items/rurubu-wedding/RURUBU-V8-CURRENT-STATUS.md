## Latest verified improvement — interview/profile support copy must frame the editorial job, not invent personality

### New/deeper professional research

This run rotated to interview/profile art direction. Society of Publication Designers' Anna Alexander describes the attraction of *Interview* as a strict Q&A format paired with portraiture, and emphasizes photographs meaningful enough to stand on their own rather than depending on explanatory editorial furniture. ori.studio's `Da大` similarly lets the conversation dominate and arranges typography to support that conversation. The useful principle for Rurubu is not a surface style: the interview material should carry the person; support copy should frame the reader's job rather than manufacture personality to make a sparse page feel finished.

### New/deeper professional research

This run rotated to whole-publication/photo-book editing and sequencing. Aperture's Stuart Smith material treats picture edit and sequence as central to what a book is, not as decoration added after page templates. Aperture production guidance also stresses that edit, sequence, paper, binding and page-turn affect each other.

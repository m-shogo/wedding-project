# Rurubu V8 Cafe/Table AF — design-learning feedback

Date: 2026-08-22
Scope: Rurubu WEDDING only

## Before

Cafe AC `2226:2` was structurally clean and intentionally quiet. Its left page already had a useful lived-in sensory rhythm, but the right Dinner page still relied on a small intermediate rule and did not give the semantic close enough weight.

## New professional knowledge that changed the decision

This run focused on the book as a two-page/physical reading system rather than treating each Figma page as an isolated poster.

The useful decision principle was not 'make more things cross the gutter'. It was: **a spread should feel unified through complementary editorial jobs, page-turn cadence and content-owned hierarchy.**

## Failed experiment

AE `2230:2` deliberately exaggerated a diagonal progression of left-page sensory text toward the gutter.

Why it failed:

- the geometry became the idea instead of the content;
- the result looked more like a graphic-design demonstration;
- Japanese closing copy wrapped awkwardly;
- the extra movement did not improve reading order or meaning.

The failure changed the next method, so AE is preserved as hidden rejected evidence rather than erased.

## Corrected experiment

AF `2230:26` started again from AC.

- left Cafe page: unchanged;
- right Dinner title: stronger opening mass;
- action line: retained as support;
- emotional close: stronger terminal mass;
- short rule: removed because its binding job could not be defended at whole-spread scale;
- no added image, card, badge, gradient, shadow or decorative English.

## Learning effect

The system should now be less likely to respond to a quiet spread by adding arbitrary diagonal movement or other pseudo-editorial choreography. First test whether one page simply needs clearer article-owned scale or whether a real binder exists.

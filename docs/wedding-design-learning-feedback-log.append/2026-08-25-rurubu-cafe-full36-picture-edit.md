Date: 2026-08-25
Scope: Rurubu WEDDING only

## New researched knowledge

Food/editorial photography is not reliably designed by filling a generic rectangle with a pretty image. Professional food photo production deliberately coordinates photo editing, photographer, food/prop styling and shot selection to produce appetite, context and publication-specific meaning.

The live decision changed in two ways:

1. **V7 H10**: stop treating its existing dummy rectangles as final contracts. Select three distinct photo jobs first (Cafe dominant / transition / Table dominant) and picture-edit the set before deciding crop/area.
2. **V8 AS7**: do not reuse V7's three-photo grammar. Test one dining-essay image that can hold food + gesture + place while preserving restrained book rhythm.

This becomes RSL-271. The lesson is about verifying resulting geometry, not banning auto-layout.

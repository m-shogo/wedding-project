Observed main before this write: `123adece88c253a719061459f2e2b37a3e142a50`

## Visible problem

The V6 direction is now substantially stronger, but it still carries a Yokohama/travel-memory visual identity. A materially different Hawaii-oriented V7 was required, and the new project-wide hybrid authoring rule needed to be proven in an actual Rurubu redesign rather than remaining documentation only.

## Research input

Current public JTB Hawaii/Honolulu guide references were reviewed for genre grammar only. The useful cues were high tropical color energy, dominant ocean/destination photography, large Japanese/destination headline hierarchy, compact food/travel support imagery and asymmetric travel-guide density.

No protected logo, exact page, source wording or literal cover composition was copied.

## Design method tested

- native/editable copy only for wording and factual data;
- stable clipped `MASK / ...` roles for every replaceable photograph;
- no rounded UI cards, shadows or decorative Figma micro-geometry;
- use photo scale, overlap, crop and Japanese typography to create visual energy;
- keep destination-specific photography as a separate semantic asset problem rather than faking it with text.

## Candidate A

Outer A `1244:2` established the coral/aqua Hawaii mood and photo-led skeleton, but remained too evenly arranged and read closer to a poster/landing-page system.

Decision: `SUPERSEDED`; hidden after B was verified.

## Candidate B

Outer B `1245:2` changed relationships rather than adding decoration:

- Japanese headline became dominant;
- photos use unequal scale;
- flatlay/cafe supports overlap and rotate asymmetrically;
- front and back have deliberately different rhythms;
- all wording remains native;
- six photo roles remain replaceable masks.

Result: preferred Outer study.

## Inside A

Inside A `1247:2` was created separately instead of recoloring V6 I:

- left = profile/Q&A;
- right = history/NEXT TRIP;
- native factual/profile copy remains editable;
- six photo roles remain masks;
- profile title was corrected after actual-size QA showed the first wording was too tight.

Result: preferred Inside study.

## Important rejection / blocker

The ocean/coast source is useful for composition testing but is **not a verified Hawaii master**. It is therefore explicitly a mood placeholder and cannot be called literal Hawaii photography.

Final V7 hero needs a role-specific Hawaii/Oahu asset with:

- roughly `1.89:1` hero crop;
- believable ocean/beach/tropical-island cues;
- no fake signage/text;
- no synthetic luxury-stock polish;
- real travel-editorial detail;
- text-safe region for native Japanese copy;
- sufficient print resolution;
- Drive master/provenance/readback before adoption.

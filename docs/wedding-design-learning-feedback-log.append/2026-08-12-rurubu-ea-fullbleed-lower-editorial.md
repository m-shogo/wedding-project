# Rurubu EA — photo-led subtraction lesson

Date: 2026-08-12
Scope: Rurubu WEDDING

- Visible problem: a strong destination hero can still collapse into brochure rhythm when the lower half returns to a light information field plus a framed photo.
- Principle tested: subtract large UI-like color fields first, then let one verified photo own the lower page and attach text directly to photography with only one compact caption band and one thin rule.
- Expected improvement: stronger whole-cover continuity, more varied photographic scale, denser but readable travel-magazine rhythm, less landing-page/card geometry.
- Regression risk: dark-photo caption contrast, z-order hiding support fields, text boxes colliding when numbers and titles are compressed, low-value micro-copy falling into photo seams.
- Evidence: EA Working `1024:2`, front `1024:131`, Review `1027:2`; 500px thumbnail, 1000px whole-spread, 794×1123 actual-size front; final visible native text 36, IMAGE fills 7, same-parent text intersections 0.
- Adopted: YES. EA selected over DV.
- Rejected states: initial dark feature-03 text; temporary dark ink-card treatment; feature-02/03 fields behind image; feature-01 micro-description at photo seam. All repaired or subtracted before promotion.
- Next application: when a print piece feels too digital, first test whether the bottom/secondary zone can become one photographic field with text hierarchy attached to the image rather than adding another container.

## New knowledge that changed a decision

### Destination ownership

Nippon Design Center's `GQ Japan` localization case treats research, typeface choice, grid and paper as one localization system. The useful project hypothesis was not to copy its look, but to make the issue's real place/topic own the hierarchy before adding decorative local motifs.

Applied result: Outer U promotes native `横浜` into the primary destination mass and demotes the existing generic generated atmosphere field.

### Editorial stance over pseudo-editorial residue

`SUBKAMITURE` and Irobe Design Institute material reinforced that page experience and editorial stance should determine graphic treatment. Combined with prior RSL-198, this changed Profile P's lower-page decision.

Applied result: Profile V removes the low-opacity decorative `01`, removes redundant marginalia and promotes existing article-owned closing copy instead.

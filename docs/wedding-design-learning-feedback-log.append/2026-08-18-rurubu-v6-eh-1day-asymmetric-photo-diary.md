# Rurubu V6 feedback — EH 1DAY asymmetric photo diary

Date: 2026-08-18

## Observation

EG's route/content hierarchy was already useful, but its four STOP photos still looked like repeated rectangular modules.

## Hypothesis

The page needed stronger photographic rhythm rather than more labels, cards or generated decoration.

## Experiment

Cloned EG to EH and varied only photo scale, position and subtle rotation. Kept route rail, times, native copy, practical metadata and replaceable image sources unchanged.

Initial candidate produced small unintended STOP03/04 text/photo overlaps. It was not adopted. Corrected geometry removed all unintended overlaps.

## Result

`ADOPTED / VERIFIED_LOCAL`.

EH is visually stronger at whole/reading/actual scales, while native text and replacement semantics remain intact. V7 unchanged.

## Next application

Use this only where repeated photo geometry is a demonstrated defect. Do not turn every Rurubu spread into the same rotated-polaroid motif.

## What changed

V8 Cafe/Table AS7 used the display line `料理、皿、手元、店の空気。`. The phrase matched the internal photo-direction concepts but read too much like a visible shot list at 58px display scale.

AS8 `2533:2` changes only that role to:

`一皿の向こうに、\n店の空気まで。`

AS7 `2454:25` remains hidden rollback.

## What new professional knowledge changed the decision

This run studied photobook picture editing and food-editorial production rather than reusing the same pagination/source-truth lessons. Aperture's bookmaking material reinforces that image edit, sequence and design decisions are editorial responsibilities, while Bon Appétit's food-photography process makes explicit that visuals, food styling, prop styling and production form an internal collaboration system. SAVEUR's historical cover practice also shows that final reader-facing image language can be documentary and natural rather than a display of the production mechanism itself.

Before this research, it would have been easy to keep polishing the existing noun list because it accurately described the desired photograph. The research changed the question from `is the semantic concept correct?` to `whose language is this — the photo team’s or the reader’s?`.

## Before / after learning check

**YES.**

New knowledge materially changed a live design decision:

- before: keep valid photo-role vocabulary as display typography;
- after: keep that vocabulary inside the photo authority, translate the publication-facing layer into a reader sentence, and let the photograph eventually prove the shot requirements visually.

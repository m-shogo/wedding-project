# Rurubu WEDDING — EL profile-collage clean-room learning

Scope: RURUBU WEDDING V5 only.

Visible problem: verified Best Inside EG still read as two nearly equal rectangular profile photographs above a conventional information field. At thumbnail scale the profile page felt closer to a clean wedding brochure than an energetic Japanese travel-information spread.

Principle tested: subtract panel thinking and create hierarchy through a dominant photograph, a smaller overlapping photograph, slight editorial rotation, direct-on-photo native Japanese display type, and unequal scale while preserving the verified identity-safe images and all factual/native text.

Experiment: safe duplicate EL `1086:2` from EG `1057:2`. Groom image became the dominant field; bride image became a smaller white-edged overlapping portrait. Initial title placement at the photo/cream boundary became visually muddy and was rejected. The final title/subtitle were moved directly onto the dominant photograph in solid native white type, with no new card, gradient, or shadow.

Expected improvement: stronger magazine recognition at thumbnail scale, clearer profile hierarchy, less equal-grid perception, and a more print-native collage rhythm.

Regression risk: crop changes could weaken identity-safe framing; display type could collide with people or lose contrast; overlap could create safe-area or text-box issues.

Evidence: EL whole spread reviewed at 500px thumbnail and 1000px reading scale; left page reviewed at natural 794×1123; Review snapshot `1089:2` re-rendered after promotion. Final structure: 53 visible native text nodes, 6 visible IMAGE fills, 0 same-parent text intersections, 0 bounded safe-area text risks, fold `1086:283` at x=792.7 with 2px width and 1122.5 height. All six inherited image hashes were preserved.

Decision: ADOPTED. EL promoted over EG as Best Inside. EG Review snapshot `1058:2` remains hidden rollback. Current `77:18 / 77:290` was not changed.

Next application: prefer unequal photo hierarchy and direct-on-photo type over equal profile panels; rendered glyph fit remains mandatory even when collision checks pass. Q60 cover lifecycle remains a separate open blocker and was not counted as progress in this experiment.

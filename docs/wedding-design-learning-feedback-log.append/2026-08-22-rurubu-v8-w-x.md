## New knowledge that changed a decision

### Reader voice before internal taxonomy

Official D&DEPARTMENT material for `d design travel` emphasizes discovering the real character of a place, writing in the editor's own truthful words, and avoiding visual exaggeration merely for spectacle.

Applied result: Outer W retains a functional index but changes its labels from internal English section taxonomy to reader-facing Japanese semantic roles already present in the publication. Redundant English residue is removed only where it no longer performs a job.

### Editorial structure before scattered graphic effect

Fresh IDEA material on Takeo Nakano describes book design as editorial work designed from a book's fundamental structure and relationship to the reader, not only graphic surface treatment.

Applied result: Cafe X converts four scattered semantic food/place words into a deliberate two-line native reading sequence while retaining support and closing copy.

# Rurubu V7 C6E — grounded planning-memo model-course feedback

Date: 2026-08-25
Scope: Rurubu WEDDING only

## What changed in judgment

New professional research on travel-guide reader utility changed the decision from `keep truth-safe generic dayparts` to `use a real planning source, while preserving its source status`.

The important learning was not visual style. It was recognizing three distinct truth states:

- generic structural dummy;
- grounded **plan**;
- verified completed-event/final fact.

C6D was safe but generic. C6E is more useful because it is grounded in the user's 2025 Hawaii planning memo, while the page visibly states that the information is a travel memo/plan and that update-sensitive booking/event details must be reconfirmed.

## Before / after learning check

YES — the new knowledge materially changed the design decision.

Without this research, the likely next pass would have polished C6D's generic `朝 / 昼 / 午後 / 夜` rhythm or added more visual styling. Instead, the page's reader job and source authority were reconsidered first.

## Professional critique summary

- Art direction: stronger publication personality because the page now contains specific, source-owned Hawaii planning information rather than generic itinerary filler.
- Editorial design: stronger reader utility; exact times and named stops now have a source-status explanation.
- Book design: V7 gains a deliberately text/information-heavy tempo beat instead of another photo-module spread.
- Typography: unequal stop treatment works; two geometry collisions were found and fixed before promotion.
- Photo editing: unverified right-page dummies were withheld so the named places are not falsely authenticated.
- Print: design-scale QA passes, but final printer/template/preflight/proof gates remain open.

## Failure-learning behavior

The first type-led candidate had two text collisions. They were detected by structure readback despite the screenshot looking broadly acceptable. The candidate was corrected before promotion.

No new Figma/tool failure fingerprint was created because this was not a repeated or distinct production-method failure.

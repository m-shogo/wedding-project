# Wedding design learning feedback — Rurubu EZ

Date: 2026-08-13
Scope: Rurubu WEDDING only.

For editorial print work, a composition can be photo-heavy yet still feel digital if photography is partitioned into evenly stacked horizontal sections. EZ confirms that continuity and overlap matter more than adding assets: one larger image field, asymmetric overlap, native Japanese type, and a single role-specific caption strip outperformed the prior segmented hierarchy.

The experiment also reinforced two QA rules. First, actual-size rendered glyph/caption review is mandatory because small 1–2px display-type collisions are easy to miss at thumbnail scale. Second, rotated text/photo groups must be checked in absolute coordinates; same-parent layout intuition is insufficient.

Do not generalize the cyan band as a reusable UI card. Its accepted use is narrow: it is physically attached to Feature 02 photography and exists to solve caption contrast while preserving a magazine-cutout rhythm.

# Rurubu V7 G10 — asymmetric verified-photo evidence feedback

Date: 2026-08-25

## New knowledge used

This pass deliberately moved away from another microcopy/grid cleanup and studied travel-photo storytelling / picture editing: publication photography should be selected for scene, subject, detail, transition and narrative responsibility rather than for filling every nominal module.

## Decision changed by the new knowledge

Before this pass, G9 still visually behaved as if `沖縄 / 韓国 / ハワイ / 横浜` all deserved photographic coverage, even though several visible images were explicitly structural dummies and verified place evidence was asymmetric.

The new decision was not “replace every dummy with a Hawaii photo.” Instead:

- use verified real-couple Hawaii imagery only where it truthfully contributes human/destination evidence;
- do not claim the images document the proposal event;
- withhold unrelated place dummies for Okinawa / Yokohama and an unrelated secondary role;
- let typography carry the remaining travel-guide beats.

This produced G10 `2563:35`, which is stronger at thumbnail and reading scales while reducing false documentary implication.

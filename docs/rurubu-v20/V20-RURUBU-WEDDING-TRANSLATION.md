## Current page roles through a wedding lens

### P01 — Wedding Special Issue Cover
Promise the couple, wedding date and travel-magazine excitement immediately.

The couple remains the protagonist even when environmental travel photography is strong.

### P02 — Meet the Couple + Q1/Q2
Show personality and recognizable details without employee-card symmetry or a six-question dump.

### P03 — Their Story + Q3/Q4
Tell the relationship progression with emotional/chronological meaning. Travel imagery supports the story but does not become a destination gallery.

### P04 — Travel Memories
This is the strongest interior travel page.
Destinations are real memories, not recommendations or internet travel facts.

### P05 — Friends Memories Only
Current role:
- `友達との思い出`
- `SHOGO FRIENDS`
- `SHIORI FRIENDS`
- about 3–4 final photos per cluster when sources support it
- no family
- no single giant hero

This page is about friendship and shared time, not a destination, proposal or family archive.

### P06 — Real Life + Q5/Q6
Show ordinary life, pets/food/fun when grounded, relaxed personality and playful contrast.

### P07 — Thank You / Closing
End the interior story warmly with one calm photo and short approved closing message.

This is not a discovery guide, timetable, seating/table guide or venue information page.

### P08 — Magazine Back Cover
Close the physical object like a restrained magazine back cover.
Minimal meta, optional tiny names/date, one tiny motif and decorative barcode digits exactly `2026102400000`.

It is not the main emotional thank-you page.

## Wedding warmth without template clichés

Avoid default shortcuts:
- endless hearts
- generic script `Love`
- rings/doves/champagne everywhere
- rose-gold luxury-template styling
- fake vows/quotes
- generic romantic filler

Wedding feeling comes from:
- real people
- real memories
- real relationship story
- friends
- ordinary life
- sincere closing copy
- wedding date
- authentic photography

## Travel motif dosage

Airplanes, routes, tickets, stamps and tropical cues are optional vocabulary.

`TRAVEL MOTIF MUST SERVE PAGE ROLE OR READING FLOW.`

P04 may use the most literal travel language.
P05 should use much less travel-coded decoration so it reads as friends, not another destination page.
P07/P08 use very little.

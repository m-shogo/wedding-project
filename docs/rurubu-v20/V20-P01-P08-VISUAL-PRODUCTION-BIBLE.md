# 1. TARGET TRAVEL-MAGAZINE ENERGY — CALIBRATION, NOT QUOTAS

The target is the browse excitement of a high-quality Japanese travel information magazine: dense, layered, highly discoverable, tactile, but still immediately readable.

These ranges are **calibration bands**, not mechanical quotas. A page may sit outside a range when the page job clearly benefits.

## Common dense-page calibration

A high-energy page/spread often contains:
- `FIRST READ`: 1 dominant event;
- `SECONDARY EVENTS`: 2–4 distinct medium-weight events;
- `MICRO DISCOVERIES`: roughly 4–12 small rewards;
- `PHOTO ROLES`: 1 dominant image plus unequal satellites/details when content supports them;
- `DEPTH LEVELS`: visually apparent interaction across roughly 5–9 semantic layers;
- `EDGE EVENTS`: usually 1–3 noncritical elements intentionally crop/enter bleed;
- `CALM ISLAND`: roughly 12–30% perceived area where the eye/body copy can recover;
- `COLLISION`: at least one composition-defining interaction involving three or more roles, e.g. photo + title + subtitle, or photo + route + vessel + stamp.

The book should **not** use these numbers identically on all pages.

## Scale hierarchy

Prefer a visibly unequal sequence:
- 1 giant event;
- 2–4 medium events;
- many tiny events.

Avoid a page where title, photos, vessels and labels all occupy comparable visual weight.

## Layer principle

Prepared assets should frequently behave as **units** rather than sticker atoms.

Examples:
- title + subtitle holder + burst + route fragment;
- article field + chapter tab + edge texture + marker;
- photo frame + backing paper + caption tab + tape;
- destination shell + tiny icon + route cue + cut-paper collision.

Figma should primarily compose these units, place/crop photography and overlay native factual text.

# 3. GLOBAL GENERATED-ASSET RULES

## 3.1 Zero-base visual creation

Legacy SVG or SVG-derived PNG aesthetics are not visual references. Preserve only the required job. Redesign silhouette, material, edge, color, iconography, depth and composition from zero.

`KEEP THE JOB; REDESIGN THE OBJECT.`

## 3.2 Prepared PNG families

Preferred asset classes:
- `TITLE_UNIT`;
- `SUBTITLE_UNIT`;
- `ARTICLE_UNIT`;
- `PHOTO_FEATURE_UNIT`;
- `CAPTION_UNIT`;
- `DESTINATION_UNIT`;
- `PROFILE_UNIT`;
- `DISCOVERY_UNIT`;
- `EDGE_CLUSTER`;
- `ROUTE_COMPOSITE`;
- `MICRO_ICON_FAMILY`.

## 3.3 Technical expectation

- high-resolution PNG;
- true alpha when transparency is needed;
- target effective resolution at placement: 300 ppi+ preferred;
- no baked checkerboard;
- no authoritative long copy baked into art;
- no random glyphs/fake paragraph;
- no glossy generic 3D logo treatment;
- no corporate reusable icon-pack look;
- no visual dependency on rejected old SVG silhouettes.

## 3.4 Human copy-fit contract

The visual vessel may be designed before final copy is exact.

Native copy may later be:
- shortened;
- line-broken;
- edited;
- slightly resized within the approved readable band;

so that strong vessels do not have to be weakened to fit temporary draft wording.

# 4. P01 — COVER / DEPARTURE

## Editorial promise

Within three seconds: joyful travel-magazine special issue, the couple are protagonists, today is the destination, and the page is rich enough to open.

## Current baseline

Current P01 is one of the strongest surfaces. Treat it as a protected baseline, **not a permanent lock**.

## Target visual recipe

- `FIRST READ`: original wedding-travel masthead/title;
- `PHOTO`: 1 dominant real HERO + 1–2 support/photo details;
- `SECONDARY EVENTS`: 2–4 cover hooks / issue cues;
- `MICRO DISCOVERIES`: 4–8;
- `DEPTH`: 6–9 visually interacting levels;
- `EDGE EVENTS`: 2–3, especially top/right/lower edge;
- `CALM`: small, mostly around factual names/date/location.

## Desired silhouette

Not centered. Masthead should create a wide irregular top event and physically interact with the hero/photo-frame environment. Lower hooks should not form a neat horizontal card row.

## Prepared PNG package to test only if candidate can beat current

1. `P01_MASTHEAD_TITLE_UNIT`
   - masthead art;
   - subtitle/issue-holder zone;
   - one small burst/tab;
   - designed quiet zone for native names/date if needed.
2. `P01_BOTTOM_EDITORIAL_HOOK_UNIT`
   - 2–3 unequal cover-hook vessels in one composition, no fake text.
3. `P01_EDGE_TRAVEL_CLUSTER`
   - page-edge travel/plant/print cues, deliberately partly cropped.
4. optional `P01_SUPPORT_PHOTO_FEATURE_UNIT`
   - one support-photo backing + caption tab + small accent.

## Principal collision script

`BACKGROUND → HERO → support frame/photo → masthead → small hook vessel partly over HERO → edge cluster behind/above selected layers → native facts → tiny meta`.

At least one title/photo interaction should be deeper than a simple 5% overlap.

## Reject candidate when

- masthead becomes a standalone logo floating above photo;
- cover gains equal-weight stickers everywhere;
- couple loses visual dominance;
- current is still more immediate at thumbnail scale;
- micro copy looks fabricated.

# 5. P02 — WHO WE ARE / PROFILE

## Editorial promise

Meet two distinct people quickly, then reward a second look with personality facts and shared-life cues.

## Current baseline / gap

The asymmetric people-first structure works. The remaining risk is looking like a polished profile UI rather than a travel-magazine personality feature.

## Target visual recipe

- `FIRST READ`: WHO WE ARE / Japanese kicker relationship;
- `PHOTO`: 2 main people/profile roles + 1–2 lifestyle/detail satellites when real sources allow;
- `SECONDARY EVENTS`: 3–4 (SHOGO cluster, SHIORI cluster, Q&A/shared-life, lower profile index);
- `MICRO DISCOVERIES`: 5–9;
- `DEPTH`: 6–8;
- `EDGE EVENTS`: 1–2;
- `CALM`: 18–25%, distributed as readable fact-support areas rather than one giant empty field.

## Desired silhouette

One portrait/photo has more weight than the other; the opposite side answers with a stronger title/fact cluster. Do not mirror left/right halves.

## Prepared PNG package

1. `P02_TITLE_UNIT`
   - WHO WE ARE display treatment;
   - separate Japanese subtitle holder;
   - one quiet micro-tab.
2. `P02_SHOGO_PROFILE_UNIT`
   - image-bound name/fact support;
   - 2–3 short fact quiet zones;
   - tiny annotation cue.
3. `P02_SHIORI_PROFILE_UNIT`
   - materially different silhouette from SHOGO, same publication DNA.
4. `P02_QA_OR_SHARED_LIFE_UNIT`
   - title/support/icon interaction;
   - quiet zone for native answer only if grounded.
5. `P02_PROFILE_INDEX_UNIT`
   - editorial index/strap that feels printed, not a form row.
6. `P02_MICRO_ICON_FAMILY`
   - 2–4 tiny page-specific cues only.

## Collision script

`title intersects top photo environment → main portrait overlaps profile vessel → support photo or badge covers a profile-field edge → index strap partly intersects Q&A/shared-life → micro marker sits across paper/photo boundary`.

## What should remain native

- names;
- factual profile text;
- actual Q&A answers;
- factual labels when they could change.

## Reject when

- left/right profile geometry becomes symmetrical;
- every fact has an isolated pill;
- generated title is louder than the people;
- micro icons become a sticker pack;
- body text must shrink below comfortable A5 size.

# 6. P03 — OUR STORY / QUIET HIGH-DENSITY FEATURE

## Editorial promise

Slow down after P02 and understand the relationship as a short emotional article, without turning it into a corporate timeline.

## Current baseline / gap

Current calm behavior is useful. The remaining issue is that “calm” can accidentally become “clean design page.” Target **quiet high density**, not emptiness.

## Target visual recipe

- `FIRST READ`: OUR STORY + emotional anchor;
- `PHOTO`: 1 strong anchor + 1–2 supporting memories where grounded;
- `SECONDARY EVENTS`: 2 episode units + 1 arrival/today marker;
- `MICRO DISCOVERIES`: 3–6;
- `DEPTH`: 5–7;
- `EDGE EVENTS`: 1;
- `CALM`: 28–38%, primarily body/article field.

## Prepared PNG package

1. `P03_TITLE_UNIT`
   - quieter than P02/P05;
   - title + short subtitle holder;
   - no giant burst.
2. `P03_EPISODE_01_UNIT`
   - chapter tab + paper edge + native-text zone + one tiny marker.
3. `P03_EPISODE_02_UNIT`
   - different material/silhouette; avoid repeated cards.
4. `P03_NARRATIVE_ARTICLE_UNIT`
   - stable article paper with one intentionally intruding visual edge.
5. `P03_TODAY_ARRIVAL_UNIT`
   - stronger than a normal episode but smaller than main title.
6. optional `P03_ROUTE_THREAD`
   - restrained, semantically connecting episodes.

## Collision script

`anchor photo enters title area → Episode 01 partially overlaps photo or its backing → Episode 02 crosses article-field edge → restrained route disappears behind one vessel → arrival marker breaks the article baseline`.

## Copy behavior

Final episode facts/copy remain native. The generated vessels should support editing from shorter to moderately longer real copy without redesign.

## Reject when

- page looks like a vertical timeline;
- both episodes use identical cards;
- extra decoration eliminates the deliberate slowdown after P02;
- article field floats separately with no physical interaction.

- `DEPTH`: 6–9;
- `EDGE EVENTS`: 2–3;
- `CALM`: 18–28%, but calm area must still contain editorial intention (route/mini-note/negative-space function).

## Prepared PNG package

1. `P04_JOURNEY_TITLE_UNIT`
   - title + subtitle holder + small route-origin cue.
2. `P04_PLACE_PHOTO_FEATURE_UNIT`
   - HERO support/frame only if the real photo benefits; avoid overframing.
3. `P04_ACTION_CAPTION_UNIT`
   - photo-bound caption + small travel/action cue.
4. `P04_DETAIL_DISCOVERY_UNIT`
   - detail frame + micro label + icon/marker.
5. `P04_LOWER_MEMORY_UNIT`
   - intentionally fills/activates part of the current lower calm area without becoming a fourth giant panel;
   - may be a short note, tiny postcard/detail, route waypoint or destination fragment.
6. `P04_ROUTE_BACK_SECTION`
   - fold-safe portion designed to go behind selected photo/vessels.
7. `P04_EDGE_CLUSTER`

## Collision script

`title cuts into HERO → ACTION overlaps HERO edge → DETAIL crosses ACTION/route zone → route appears behind HERO, re-emerges lower, then aims to fold → lower memory unit sits on/near route without stopping flow`.

## Reject when

- P04 becomes as loud as P05;
- all three photos get the same frame treatment;
- lower area is filled with arbitrary stickers;
- route becomes a decorative line unrelated to memory roles.

# 13. FULL-BOOK MICRO-DISCOVERY DISTRIBUTION

Do not distribute the same number/type of small objects evenly.

Working direction:
- P01: 4–8 cover/editorial micro cues;
- P02: 5–9 profile/personality cues;
- P03: 3–6 story cues;
- P04: 5–9 travel-memory cues;
- P05: 4–8 peak/arrival cues;
- P06: 5–10 everyday/fun cues;
- P07: 8–15 discovery cues;
- P08: 1–3 closing cues.

A “micro cue” can be a tiny caption, icon, marker, short note, page-edge fragment, stamp edge, mini number or detail image. It is not automatically a sticker.

# 14. CANDIDATE REVIEW SCORE — BEFORE ADOPTION

Do not auto-adopt generated assets because they are technically valid.

For every serious candidate, score qualitatively:

1. `PAGE JOB`: strengthens the page’s real promise?
2. `FIRST READ`: clearer/stronger at 3 seconds?
3. `SCALE`: better giant/medium/tiny inequality?
4. `LAYER DEPTH`: produces meaningful front/back interaction?
5. `PHOTO`: improves rather than decorates/obscures important photography?
6. `VESSEL`: feels page-authored rather than generic component?
7. `MICRO DISCOVERY`: adds something worth noticing?
8. `ANTI-AI`: less grid/card/sticker-pack/template feeling?
9. `BOOK RHYTHM`: improves spread/full-book tempo?
10. `A5`: survives actual size and print geometry?

Reject if the candidate merely makes the page busier.

# 17. FINAL PRINCIPLE

V20 should not look like a clean Figma layout decorated with travel stickers.

It should look like a physical magazine composition in which:
- photography is unequal;
- titles behave as graphic masses;
- subtitles are secondary editorial events;
- article/photo/caption vessels are designed objects;
- routes and marks travel behind and in front of layers;
- small discoveries reward browsing;
- some objects are intentionally hidden/cropped;
- dense pages still contain readable calm islands;
- every page has its own silhouette;
- the eight-page sequence has a clear rise, peak, human reset, discovery burst and exhale.

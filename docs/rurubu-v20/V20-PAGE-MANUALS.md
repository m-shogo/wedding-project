# V20 Page Manuals — P01 to P08

Status: `CANONICAL_PAGE_EXPLANATIONS / P05_FRIENDS_ONLY_2026-09-01`

This file follows `V20-CURRENT-PAGE-ARCHITECTURE.md` and `V20-PAGE-BY-PAGE-DESIGN-SPEC.md`.

Global rules:
- A5 portrait / 8 pages
- `RURUBU FEEL = 100%`
- information density is 75% of previous max-clutter tests
- wedding date `2026.10.24`
- real photos replaceable
- factual/personal copy native/editable
- no fabricated memories/captions

## P01 — COVER

Job: make guests want to open the booklet immediately.

Photo plan: 1 dominant hero + 0–2 supports.

Content: `るるぶ WEDDING`, SHOGO & SHIORI, `2026.10.24`, 1–2 hooks.

Density: 7.5/10.

## P02 — PROFILE + Q1/Q2

Job: quickly understand SHOGO and SHIORI.

Photo plan: 2 main portraits/personality photos + optional 1 shared support.

Content: 3–5 grounded facts per person + exactly Q1/Q2.

Do not add story chronology, travel archive or friend memories.

Density: 6/10.

## P03 — OUR STORY + Q3/Q4

Job: tell how the relationship progressed to the wedding day.

Photo plan: 2–3 preferred, max 4 if page still breathes.

Content: 3–4 grounded chapters + exactly Q3/Q4.

P03 owns proposal relationship meaning; P04 may show Hawaii as a travel memory.

Density: 5–5.5/10.

## P04 — ALL TRAVEL MEMORIES

Job: strongest interior travel-magazine page.

Photo plan: 1 hero + 4–5 unequal supports/details.

Content: verified Okinawa/Korea/Hawaii/other trips, 3–4 memory clusters, short personal captions.

Do not use generic tourism facts or equal destination cards.

Density: 8/10.

## P05 — FRIENDS MEMORIES ONLY

Job: show friendship memories, separated clearly into SHOGO and SHIORI friend clusters.

Reading path:
1. `友達との思い出`
2. `SHOGO FRIENDS`
3. `SHIORI FRIENDS`
4. friend photos
5. short captions

Photo plan:
- SHOGO FRIENDS: about 3–4 photos
- SHIORI FRIENDS: about 3–4 photos
- total about 6–8
- **no single giant hero/anchor photo**

Composition:
- two editorial territories, not two rigid UI cards
- medium/small photos with controlled inequality
- each side may have one somewhat larger photo, but neither becomes a page-wide centerpiece
- light overlaps inside each cluster
- enough calm space that SHOGO/SHIORI grouping is obvious
- faces readable at A5

Parts:
- friends-memory title
- SHOGO FRIENDS label
- SHIORI FRIENDS label
- varied photo frames/backings
- short caption vessels
- restrained tape/camera/heart/travel-memory accents

Density: 7/10.

Hard rejects:
- family content
- FAMILY label/cluster
- giant hero photo
- old Hawaii/proposal/arrival role
- old Hawaii title/proposal vessels as current authority
- friend directory with tiny labels
- invented friend identities/relationships/anecdotes

## P06 — REAL LIFE + Q5/Q6

Job: show ordinary, fun, relaxed life.

Photo plan: 1 candid hero + 3–4 supports/details.

Content: everyday couple, Cookie/Melon, food, hobbies/fun as real material supports + exactly Q5/Q6.

Density: 6.5/10.

## P07 — CLOSING / THANK YOU

Job: emotionally close the interior.

Photo plan: 1 strong calm photo.

Content: short thank-you, SHOGO & SHIORI, `2026.10.24`, one restrained final motif.

Density: 3.5–4/10.

Do not restore old guide/schedule/11-destinations content.

## P08 — MAGAZINE BACK COVER

Job: look like a quiet real magazine back cover.

Photo plan: 0–1.

Content ceiling: tiny issue/meta, optional names/date, one small motif, barcode `2026102400000`.

Density: 2–3/10.

No long thank-you, collage, fake publisher/price/ISBN/JAN claims.

## Spread logic

- P02–P03: `WHO → STORY`
- P04–P05: `PLACES WE REMEMBER → FRIENDS WE REMEMBER`
  - P05 is friends-only
  - SHOGO FRIENDS + SHIORI FRIENDS
  - no family
  - no giant P05 hero
- P06–P07: `REAL LIFE → THANK YOU`
- P08: standalone back cover

`P05 = FRIENDS ONLY. SHOGO FRIENDS + SHIORI FRIENDS. NO FAMILY. NO GIANT HERO.`

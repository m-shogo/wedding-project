## 1. Design thesis

V20 should feel like:

`Japanese travel editorial + personal scrapbook tactility + real wedding memories`

It should NOT feel like:
- a Canva wedding template;
- a web dashboard;
- a luxury brochure with empty margins;
- a direct replica of a commercial magazine brand;
- an AI collage with random stickers.

Core principle:

> High energy comes from hierarchy, scale contrast, crop, overlap and editorial clustering — not from filling every gap.

Book-wide calibration:
- `RURUBU FEEL = 100%`
- `EDITORIAL DENSITY = 75% OF PREVIOUS MAXIMUM-CLUTTER TESTS`
- `READABILITY = HIGH`

The 75% target reduces clutter/information count only. It does not reduce the travel-magazine character.

## 2. Five visual levels

Most pages may use:
1. `FIRST READ` — title or primary visual event;
2. `SECOND READ` — main photo/story cluster;
3. `EDITORIAL SUPPORT` — secondary photos/labels;
4. `FACTUAL COPY` — names, dates, captions, Q&A, story copy;
5. `MICRO DISCOVERY` — tiny stamp, arrow, ticket fragment, doodle or meta detail.

Not every page needs all five levels.
P07 and especially P08 intentionally use fewer.

A page fails when four or more elements compete at the same scale/contrast.

## 3. Composition grammar

Use:
- asymmetric clusters;
- strong large/small image contrast;
- partial overlap;
- 2–3 meaningful anchor zones rather than even distribution;
- edge cropping where appropriate;
- one calm/readable field inside busy pages;
- mixed print-object treatments only when they serve different semantic roles.

Avoid:
- equal card grids;
- four identical image tiles;
- equal spacing everywhere;
- all-centered text;
- one Auto Layout stack controlling a whole editorial page;
- repeated pill badges;
- generic soft SaaS shadows;
- decorations distributed evenly merely to fill space.

## 4. Scale contrast

Use obvious hierarchy before adding decoration.

General working heuristic:
- energetic pages usually have one unmistakable dominant event;
- support photos use 2–3 visibly different scale classes;
- thumbnail view should not collapse into similar rectangles.

### Critical P05 exception

P05 is deliberately different.

Current P05 = `友達との思い出` with:
- `SHOGO FRIENDS` cluster;
- `SHIORI FRIENDS` cluster;
- roughly 3–4 final photos per cluster when sources support it;
- no family content;
- **no single giant page-wide HERO/anchor photo**.

P05 still needs photo-size variation, but the largest photo must not dominate the whole page.
Think `balanced unequal clusters`, not `one hero + satellites`.

## 5. Photo behavior

Photography carries the autobiographical truth.

Useful roles:
- HERO;
- PORTRAIT;
- TRAVEL;
- CANDID;
- FRIEND MEMORY;
- DETAIL;
- CUTOUT;
- BACKGROUND SUPPORT.

General rules:
- real photos remain independently replaceable;
- preserve faces/subject focal points;
- frame art stays separate from image source;
- avoid blind hero-photo repetition across pages;
- decoration supports real photography rather than replacing it;
- do not create generated fake autobiographical memories.

Page-specific rule overrides generic photo grammar.
Therefore P05 does not inherit a mandatory HERO role.

Working rotation range for paper/photo objects:
- normally 0–6 degrees either direction;
- stronger angles only when composition needs them;
- do not rotate every photo.

## 6. Overlap grammar

Purposeful overlap creates tactile editorial depth.

Good examples:
- title overlaps a photo edge;
- flower/leaf cluster enters bleed and crosses one frame corner;
- tape/stamp crosses one printed-photo edge;
- route disappears behind one P04 travel object and reappears;
- cutout subject breaches a backing field;
- P05 friend photos overlap slightly within each cluster while faces stay readable.

Bad overlap:
- body text covered;
- faces/eyes blocked;
- every object overlaps everything;
- overlap used to hide weak spacing.

Typical target:
- energetic pages: about 2–5 meaningful overlap relationships;
- P07: 0–2;
- P08: 0–1.

## 7. Edge tension

Energetic pages may activate page edges through:
- foliage/flowers;
- photographic crop;
- abstract color/paper shape;
- decorative stamp fragment;
- P04 travel route/line;
- noncritical pattern or texture.

Critical copy, faces, eyes, dates and essential labels remain safely inside trim/fold zones.

Do not force every edge to be active.

## 8. Working V20 color family

Working publication family:
- `OCEAN` #17A6EE
- `SKY` #5BC1EB
- `HOT_PINK` #F83176
- `SOFT_PINK` #F16596
- `SUN` #F1C120
- `SAND` #F3C069
- `LEAF` #508849
- `PAPER` #FDF6E8
- `INK` #1C2430
- `WHITE` #FFFFFF

These are working editorial jobs, not a requirement to use every color on every page.

Page rule:
- one dominant accent;
- one support accent;
- optionally one surprise accent;
- PAPER/INK stabilize readability.

## 9. Current color rhythm by page

- P01: OCEAN + HOT_PINK + SUN; maximum opening energy at 75% information load.
- P02: PAPER dominant with lively person-specific accents.
- P03: warm PAPER/SAND with restrained emotional pink and small blue progression cue.
- P04: photography dominant; OCEAN route/travel cues + limited SUN/HOT_PINK accents.
- P05: warm PAPER/cream/coral/navy human-memory mood; OCEAN only as a small publication link to P04.
- P06: PAPER/light field with playful accents; less travel-coded than P04.
- P07: calm photo/paper field, restrained reprise accent only.
- P08: quietest field; one small publication accent at most.

P04 and P05 are adjacent but must not look like two destination pages.

## 10. Typography grammar

Japanese is primary.

Roles:
- masthead / display title;
- feature/section heading;
- body;
- caption;
- Q&A;
- micro meta.

Working A5 ranges before actual-size proof:
- page title ~22–34 pt equivalent;
- section title ~13–20 pt;
- body ~9–10.5 pt;
- captions ~8–9 pt;
- micro meta ~7.5 pt floor for noncritical information.

Rules:
- never shrink important text to rescue an overloaded layout;
- authoritative Japanese words remain native/editable unless a generated title treatment is explicitly approved;
- long body copy is never baked into raster decoration;
- meaningless decorative English is avoided.

## 11. Display-title behavior

Use selectively:
- thick color + white/cream keyline;
- hard offset shadow;
- ribbon/backplate;
- marker/brush underline;
- small subtitle;
- one overlapping travel/flower/paper element.

Do not use every treatment on every title.

Page differentiation matters:
- P01 = masthead-scale mass;
- P02 = people-feature title;
- P03 = quieter story title;
- P04 = strong travel-feature title;
- P05 = warm friend-memory title with clear SHOGO/SHIORI sublabels;
- P06 = playful everyday-life title;
- P07 = restrained closing phrase;
- P08 = little or no large title.

## 12. Shape language

Prefer:
- straight-edged photo paper;
- irregular editorial paper shapes;
- circle marker;
- ticket/notch shape;
- stamp ring;
- ribbon;
- hand-drawn line;
- organic foliage silhouette.

Limit generic rounded-rectangle cards.

A rectangular support should feel like editorial paper/ticket/label material, not a UI card.

## 13. Texture / tactility

Optional, restrained:
- paper grain;
- print-dot/halftone accent;
- stamped ink imperfection;
- tape/paper edge;
- hand-drawn underline.

Texture must not harm body-copy readability or become fake-vintage noise.

## 14. Calm-zone rule

Busy pages still need recovery space.

Every copy-heavy page identifies at least one calm field where:
- background is stable;
- line length is comfortable;
- decoration does not invade body copy.

Page-specific behavior:
- P03 has a clear narrative calm field;
- P05 uses breathing space between SHOGO and SHIORI clusters rather than one central hero;
- P07 is mostly a calm field;
- P08 is overwhelmingly calm.

## 15. Current spread rule

### P02–P03
`WHO → STORY`

`REAL LIFE → THANK YOU`
- P06 retains playful photo energy;
- P07 deliberately slows down.

Standalone magazine back cover.

## 18. Originality / brand distance

V20 should evoke the excitement and editing behavior of Japanese travel magazines without claiming official affiliation or tracing commercial trade dress.

Therefore:
- use original page compositions;
- derive editorial principles rather than exact layouts;
- do not trace official commercial logo vectors;
- use reference imagery as taste/energy calibration only.

## 19. Anti-AI / anti-template test

Immediately revise if a page shows:
- 3+ equal cards in a clean grid;
- same radius on all images/boxes;
- every section centered;
- generic gradient background;
- soft shadow on all cards;
- meaningless sticker filling;
- identical photo counts/positions on adjacent pages;
- tiny text used to preserve too much content;
- fake facts or generated autobiographical claims;
- P05 accidentally forming one giant hero + tiny satellites;
- P07 accidentally returning to a guide/map/schedule page.

## 20. Final principle

`PAGE ROLE FIRST → HIERARCHY → REAL PHOTOGRAPHY → EDITORIAL COLLISION → CALM/READABILITY → MICRO DISCOVERY LAST.`

# Book-wide editorial principle

The booklet should feel strongly inspired by a real high-energy Japanese travel magazine, but the current target is not maximum clutter.

Use roughly `75%` of the previously tested maximum visual clutter:
- one strong first read;
- 2–3 meaningful secondary reads on energetic pages;
- fewer, better micro discoveries;
- large photography remains visible;
- do not fill every empty area;
- local calm zones are intentional;
- no tiny-information dumping.

`AUTHENTIC MAGAZINE ENERGY > NUMBER OF ELEMENTS.`

# P01 — COVER / るるぶ WEDDING

## Job
Make guests immediately want to open the booklet.

## Core content
- approved `るるぶ WEDDING` masthead direction;
- SHOGO & SHIORI;
- `2026.10.24`;
- YOKOHAMA when useful;
- one strong couple/Hawaii hero image;
- 1–2 smaller cover hooks;
- optional 1–2 small support photos/details only if they improve the cover.

## Do not put here
- detailed profile;
- full story chronology;
- long table of contents;
- many tiny feature lists.

## Density
`7.5/10` — energetic but cleaner than the earlier maximum-clutter tests.

# P02 — PROFILE / WHO WE ARE

## Job
Let guests understand each person quickly.

## Core content
- SHOGO profile;
- SHIORI profile;
- 2 main portraits or personality photos;
- approximately 3–5 short facts per person when real facts exist;
- optional shared mini feature or maximum 1–2 short Q&A items.

## Do not put here
- relationship timeline;
- full travel history;
- family/friend history;
- many Q&A blocks;
- wedding-day guide content.

## Density
`6/10` — lively people feature with enough breathing room.

# P03 — OUR STORY

## Job
Explain how the two became a couple and arrived at the wedding day.

## Core content
Use 3–4 short story chapters, for example only when grounded:
- meeting / early outings;
- becoming a couple;
- proposal;
- marriage registration / arriving at the wedding day.

Use 2–4 real photos and short copy. Exact dates are optional unless confirmed.

## Do not put here
- detailed travel destination gallery;
- family/friend photo archive;
- rigid corporate timeline with many nodes.

## Density
`5–5.5/10` — quieter narrative page.

# P04 — ALL TRAVEL MEMORIES / OUR JOURNEY

## Job
This is the single page where the couple's travel memories are gathered together.

## Core content
Travel memories live here, including destinations such as Okinawa, Korea, Hawaii and other verified trips/photos selected later.

Recommended structure:
- 1 dominant travel hero;
- 4–6 unequal support/detail photos;
- 2–4 destination/memory clusters;
- short captions or micro memories;
- route/stamp/travel graphics only when they connect real memories.

Hawaii/proposal may appear here as a travel memory, but P04 is not required to become a Hawaii-only page. The proposal story itself may also be referenced on P03.

## Do not put here
- family/friend memory archive;
- wedding-day table destination guide;
- generic travel facts copied from the internet;
- equal destination cards.

## Density
`8/10` — the most travel-magazine-like interior page, but still below maximum clutter.

# P05 — FAMILY & FRIENDS MEMORIES

## Job
Show the people and relationships that surrounded the couple before this wedding day.

## Core content
- family memories;
- friend memories;
- real group/candid photos;
- short, grounded captions;
- visually separate but connected FAMILY / FRIENDS clusters when the photo pool supports it.

Recommended photo plan:
- 1 emotional or representative anchor;
- 4–6 supporting memories;
- no need to identify every person or relationship unless the user supplies/approves the wording.

## Do not put here
- Hawaii feature-door content merely because the old layout had it;
- invented friend/family anecdotes;
- an exhaustive people directory;
- wedding-day logistics.

## Density
`7/10` — warm, lively and human, with large recognizable photos.

# P06 — REAL LIFE / FAVORITES / BEST SHOTS

## Job
Show what the couple are like in ordinary life beyond formal wedding/travel storytelling.

## Core content
- everyday couple photos;
- food where personally meaningful;
- Cookie / Melon when real photos are available;
- hobbies/fun moments;
- best shots / funny or relaxed moments;
- short captions.

Recommended photo plan:
- 1 candid hero;
- 3–5 support photos;
- one small pet/food/fun cluster as material allows.

## Do not put here
- duplicate P04 travel heroes;
- long prose;
- fabricated handwritten quotes;
- equal Instagram-grid tiles.

## Density
`6.5/10` — playful but readable.

# P07 — CLOSING MESSAGE / THANK YOU

## Job
End the interior story emotionally before the physical back cover.

## Core content
- one calm strong photo;
- short thank-you / closing message;
- SHOGO & SHIORI;
- `2026.10.24`;
- one restrained closing/continuation motif if useful.

Recommended message budget:
roughly 40–100 Japanese characters, subject to final user copy.

## Do not put here

- `11 DESTINATIONS`;
- `LOOK AROUND`;
- `EDITOR'S PICK`;

`3.5–4/10` — deliberate emotional release.

# P08 — BACK COVER / MAGAZINE BACK COVER

## Job
Look like the restrained back cover of a real magazine, not another editorial feature page.

## Core content
Keep information minimal.

Allowed/default elements:
- subtle publication/issue mark;
- SHOGO & SHIORI only if composition needs it;
- `2026.10.24` only if composition needs it;
- one tiny decorative travel/wedding mark;
- decorative barcode near the conventional lower-right/lower area;
- barcode digits exactly `2026102400000`;
- optional very small legal/editorial-style decorative line, but do not invent real publisher/legal claims.

Background may be:
- calm photo;
- quiet color/paper field;
- restrained travel/wedding artwork.

## Do not put here
- long thank-you message;
- photo collage;
- many feature hooks;
- QR unless explicitly supplied later;
- fake price/publisher/ISBN/JAN claims presented as real commerce metadata;
- `11 DESTINATIONS` or old P07 content.

## Density
`2–3/10` — the quietest page in the booklet.

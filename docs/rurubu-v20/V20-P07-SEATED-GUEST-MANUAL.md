# V20 P07 — TODAY'S TRAVEL GUIDE / LOOK AROUND THE WEDDING

Status: `CANONICAL_P07_OVERRIDE / DISCOVERY_FIRST / NO_SCHEDULE_LEGACY`

This file supersedes every older schedule/timetable-first P07 concept, including the obsolete P07 section inside `V20-PAGE-MANUALS.md`.

Historical timetable text may remain in Git for provenance only. It has zero current production authority under `V20-LEGACY-QUARANTINE.md`.

## Page job

Guests are already seated. P07 makes the physical wedding around them more fun to notice, discuss and remember.

It is not a timetable, dress-code, access, seating or arrival page.

## First read

`TODAY'S TRAVEL GUIDE`

Second-read anchor:
- giant `11` / `11 DESTINATIONS AROUND YOU` family;
- native exact wording remains editable.

Supporting metadata can include `YOKOHAMA / 2026.10.24` when useful.

## Editorial thesis

`The couple's travel story is now all around the reader inside today's wedding.`

## Reading path

1. giant title collision;
2. giant `11` / destination-discovery second read;
3. 3–4 visually stronger destinations;
4. remaining destination discoveries at smaller scale/depth;
5. 2–5 `LOOK AROUND` / verified wedding-detail discoveries;
6. one stronger `EDITOR'S PICK` detail;
7. micro guide discoveries / stamps / arrows / route cues;
8. a small resolve connecting today's venue back to the couple's journey.

## Content modules

### 1. DESTINATION CLUSTER

Current grounded candidate themes require final wording/order confirmation:
- Hawaii;

This is editorial atmosphere/discovery, not a seating chart.

Do not attach table numbers, guest names or navigation functions unless separately confirmed and intentionally requested.

### 2. LOOK AROUND

Target 3–6 verified wedding details, e.g. only after confirmation:
- destination-themed decor;
- flowers / venue decoration;
- travel-related paper/signage visible in the room;
- cake/design detail;
- couple-made item;
- real hidden/easter-egg-like detail.

Usually 1–3 lines each, not paragraphs.

### 3. EDITOR'S PICK

One stronger real detail/story with slightly more space. Do not invent a feature simply because the layout wants a box.

## Composition verb

`MAP + SCATTER + CIRCLE + POINT + COLLIDE + DISCOVER`

Not:
`TABLE + GRID + TIMETABLE + LIST`.

## Visual silhouette

- title enters upper-left/top and overlaps journey art strongly;
- giant `11` participates in title/background depth;
- one large map/route/travel composite can occupy roughly 55–70% perceived page area;
- destination vessels vary in silhouette/scale/depth;
- one large editorial vessel sits partly on the journey composition;
- 2–4 smaller discovery vessels use different silhouettes;
- arrows/route cues point into actual content;
- one calm field protects the strongest longer detail;
- noncritical destination/artwork may crop at edges/bleed.

Do not make eleven identical chips or six equal rounded cards.

## Strong overlap

Encouraged:
- title over journey art + destination vessel;
- giant `11` behind/through title;
- vessel over route/map/photo;
- stamp over vessel/photo;
- route under one label and over another via BACK/FRONT assets;
- discovery note covering part of journey art;
- small verified detail photo crossing the map/journey boundary;
- edge cluster partly hiding noncritical decorative shell.

## Information vessels

Use a related but varied family:
- 2–3 stamp shells;
- 1–2 ticket/tab forms;
- 1 large irregular destination slab/burst;
- 1–2 paper-note forms;
- 1 circular/oval locator;
- 1 vertical strip/tag;
- some text-only destination names with keyline/shadow.

The family shares print DNA, not identical geometry.

## Photography

P07 can succeed with zero photos.

If verified venue/wedding-detail photography improves discovery:
- use roughly 1–3 small details maximum;
- layer them into the map/route composition;
- do not convert P07 into another gallery;
- never use unrelated generated/stock venue imagery as factual evidence.

## Density

Target `HIGH / roughly 8/10`.

At thumbnail:
- TODAY'S TRAVEL GUIDE;
- giant `11`;
- 3–4 stronger destinations
must survive.

At A5:
- remaining destinations;
- LOOK AROUND details;
- EDITOR'S PICK;
- micro discoveries
reward closer reading.

Dense page calibration can support roughly 8–16 small visible discoveries after hierarchy works.

## Native text

Keep native/editable:
- title when not intentionally generated display art;
- destination names;
- count wording;
- wedding-detail descriptions;
- date/location;
- couple-intention explanation;
- any factual prompt/question.

Generated art contains no authoritative paragraph text.

## Explicit exclusions

- dress code;
- access/station/parking;
- check-in/reception instructions;
- RSVP;
- seating-chart function;
- menu/drink list owned elsewhere;
- ceremony/reception timetable as primary content;
- fake airline/flight/gate data;
- generic wedding etiquette;
- pre-arrival utility.

## Anti-cheap / anti-AI failures

Reject when:
- destination labels are an even grid;
- all discoveries use identical cards;
- page resembles dashboard/map app/seating chart/timetable;
- travel icons are evenly distributed to fill gaps;
- old SVG/icon-pack design returns;
- title, giant `11`, background and vessels do not physically interact;
- every detail has identical copy length/visual weight;
- page could belong to any generic wedding because no grounded detail exists;
- bright colors/stickers are carrying the style while the composition remains weak.

## Completion test

Within ~5 seconds the guest understands `look around today's wedding / 11 destination discoveries`.

Then the page should reward actual observation and conversation.

The ideal response is curiosity, not logistical compliance.

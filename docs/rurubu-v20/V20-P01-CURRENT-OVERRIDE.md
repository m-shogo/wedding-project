## Authentic cover target

P01 should read immediately as a convincing Japanese travel-magazine cover, not a wedding card with a travel theme.

Target behaviors:
- the canonical masthead/title is a dominant graphic mass, roughly cover-defining rather than timid;
- saturated red/yellow/cyan-blue/navy/cream energy where the current photos support it;
- thick display lettering, strong keyline and hard offset-shadow behavior;
- environmental HERO + clearly smaller but forceful personality/support photo;
- multiple cover hooks / short secondary reads;
- 1–2 strong medium editorial vessels;
- active edge/bleed events;
- several micro discoveries only after cover hierarchy works;
- title, photos, frame, vessel, stamp/route physically collide;
- enough calm/local support to keep native names/date/location readable.

North-Star calibration matters more than preserving current decorative geometry.

## Production method

First use the page Bible to create a full-page art-direction proof if useful, but use the canonical masthead asset rather than generating a substitute by default.

If the proof convincingly reaches the target:
1. identify successful background/color behavior;
2. preserve verified real HERO/support photos as replaceable sources;
3. place the canonical `るるぶ WEDDING` masthead PNG as the primary title unit;
4. generate subtitle/cover-hook vessels around it;
5. generate photo-frame/backing and edge cluster units;
6. generate route/stamp/medium editorial collisions as needed;
7. assemble in Figma from back to front;
8. keep names/date/location/variable factual copy native where additional text is required;
9. add micro discoveries only after the main composition is strong;
10. compare against current baseline at thumbnail, page, contact sheet and A5 actual size.

## Reject

Reject a P01 candidate if:
- it looks cleaner but less magazine-like;
- the canonical masthead is made small/polite or visually weakened;
- photos sit inside isolated cards;
- old SVG/vector aesthetics return;
- it is only red/yellow/blue stickers over a normal wedding layout;
- it has many micro objects but weak HERO/title collision;
- it looks like a social template rather than a real printed travel-magazine cover;
- it substitutes a generic masthead without explicit review.

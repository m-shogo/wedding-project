## 7. Editable/native boundary

Keep independent:

- Shogo/Shiori names;
- date/location;
- factual cover hooks;
- issue/meta copy;

Generated:
- masthead/display art;
- vessel shells;
- photo-frame art;
- edge clusters;
- stamps/route/decor without variable facts.

## 8. Anti-cheap rejection

Reject if:
- small/plain masthead;
- centered logo/photo/date wedding-card layout;
- equal photo cards;
- generic old SVG/vector icons;
- symmetrical floral frame;
- evenly sprinkled stickers;
- `red + yellow + blue` with weak hierarchy;
- every asset fully visible;
- weak/no title-photo collision;
- looks like a social-media/wedding template rather than an edited travel-magazine cover.

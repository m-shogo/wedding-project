# V20 Rurubu Editorial Design Decode

Status: `CANONICAL_DESIGN_DECODE / HIGH-FIDELITY_EDITORIAL_PHENOMENA / CURRENT`

Purpose: explain the visual/editing phenomena that make a high-quality Japanese travel magazine feel dense, joyful, tactile and professionally authored, so V20 can reproduce that experience deliberately.

This is not a timid `travel-inspired` style guide. V20 should push strongly toward a convincing Rurubu-like editorial experience while re-authoring the actual pages for this wedding.

For current private/noncommercial masthead and homage direction, read:
- `V20-LEGACY-QUARANTINE.md`;
- `V20-PRIVATE-HOMAGE-AND-LAYERED-PAGE-GENERATION-RULE.md`;
- `V20-P01-CURRENT-OVERRIDE.md`.

## 0. Core conclusion

The target feeling does **not** come from red/yellow/blue, tropical stickers, maps or travel icons by themselves.

It comes from a coordinated editorial system:

`EDITORIAL IDEA`
→ `CLEAR READER PROMISE`
→ `DOMINANT PHOTO / TITLE GESTURE`
→ `EXTREME SCALE JUMPS`
→ `MULTI-SCALE INFORMATION HIERARCHY`
→ `ASYMMETRIC CLUSTERS`
→ `STRONG LAYER COLLISION`
→ `PURPOSE-BUILT INFORMATION VESSELS`
→ `ACTIVE EDGES`
→ `MICRO DISCOVERIES`
→ `EMBEDDED CALM ISLAND`
→ `PAGE-TO-PAGE TEMPO`
→ `PRACTICAL READABILITY`

The success test is:

`THE PAGE FEELS RICH BEFORE THE READER READS THE BODY COPY.`

## 1. Editorial idea first

Every page/spread needs one sentence that explains what the reader is invited to enjoy.

Examples:
- P01: `This is the joyful travel-magazine cover for today's wedding journey.`
- P02: `Meet the two people behind today's wedding.`
- P03: `Follow how the two became us.`
- P04–P05: `Travel through the memories and hit the emotional Hawaii peak.`
- P06: `See who they are in ordinary life.`
- P07: `Look around: today's wedding itself is another travel spread.`
- P08: `The journey continues after this page.`

A page is weak if its only idea is `profile page`, `photo page`, `information page` or `cute page`.

## 2. First read must be unmistakable

A strong page gives one clear promise within about three seconds.

Usually:
- dominant headline silhouette;
- HERO photography;
- giant destination/number event;
- one large feature vessel;
- one route/diagram gesture;
- one unusual crop/cutout.

`ONE FIRST-READ EVENT > MANY EQUAL DECORATIONS.`

For P01 the working private masthead may be `るるぶ WEDDING`, and it should behave like the dominant cover graphic mass rather than a small label.

## 3. Title silhouette is a graphic shape

Major titles are page-defining objects, not text labels.

Useful behavior:
- major scale jump from display title to body copy;
- thick outlined letters;
- white/cream keyline;
- hard offset shadow;
- 2-line irregular stack;
- attached ribbon/tab/burst;
- title crossing photography;
- title partly behind a subject/photo edge;
- title entering/cropping at page edge;
- diagonal/asymmetric placement when appropriate.

Reject timid centered headings floating above a clean grid.

## 4. Subtitle is a real second read

Subtitle should often behave as a generated/editorial object:
- ribbon;
- slanted paper strip;
- attached feature plaque;
- small burst;
- ticket fragment;
- strong native text on a generated holder.

It should physically interact with title/hero rather than merely sit below the heading as body typography.

## 5. Photo hierarchy is unequal

Use roles:
`HERO / EMOTION / PLACE / ACTION / DETAIL / LIFE / FUN / BRIDGE`.

Strong behavior:
- one photo clearly dominates;
- satellites differ drastically in size;
- crop shapes/aspects vary;
- some photos partly hide others;
- some break containment;
- tiny detail photos are allowed to remain tiny because discovery is their job.

Reject equal photo-card galleries.

## 6. Controlled high density

High-energy pages combine:
- 1 dominant event;
- 2–4 medium events;
- several to many micro discoveries;
- one local calm/recovery field.

North-Star extraction indicates roughly 8–18 small discoverable events can be appropriate on dense pages, but they must not be equal stickers.

Density needs a peak and a gradient.

## 7. Full editorial collision

Layer collision is a primary design language.

Useful patterns:
- title over HERO;
- subject/photo in front of title;
- support photo covering HERO corner;
- route under one object and over another;
- article vessel covering a substantial photo region;
- stamp partly hidden;
- flower/travel cluster entering bleed, disappearing behind photo and reappearing via foreground assets;
- caption half inside / half outside a photo;
- P07 giant `11` behind title/destination vessels.

`COLLISION MAY BE STRONG; READABILITY AND HIERARCHY MUST BE STRONGER.`

## 8. Text support is editorial art direction

Busy pages stay readable through local support:
- paper plate;
- irregular color slab;
- ribbon/band;
- keyline/outline;
- hard offset shadow;
- local contrast field;
- photo quiet zone;
- postcard/article field;
- selective local image darkening/lightening.

The support itself should feel authored for the exact page.

## 9. Information vessels form an ecology

Do not solve every content item with one component family.

Examples:
- FEATURE = slab / burst / large paper field;
- DESTINATION = stamp / locator / ticket / postcard vessel;
- BODY STORY = calm article-paper field;
- CAPTION = attached tab / mini note;
- PICK = stronger callout vessel;
- META = tiny strip / folio / issue mark;
- TODAY DISCOVERY = observation badge / destination stop / map fragment.

Related items share print DNA, not identical silhouettes.

## 10. Color has editorial jobs

High-energy Rurubu-like color may strongly use red/coral, yellow, cyan/blue, navy/ink and cream/white, but not as equal rainbow blocks.

Assign jobs:
- masthead/display emphasis;
- secondary title/subtitle;
- information support;
- destination distinction;
- micro accent;
- calm paper field.

Photo colors remain input. Do not mechanically paste the same palette distribution on every page.

## 11. Active edges make the page feel physically full

Energetic pages should often activate 2–4 edges/corners through:
- cropped title letters;
- photo corners;
- foliage/flowers;
- route lines;
- ticket/paper fragments;
- travel objects;
- color slabs;
- giant numerals.

Critical facts remain safely inside trim.

## 12. Real → generated bridge

Generated art is not an unrelated sticker layer.

Before generating:
1. inspect real HERO/support photos;
2. read source palette/light/negative space/crop behavior;
3. define page `LINE / COLOR / TEXTURE / DEPTH / ERA`;
4. generate page-specific artwork to bridge those sources;
5. place with real photography;
6. reject anything that looks imported from another design world.

## 13. One-shot page proof

A full-page generated mockup may be used to test whether the written page brief can actually produce the intended professional-magazine world.

This is a proof, not the final master.

If successful:
- analyze why it works;
- decompose into background, title, subtitle, frames, vessels, route BACK/FRONT, edge cluster and micro units;
- preserve real photos and factual copy as independent layers;
- reconstruct in Figma.

If the one-shot proof itself still looks like a wedding template, the written brief is not yet specific enough.

## 14. Page rhythm

- P01: VERY HIGH / cover impact;
- P02: HIGH / people feature;
- P03: MEDIUM / quiet high-density story;
- P04–P05: VERY HIGH / visual-emotional peak;
- P06: HIGH / candid playful collage;
- P07: HIGH / graphic destination-discovery guide;
- P08: LOW–MEDIUM / deliberate release.

Do not make all eight pages equally dense or equally sparse.

## 15. Anti-cheap diagnosis

Reject if:
- page identity comes mostly from bright colors;
- stickers are evenly sprinkled;
- generic old SVG/icon-pack aesthetic appears;
- title is too small;
- all photos are equal cards;
- all information lives in repeated rounded boxes;
- every element is fully visible;
- background is dead empty space rather than intentional calm;
- there is no deep front/back relationship;
- it looks like wedding stationery with travel decorations rather than a real edited travel magazine.

## 16. Current legacy boundary

Old V10/V11/V12 layouts, old SVG design grammar, old P07 schedule concepts, old permanent-lock statements and superseded mastheads are not current V20 visual authority.

Use `V20-LEGACY-QUARANTINE.md`.

Recover real facts and real source photography only when still valid.

## 17. Final principle

V20 should push hard enough that a guest's first reaction is:

`これ、ほんとに旅行雑誌みたい。`

not:

`旅行雑誌っぽいウェディングテンプレだね。`

The path to that result is authentic editorial behavior — title mass, photo inequality, edited abundance, tactile vessels, edge activity, deep collision and disciplined readability — not superficial style tokens.

## Global

- A5 portrait / 8 pages
- `RURUBU FEEL = 100%`

## Book silhouettes

- P01: BIG MASTHEAD + BIG COUPLE/ENVIRONMENT
- P02: TWO PEOPLE / OFFSET PROFILE + Q1/Q2
- P03: STORY ANCHOR + EPISODES + Q3/Q4
- P04: TRAVEL HERO + UNEQUAL TRIP CLUSTERS
- P05: TWO FRIEND CLUSTERS / NO GIANT HERO
- P06: CANDID REAL-LIFE HERO + SUPPORTS + Q5/Q6

## P01

Test one dominant couple/environment hero, limited support and strong masthead. Do not create a cover-index dump.

## P02

Two-person profile hierarchy with 2 main portraits/personality roles, 3–5 facts each and Q1/Q2. Avoid employee-card symmetry.

## P03

2–3 preferred photos, 3–4 grounded story chapters and Q3/Q4. Story order must work without forced dates.

## P04

Travel-memory page:
- 1 large elastic travel hero
- 4–5 support/detail roles
- 3–4 verified destination/memory clusters

Okinawa/Korea unresolved sources remain elastic; do not freeze exact aspect assumptions.

## P05 — FRIENDS ONLY

Greybox structure must prove the page **without any page-wide hero**.

Required:
- title zone `友達との思い出`
- SHOGO FRIENDS territory
- SHIORI FRIENDS territory
- about 3–4 photo masks per cluster in IDEAL state
- short caption support
- calm separation so cluster ownership is obvious

Test states:
- `MIN`: 3 SHOGO + 3 SHIORI photos
- `IDEAL`: 4 SHOGO + 4 SHIORI photos
- `REFLOW`: one photo removed from either side

No family/FAMILY zone.
No giant anchor.
No Hawaii/proposal/arrival zone.
No route crossing required.

Pass when thumbnail reads:
`友達との思い出 → SHOGO FRIENDS / SHIORI FRIENDS`, not one dominant people photo.

## P06

Test 1 candid hero + 3–4 supports/details + Q5/Q6. Layout should survive one support photo disappearing.

## P07

Current P07 greybox is a closing page:
- one strong calm photo zone
- short thank-you/message field
- names/date
- one tiny final motif

Do not include:
- TODAY'S TRAVEL GUIDE
- 11 DESTINATIONS
- LOOK AROUND / EDITOR'S PICK
- discovery modules
- timetable/schedule

## P08

Sparse back cover:
- 0–1 calm background/photo
- tiny meta
- barcode area with exact digits `2026102400000`

No large message field or collage.

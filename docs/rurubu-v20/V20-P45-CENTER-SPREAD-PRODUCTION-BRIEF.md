# V20 P04–P05 Center Spread — Production Brief

Status: `PRODUCTION_AUTHORITY_BEFORE_DECORATED_FIGMA`

Purpose: make the center spread the visual/emotional peak of the booklet through real travel photography, strong editorial collision, prepared route/print assets and one local calm proposal-reading island.

P04–P05 is not two independent pages. It is one 296 x 210 mm logical spread after trim, with the physical fold protected for critical content.

## 1. Spread story

The spread should read as one journey:

`DEPARTURE / EXPLORE`
→ `OKINAWA`
→ `KOREA`
→ cross-fold visual travel cue
→ `HAWAII / emotional peak`
→ `PROPOSAL`
→ `ARRIVAL / YOKOHAMA / 2026.10.24`

Exact dates/places beyond confirmed facts remain optional until verified.

## 2. Emotional hierarchy

The spread must NOT give every destination equal weight.

Working hierarchy:
1. Hawaii HERO / emotional peak;
2. strongest P04 destination HERO (likely Okinawa once real sources are confirmed);
3. Korea support/exploration cluster;
4. proposal story support field;
5. arrival/Yokohama marker;
6. micro travel discoveries.

If Okinawa/Korea real sources later prove stronger/weaker than expected, source behavior may change photo geometry without changing the spread story.

## 3. Full-collision composition model

This spread is allowed to be one of the most layered pages in the booklet.

Explicitly allowed:
- route under one photo, over another photo and under a stamp;
- destination title crossing photo + paper frame;
- postcard/photo stack covering large portions of another photo corner;
- proposal support paper overlapping Hawaii photography heavily;
- tropical edge art disappearing behind photo and reappearing in front via split assets;
- destination stamp partly hidden by another paper/photo object;
- giant destination numeral behind photo and title;
- paper/ticket fragments crossing the fold visually when noncritical;
- large title fragment spanning both pages if fold-safe.

The result should resemble one edited magazine feature, not separate cards on a map.

## 4. Fold safety

May cross the fold:
- route line;
- broad color/background field;
- noncritical illustration;
- giant decorative typography fragment;
- empty/low-information part of photography;
- print marks.

Do NOT rely on the fold for:
- faces/eyes;
- small destination copy;
- proposal paragraph;
- times/dates that must be read;
- critical locator marker;
- thin frame continuity that must align perfectly.

## 5. Photo behavior

### P04 / left side
Target final behavior: 3–5 real images if sources support it.

Roles:
- `P04_HERO / PLACE + PEOPLE`;
- `P04_SUPPORT / KOREA PEOPLE OR ACTION`;
- `P04_DETAIL / FOOD / OBJECT / PLACE`;
- optional small transition photo.

Current state:
- Okinawa/Korea real-photo pools are still source gaps;
- therefore no final aspect ratio is frozen;
- masks remain elastic until source review.

### P05 / right side
Current Hawaii raw-photo review supports:
- strong environmental HERO candidates;
- emotion candidates;
- personality/detail support.

Target behavior:
- one Hawaii HERO clearly dominant;
- one emotional support image;
- zero-to-two support/details;
- proposal copy does not require a separate literal “proposal event photo” unless a verified photo truly depicts it.

## 6. Prepared asset list

### `V20_P45_ROUTE_MASTER_A`
Job:
- create one continuous travel movement across the spread;
- connect real editorial clusters, not act as decorative map UI.

Production:
- transparent artwork;
- hand-drawn / printed route behavior;
- can include a few route nodes, arrows, tiny print marks;
- no destination names baked in;
- no fake distances/flight data;
- designed to weave under/over photography through z-order splits if needed.

Recommended delivery:
- `ROUTE_BACK_A` (under photos);
- `ROUTE_FRONT_A` (selected marks/arrow that can sit over photos);
so Figma can create real depth without drawing the route manually.

### `V20_P45_LEFT_EDGE_TRAVEL_CLUSTER_A`
Job:
- create P04 departure/exploration energy from the left/top/bottom bleed.

Composition:
- asymmetric edge foliage / small travel print cues;
- one possible ticket/postcard fragment;
- large transparent openings for real photography;
- designed to be intentionally cropped by bleed;
- no words.

### `V20_P45_DESTINATION_STAMP_SHELL_SET_A`
Job:
- visually unify destination labels without turning them into identical cards.

Set behavior:
- 3 related but not identical shells;
- different crop/shape/rotation opportunities;
- native `OKINAWA / KOREA / HAWAII` text layered in Figma;
- no fake passport authority/airline data;
- may sit half on/half off photo.

### `V20_P45_POSTCARD_FRAME_SET_A`
Job:
- support a few secondary travel photos with print-object tactility.

Set:
- 2–3 meaningful variants only;
- photo opening remains transparent;
- caption is native;
- frames should not make every photo look the same.

### `V20_P05_PROPOSAL_HIGHLIGHT_A`
Job:
- create emotional distinction without isolating the proposal into a sterile card.

Preferred behavior:
- warm paper/coral/cream support field;
- irregular editorial silhouette;
- may cover a substantial region of the Hawaii HERO/support photo;
- designed for 45–100 Japanese-character body copy plus short heading;
- one small attached stamp/tab possible;
- variable copy stays native;
- local calm island surrounded by dense travel collage.

### `V20_P05_ARRIVAL_MARKER_A`
Job:
- complete the journey toward the wedding day.

Possible behavior:
- arrival stamp / destination ticket / locator slab;
- native `YOKOHAMA / 2026.10.24` layered on top;
- can overlap route + photo/paper object;
- should feel like the final node of the spread story, not a footer.

### `V20_P45_MICRO_DISCOVERY_SET_A`
Only after first assembly proves actual need.

Possible contents:
- tiny camera mark;
- mini route arrow;
- small issue number;
- tiny star/heart;
- one travel-note scribble.

Do not produce/use if large composition already has enough density.

## 7. Destination-title behavior

Do not put `OKINAWA`, `KOREA`, `HAWAII` in three identical horizontal bars.

Possible differentiated behavior within one family:
- Okinawa: destination stamp partly inside HERO photo;
- Korea: compact ticket/vertical marker attached to support cluster;
- Hawaii: larger title crossing HERO + paper/frame boundary.

The destinations are related editorially, not identical UI components.

## 8. Background strategy

The center spread background can be active:
- warm paper base;
- very broad faint sky/ocean color field;
- giant cropped journey typography/numerals at very low informational priority;
- printed route marks;
- edge travel/tropical artwork.

Most of it may be hidden by photography and editorial objects. That is fine.

Do not flatten real photos or variable destination/fact text into the background.

## 9. Text-support strategy

Use multiple mechanisms:
- destination name: keyline / stamp shell / photo-negative-space;
- short caption: attached tab / photo caption foot;
- proposal story: opaque/near-opaque irregular paper field;
- arrival: ticket/stamp shell;
- tiny facts: direct type only when contrast is naturally safe.

No repeated white rounded info cards.

## 10. Scale jumps

At thumbnail scale, the spread should visibly contain:
- 1 very large Hawaii weight;
- 1 clearly large P04 weight;
- 2–4 much smaller photo/details;
- one clear proposal support shape;
- tiny stamps/route details.

Avoid 6 photos with nearly equal size.

## 11. Intentional occlusion

Plan at least several meaningful occlusions:
- one support photo covers HERO corner;
- one destination label is partly covered or extends beyond a photo edge;
- one stamp disappears behind another layer;
- route passes under and then over the collage through split assets;
- proposal field covers part of photo intentionally;
- one edge cluster is mostly cropped/hidden and acts as atmosphere.

Do not expose every asset completely.

## 12. What stays editable in Figma

Must remain independent:
- all real photos;
- destination names;
- captions;
- proposal heading/body;
- arrival/Yokohama/date text;
- any actual place/date facts;
- text-support dimensions if copy changes.

Prepared art:
- route back/front;
- travel/tropical edge composite;
- stamp shells;
- postcard/photo overlays;
- proposal support artwork shell;
- arrival support shell;
- micro illustration set if needed.

## 13. Anti-AI rejection criteria

Reject/redesign if:
- P04 and P05 look like two separate pages placed together;
- each destination is an equal card;
- route looks like a clean transit/map UI;
- every photo is a matching polaroid;
- all destination titles use one identical bar;
- proposal is simply another card in the grid;
- decorations are evenly sprinkled rather than clustered;
- all photography is politely separated;
- the spread becomes “travel stickers on cream background”;
- visual density is produced by micro stickers rather than large photo/title/paper collisions.

## 14. Center-spread QA

### Thumbnail
- one continuous spread story;
- Hawaii emotional peak obvious;
- P04 has enough weight to prevent right-side collapse;
- route/flow reaches the right side naturally;
- giant title/route/cluster behavior feels like one feature.

### A5/A4-open physical reading
- proposal copy readable;
- destination names clear;
- fold does not cut faces/critical facts;
- caption sizes comfortable;
- route is legible as editorial motion, not mistaken for functional navigation.

### Source-change resilience
- replacing Okinawa/Korea with alternate real-photo aspect ratios does not require rebuilding the entire spread;
- Hawaii HERO can move/crop while proposal support remains adaptable;
- destination stamp shells can move with photos.

## 15. Production order

1. locate/review Okinawa and Korea real sources as far as possible;
2. select temporary/approved Hawaii HERO behavior;
3. produce `P45_ROUTE_MASTER_A` as back/front pair;
4. produce left edge travel cluster;
5. produce destination stamp-shell family;
6. produce P05 proposal support asset;
7. produce arrival marker;
8. first decorated Figma assembly using only these major assets;
9. evaluate gaps at thumbnail + physical size;
10. only then produce postcard variants / micro discoveries that are truly needed.

The goal is not to complete an asset library. The goal is to complete one authored center spread.

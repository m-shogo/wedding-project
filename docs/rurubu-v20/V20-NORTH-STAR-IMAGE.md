## Meaning of "North Star"
This image is NOT a page template and NOT a composition to copy.
It is only the clearest current expression of the desired taste and editorial energy.

V20 should extract principles from it, then design every page from zero for the couple's actual content.

## Extracted qualities
- Japanese travel-magazine energy rather than wedding-template minimalism;
- high but controlled information density;
- strong title silhouettes;
- asymmetric editorial clusters;
- one dominant photo plus smaller satellites;
- mixed photo treatments: cutout, postcard, polaroid, circle, portrait, landscape;
- purposeful overlap between title, photo, frame and decoration;
- tactile travel objects: luggage, ticket, camera, airplane, stamps, routes, flowers;
- bright cyan / hot pink / yellow / coral / cream energy without equal-strength rainbow noise;
- page-edge tension and bleed-entering decoration;
- useful micro-discoveries and labels;
- readable calm fields embedded inside busy compositions;
- each page has a different composition silhouette while the whole booklet still belongs together.

## What must NOT be copied
- exact page order;
- exact boxes, coordinates or image positions;
- example people/content/text;
- exact color placement;
- exact number of photos;
- any page that does not fit the actual wedding content.

## V20 design question
Never ask: "How do we reproduce this page?"
Ask: "What visual principle makes this exciting, and how should that principle be re-authored for this V20 page's real job?"

# V20 Seated-Guest Editorial Audit

Status: `CANONICAL_CONTENT_REFINEMENT`

Purpose: audit what each page deserves to say now that V20 is explicitly a booklet read after guests are already seated.

This is an editorial audit, not a visual-layout document.

## Global test

Every page must do at least one of these well:
- reveal the couple;
- tell a real story;
- show real memories;
- create conversation;
- help guests notice/enjoy today's wedding concept;
- create emotional closure.

If a block only gives pre-arrival instructions, duplicates another paper item, or fills space with generic wedding content, remove it.

---

# P01 — COVER / DEPARTURE

## Deserves page space
- SHOGO & SHIORI;
- 2026.10.24;
- YOKOHAMA;
- 2–4 editorial hooks that preview the actual booklet;
- strong real couple photography.

## Best cover-hook content families
Prefer hooks such as:
- `ふたりのこと` / profile feature;
- `思い出の旅` / travel feature;
- `BEST SHOTS` / off-the-map life;
- `今日の会場にも旅がいっぱい` / today's seated discovery feature, if final wording is confirmed.

These hooks advertise reading value, not logistics.

## Remove
- dress code;
- access;
- schedule teaser;
- menu teaser if menu exists elsewhere;
- generic `WELCOME TO OUR WEDDING` filler if it competes with stronger editorial copy;
- fake magazine price/barcode/flight number unless clearly decorative and nonfunctional.

## Editorial risk
Do not overload P01 with six or more tiny fake coverlines just to imitate a magazine. Every coverline should point to real interior content.

# P02 — WHO WE ARE / PROFILE

## Page job
Give guests useful conversation material about SHOGO and SHIORI without reading like résumés.

## High-value facts, if real answers are supplied
Prioritize personality over administrative biography:
- personality / `どんな人？`;
- current obsession / hobby;
- favorite food/drink;
- favorite trip/place;
-休日の過ごし方;
- travel role: planner / navigator / food finder / photo taker / etc.;
- a short `相手から見ると` fact;
- one funny habit or real small fact.

## Lower priority
Use only if interesting to guests:
- hometown;
- job title;
- school history;
- long biography.

Do not force these because wedding profiles traditionally contain them.

## Recommended content count
- 3–6 strong facts per person;
- one shared-life/dog feature if real material exists;
- optional two short real Q&A answers maximum.

## Remove
- long self-introductions;
- ten identical fact rows;
- generic personality adjectives generated by AI;
- facts nobody at the table would naturally talk about.

# P03 — OUR STORY / HOW WE GOT HERE

## Page job
Explain the emotional sequence of the relationship in a short magazine feature.

## Strong grounded episode candidates
- early outings: bowling / board-game cafe / yakiniku / teamLab;
- relationship becoming closer;
- Hawaii proposal as a transition/teaser;
- marriage registration on 2026.02.11;
- arrival at the wedding day.

## Critical anti-duplication rule with P05
P03 must NOT tell the full Hawaii proposal story if P05 is the emotional peak.

Recommended division:
- P03: `旅の途中で、大きな節目へ` — short proposal teaser / milestone only;
- P05: full emotional proposal feature with stronger photo and longer native copy.

This prevents the same story being told twice.

## Date policy
Use dates only when confirmed and editorially useful.
The relationship should still read correctly with no exact meeting/dating/proposal date.

## Remove
- generic `運命の出会い` copy unless personally true and supplied;
- invented first-date details;
- six-node corporate timeline;
- repeated Hawaii photography that should belong to P05.

# P06 — OFF THE MAP / BEST SHOTS + REAL LIFE

## Page job
Answer: `旅行写真以外では、ふたりってどんな感じ？`

This should be the most human, casual page.

## Strong content
- candid couple photos;
- home/daily-life moments;
- funny faces / imperfect photos;
- activities;
- dogs `くっきー` / `めろん` if real photos are supplied;
- small food/cafe detail only if it says something about the two;
- real `ふたりあるある` snippets;
- tiny Q&A only if answers are genuinely good.

## Editorial opportunity
One or two photographs do NOT need to be conventionally beautiful. A funny/awkward/sincere image can make the whole booklet feel less staged and less AI-curated.

## Recommended text
- 4–7 short attached captions;
- one shared-life note;
- optional 1–3 tiny real facts.

## Remove
- recycled Hawaii/Okinawa/Korea heroes;
- generic lifestyle stock imagery;
- invented handwritten quotes;
- captions that merely describe what the image obviously shows.

# Cross-page duplication matrix

## Profile vs Life
- P02 = selected facts / who they are;
- P06 = lived evidence / what they feel like.

Do not repeat the same hobby/fact unless P06 photograph adds a genuinely new angle.

## Story vs Journey
- P03 = relationship sequence;
- P04–P05 = destination memories and proposal peak.

Proposal is teaser on P03, full feature on P05.

## Journey vs Life
- P04–P05 = iconic trips;
- P06 = non-destination/candid life.

Do not recycle hero travel images into P06.

## Other paper items vs V20
Other paper items own functional utility such as menu/drinks/seating/escort behavior.
V20 owns editorial discovery and personal storytelling.

# Final page-value ranking

If content must be cut, protect in this order:

1. real couple/travel photography with meaning;
2. relationship/proposal story;
3. concise profile/personality facts;
4. candid/dog/shared-life discoveries;
5. today's venue/travel-theme discoveries;
6. personal closing message;
7. decorative micro-copy.

Never cut real story to preserve generic magazine filler.

# V20 Page Differentiation Matrix

Status: `CANONICAL_ANTI-REPETITION_RULE / CURRENT_P07_DISCOVERY`

V20 shares one publication identity but must not repeat one AI/template composition eight times.

## Required differentiation axes

For every page/spread, record:
- composition verb;
- hero position;
- title alignment/silhouette;
- dominant color job;
- density peak location;
- calm-zone location;
- photo count/rhythm;
- primary frame/crop language;
- principal overlap gesture;
- edge/bleed gesture.

Adjacent spreads should normally differ on at least 4 axes.

## Density rhythm

- P01: VERY HIGH first-impression energy;
- P02: HIGH / lively people feature;
- P03: MEDIUM / quiet high-density story;
- P04–P05: VERY HIGH / maximum feature peak;
- P06: HIGH / candid playful collage;
- P07: HIGH / graphic information-discovery density;
- P08: LOW–MEDIUM / release.

Lower density does not mean generic whitespace or isolated cards.

## Primary title behavior must differ

- P01: giant `るるぶ WEDDING` masthead mass;
- P02: asymmetric person-feature title interacting with portraits;
- P03: quieter story title integrated with episode/photo structure;
- P04: journey/departure title support that builds toward P05;
- P05: oversized destination-feature title (`HAWAII` behavior) colliding with HERO;
- P06: playful snapshot/life title integrated with mosaic;
- P07: `TODAY'S TRAVEL GUIDE` + giant `11` / destination cluster;
- P08: restrained closing title/message.

Do not convert these into one reusable header component.

## Anti-clone failures

Fail/revise when:
- all page titles sit top-center;
- every page uses the same 3-photo card cluster;
- all photos use one rounded-rectangle radius;
- every page peaks at the center;
- the same red/yellow/blue balance repeats mechanically everywhere;
- every page uses the same sticker density;
- old SVG/icon-pack visual language creates publication identity;
- P04–P05 feels like two normal pages rather than a center spread;
- P06 and P07 both look like photo galleries;
- P07 looks like a timetable, map app, seating chart or itinerary UI;
- P08 is as dense as P01/P04–P05.

## Rule for shared parts

Reusable visual DNA creates identity, not layout identity.

A route/stamp/frame/arrow may recur, but its:
- scale;
- crop;
- rotation;
- foreground/background role;
- information job;
- surrounding photo/title relationship
must change with the page.

Old assets may remain for provenance, but `V20-LEGACY-QUARANTINE.md` prevents them from becoming visual authority.

## Spread relationships

- P02–P03: `WHO → STORY`.
- P04–P05: `PLACES WE REMEMBER → PEOPLE WE REMEMBER`.
- P06–P07: `REAL LIFE → THANK YOU`.
- P08: standalone back cover.

Adjacent pages should differ on at least four axes such as:
- title silhouette;
- hero geometry;
- photo count;
- density peak;
- calm-zone location;
- frame language;
- dominant color job;
- overlap gesture;
- edge behavior.

## Primary title behavior

- P01: giant `るるぶ WEDDING` masthead mass.
- P02: asymmetric people-feature title attached to portraits.
- P03: quieter story title integrated with narrative structure.
- P04: strong travel-memory feature title.
- P05: warm human-memory title, not destination-feature typography.
- P06: playful title integrated with candid mosaic.
- P07: restrained closing phrase/title.
- P08: little or no large editorial title; back-cover metadata is secondary.

## Anti-clone failures

Fail/revise when:
- every page uses the same 3-photo card layout;
- all photos share one rounded rectangle;
- all pages use the same red/yellow/blue ratio;
- every page has equal sticker density;
- old SVG/icon-pack grammar returns;
- P04 and P05 both look like destination pages;
- P05 still looks like the old Hawaii feature;
- P06 becomes another travel gallery;
- P07 looks like a guide/timetable/map page;
- P08 looks like another interior editorial page;
- all quiet space is filled merely because it exists.

## Shared-parts rule

Reusable DNA creates publication identity, not layout identity.

A frame/stamp/route/arrow may recur only if its scale, crop, role and surrounding composition change with the page.

`PAGE ROLE FIRST. SHARED STYLE SECOND.`

## Book-wide calibration

- `RURUBU FEEL = 100%`
- `EDITORIAL DENSITY = 75% OF PREVIOUS MAXIMUM-CLUTTER TESTS`
- `READABILITY = HIGH`

Keep full-strength magazine phenomena: giant display hierarchy, vivid travel-magazine color, unequal photos, title/photo/frame collision, tactile paper/ticket/stamp/ribbon language, authored asymmetry and active edges. ## Shared Q&A system

Exactly six slots:
- P02 Q1/Q2 — profile/personality

# P05 — FRIENDS MEMORIES ONLY

Density: `65–70/100`.

## Purpose

Show friendship memories clearly and warmly, split by the couple rather than by family/friends.

## First reads

1. page title `友達との思い出`
2. `SHOGO FRIENDS`
3. `SHIORI FRIENDS`
4. friend-photo clusters
5. short captions

## Fixed photo architecture

**No single giant HERO or page-wide anchor photo.**

Preferred final structure:
- SHOGO FRIENDS: about 3–4 photos
- SHIORI FRIENDS: about 3–4 photos
- total about 6–8 photos depending on real source quality

Use medium and small photos with visible but controlled inequality. One photo inside each cluster may be larger than its neighbors, but neither side gets a cover-like giant centerpiece.

Keep enough white/cream breathing room so the two clusters remain instantly understandable.

## Composition

Recommended:
- title across upper area, strong but not cover-scale
- two editorial territories, not rigid equal cards
- SHOGO cluster and SHIORI cluster separated by label/color/paper behavior
- photos overlap lightly within each cluster
- some cross-boundary visual DNA allowed, but do not mix which side belongs to whom
- faces remain recognizable at A5

## Generated units

- `P05_FRIENDS_TITLE_UNIT`
- `P05_SHOGO_FRIENDS_LABEL_UNIT`
- `P05_SHIORI_FRIENDS_LABEL_UNIT`
- 3–4 varied friend-photo frame/backing types
- short caption vessels
- restrained tape/camera/heart/travel-memory accents
- optional small shared `MEMORIES` chapter tab with P04

## Native content

- SHOGO/SHIORI friend labels
- captions
- names/relationships only when explicitly supplied

## Micro discoveries

Target roughly 3–6 total beyond the main labels. Do not carpet the page with stickers.

## Hard rejects

- family content
- `FAMILY` heading/cluster
- giant page-dominating photo
- Hawaii-only title/feature
- proposal/arrival article
- old P05 Hawaii/proposal assets as authority
- exhaustive friend directory
- inferred names/relationships/anecdotes
- exact 50/50 component grid that feels like a UI

Pass when the page immediately reads as `SHOGO FRIENDS + SHIORI FRIENDS`, remains lively at 65–70 density, and no one photo takes over the page.

# P04 — MEMORIES

Status: `CANONICAL_PAGE_MANUAL`
Role: `MEMORIES / TRAVEL FEATURE`
Editorial metaphor: `photo-led travel feature`

## PAGE JOB
Make guests feel the breadth of the couple's shared memories by browsing destinations and photos like a travel magazine feature.

## READING PATH
1. FIRST READ — `思い出の旅` / equivalent page title.
2. SECOND READ — one dominant memory/destination photo.
3. THIRD READ — 3–5 supporting photos with destination labels.
4. USEFUL INFO — short memory captions / place names.
5. MICRO DISCOVERY — camera, airplane, hearts, flowers, suitcase and small editorial notes.

## ZONE MAP
- top 0–20%: title + short intro copy.
- middle 18–78%: dominant photo + satellite photo constellation.
- lower 62–98%: larger secondary memory image + lower travel-object cluster.
- corners: selective floral/travel accents.

## PHOTO PLAN
- `P04_MEMORY_HERO`: dominant landscape/portrait depending on source, roughly 1.7–2.5× smallest support image area.
- `P04_MEMORY_02..06`: 3–5 independently replaceable support images.
- at least three visible treatments across page: hero/edgeless, polaroid, white-border tilt, square/portrait/organic crop.
- captions stay physically bound to their associated image.

## TITLE / TYPOGRAPHY
- strong Japanese page title with smaller English subtitle.
- intro copy remains native on stable cream/light field.
- destination labels stronger than captions but weaker than title.

## PARTS
- page title plate;
- hero frame or edgeless mask;
- mixed support frames ×3–5;
- camera object;
- airplane/route micro-accent optional;
- tropical clusters ×2–3;
- suitcase/travel still-life ×1 near lower corner;
- handwritten microcomments ×1–3.

## COLOR JOBS
- photography leads;
- support field: warm cream;
- primary accent: cyan/blue or pink depending on dominant photos;
- burst: yellow/coral;
- body: dark ink.

## OVERLAP
- 2–3 support photos may overlap lightly;
- one floral/photo-junction cluster bridges two frames;
- one handwritten note/arrow may point to a specific image;
- lower suitcase/camera may overlap support-photo edge.

## EDITABILITY
Native:
- destination/place names;
- captions;
- memory notes;
- ranking or `BEST` labels if factual.

Replaceable:
- all travel/memory photos.

Composed:
- title art;
- frames;
- floral/travel objects;
- non-factual microdecoration.

## FORBIDDEN
- equal 2×3 gallery grid;
- same frame on every photo;
- captions detached from photos;
- decoration louder than the hero photo;
- inventing destinations/stories;
- using whole-page generated art as sole master.

## QA
Pass when one photo clearly dominates, support photos visibly vary in shape/scale, captions bind correctly, the page reads as a travel feature at thumbnail scale, and all photographs remain independently replaceable.

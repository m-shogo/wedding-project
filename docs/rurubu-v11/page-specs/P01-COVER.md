# P01 — COVER

Status: `CANONICAL_PAGE_MANUAL`
Role: `COVER`
Editorial metaphor: `Japanese travel-magazine special issue cover`

## PAGE JOB
Within 3 seconds, guests should read: `WEDDING / Shogo & Shiori / travel-magazine special / joyful and worth opening`.

## READING PATH
1. FIRST READ — giant `WEDDING` / original Rurubu-Wedding identity.
2. SECOND READ — couple hero image.
3. THIRD READ — Shogo & Shiori + 2026.10.24.
4. USEFUL INFO — 3–5 feature coverlines.
5. MICRO DISCOVERY — small photos, date seal, plane/flower/ticket details.

## ZONE MAP
- top 0–31%: identity + hero title + upper corner decoration;
- center 25–78%: couple hero + venue/scenery support;
- left 32–72%: stacked feature coverlines;
- lower 72–100%: support-photo collage + welcome/date/travel objects;
- edge/bleed: tropical clusters at 2–3 corners, not all four symmetrically.

## PHOTO PLAN
- `P01_HERO_COUPLE`: dominant, ~45–60% visual area, replaceable, may overlap title zone.
- `P01_SUPPORT_VENUE`: medium, behind/adjacent to couple, replaceable.
- `P01_SUPPORT_MEMORY_01..03`: 2–3 small polaroid/white-border travel photos, visibly different size.
- optional `P01_OBJECT_CUTOUT`: non-person travel object only.

At least three photo treatments should be visible across hero/supports.

## TITLE PLAN
- hero title must remain legible at thumbnail scale;
- pink/white/yellow or equivalent high-energy silhouette is appropriate;
- title may be composed art;
- names/date remain native unless separately verified in approved art;
- a small original identity mark may sit upper-left but must not literally reproduce a commercial `るるぶ` logo.

## PARTS INVENTORY
- `TITLE_HERO_WEDDING`;
- `IDENTITY_MARK_ORIGINAL`;
- `NAME_RIBBON`;
- `BADGE_DATE_OR_COUNTDOWN`;
- `COVERLINE_LABEL` ×3–5;
- `CLUSTER_TROPICAL_CORNER` ×2–3;
- `FRAME_POLAROID` ×2–3;
- `ICON_AIRPLANE` optional;
- `TRAVEL_STILL_LIFE` optional;
- `HANDWRITTEN_MICROCOMMENT` ×1–2.

## COLOR JOBS
- dominant: cyan/sky or vivid travel field;
- support: hot pink;
- burst: sunny yellow;
- body/facts: dark ink on stable light support.

## OVERLAP / Z-ORDER
background/scenery
→ hero/support photo sources
→ photo frames
→ couple hero/cutout if separate
→ hero title
→ native facts/coverlines
→ foreground flowers/badges/travel microdecor.

Intentional overlaps:
- couple into lower title territory;
- flower cluster across one support-photo junction;
- one badge/ribbon across a photo edge;
- lower photos overlap each other by a small amount.

## EDGE TENSION
Allow flowers/foliage and one lower support-photo/travel-object cluster to approach/crop into trim/bleed. Keep faces/date/names safe.

## EDITABILITY CONTRACT
Native editable:
- names;
- date;
- venue;
- coverline copy.

Replaceable:
- all photographs.

Composed display art:
- hero title;
- decorative logo/identity treatment;
- badges/stamps/florals/travel objects.

## FORBIDDEN
- centered web-hero layout;
- three equal bottom cards;
- all coverlines as identical pills;
- giant empty margins;
- whole-page raster production master;
- title/photo/date all competing at same weight.

## QA GATE
Pass only when:
- genre is recognizable at thumbnail scale;
- title is FIRST READ and couple is SECOND READ;
- support photos differ clearly in size/aspect;
- at least two edge-tension zones exist;
- factual text remains editable;
- replacing the hero photo does not require rebuilding title/coverlines.

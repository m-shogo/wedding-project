# P08 — MESSAGE / BACK

Status: `CANONICAL_PAGE_MANUAL`
Role: `MESSAGE / PHOTO CTA / BACK COVER`
Editorial metaphor: `postcard closing page`

## PAGE JOB
Thank guests, close the travel story warmly, and optionally invite photo sharing/participation without turning the final page into a leftovers dump.

## READING PATH
1. FIRST READ — closing message / `ゲストへのメッセージ` title.
2. SECOND READ — calm message field.
3. THIRD READ — photo-sharing/photo-contest CTA if confirmed.
4. USEFUL INFO — hashtag/date/name/venue or other confirmed closing utility.
5. MICRO DISCOVERY — postmark, passport, ticket, camera, suitcase and floral edge cluster.

## ZONE MAP
- top 0–18%: closing title.
- upper/middle 15–66%: large calm message/postcard field.
- lower 62–88%: photo CTA / small closing utility.
- lower/perimeter 80–100%: travel-object cluster + floral accents.
- optional postmark/stamp near top-right/upper-middle edge of message field.

## MESSAGE PLAN
- central message copy must be native and comfortable to edit.
- a lined/postcard-like background is allowed as composed art beneath native text.
- keep enough calm space that the page visibly reduces energy from P07.

## IMAGE PLAN
- `P08_CLOSING_PHOTO`: optional replaceable small/medium photo.
- `P08_PHOTO_CTA_VISUAL`: camera/photo motif; can be composed art if illustrative.
- no requirement for a large hero photo if the message field is the hero.

## PARTS
- closing title plate or expressive handwritten title;
- postcard/lined field;
- postmark/date stamp;
- camera object;
- passport/ticket/suitcase travel still-life;
- photo-contest/CTA label if confirmed;
- tropical corner clusters ×1–2;
- optional small support frame.

## COLOR JOBS
- dominant: warm cream/paper.
- support: pink/coral.
- travel accent: blue/cyan.
- burst: yellow sparingly.
- message text: dark ink.

## OVERLAP
- postmark may cross the edge of the message field;
- lower travel objects may overlap each other into one cluster;
- florals may frame edges while staying out of central copy;
- CTA label may overlap camera/photo edge.

## EDGE TENSION
Use perimeter decoration more than central decoration. One or two corner clusters may crop toward bleed. Keep guest message, hashtag and important utility safely inside trim.

## EDITABILITY
Native:
- closing message;
- names/date;
- hashtag;
- CTA text;
- any guest instructions.

Replaceable:
- optional closing/photo-sharing photos.

Composed:
- lined/postcard field;
- stamps/postmarks;
- travel-object cluster;
- title art;
- flowers/frames.

## FORBIDDEN
- junk drawer for unused parts;
- same density as P01/P07;
- giant decorative title that crowds message copy;
- invented hashtag/contest instructions;
- rasterized guest-facing message;
- decoration randomly sprinkled through the message field.

## QA
Pass when the book clearly lowers energy after P07, the message is comfortably readable, lower/perimeter decoration preserves publication identity, all guest instructions remain editable, and the page reads as an intentional closing/back-cover composition.

# P07 — Q&A

Status: `CANONICAL_PAGE_MANUAL`
Role: `Q&A / INTERVIEW`
Editorial metaphor: `playful magazine interview`

## PAGE JOB
Reveal personality and relationship details through 4–6 easy-to-scan questions without turning the page into a grid of UI cards.

## READING PATH
1. FIRST READ — `6つの質問 Q&A` or confirmed equivalent.
2. SECOND READ — numbered Q anchors.
3. THIRD READ — answers.
4. USEFUL INFO — contrast/comparison between the couple when relevant.
5. MICRO DISCOVERY — small photos, objects, dog/travel/food motifs, arrows and flowers.

## ZONE MAP
- top 0–17%: page title.
- upper/middle 16–48%: Q1/Q2.
- middle 43–72%: Q3/Q4.
- lower 68–97%: Q5/Q6.
- support visuals can interrupt the repeated rhythm at right/lower edge.

The three bands are semantic, not an exact 3×2 grid. Vary x position, width, label shape and supporting motifs.

## QUESTION SYSTEM
Each question contains:
- `Q01..Q06` native or verified label;
- question text native;
- answer text native;
- optional small associated motif.

Question wrappers may vary among:
- burst;
- brush/tape label;
- small flag;
- number only;
- speech-bubble edge;
- photo-adjacent annotation.

Answer areas should remain visually calm enough for comfortable reading.

## IMAGE PLAN
- `P07_QA_SUPPORT_01..03`: 1–3 independently replaceable photos if useful.
- small non-photo motifs may be generated/composed.
- one stronger lower-right or side character/object cluster may serve as an intentional interruption.

## PARTS
- Q&A title plate;
- Q-number labels ×4–6;
- calm answer fields;
- speech-bubble/label variants;
- small food/travel/camera/home/etc. motifs only when related to question content;
- tropical clusters ×1–2;
- one character/object anchor optional.

## COLOR JOBS
- dominant: warm cream/light field;
- Q anchors: pink + cyan/blue family;
- burst/support: yellow/coral;
- answers: dark ink.

Keep color families coherent; do not make every question an unrelated rainbow.

## OVERLAP
- Q label may cross the answer-field edge;
- small motif may partly cross in/out of a question area;
- one support photo/object may interrupt the repeated two-column rhythm;
- florals can anchor lower/right edge.

## EDITABILITY
Native:
- all questions;
- all answers;
- Q numbers when practical;
- factual comparison labels.

Replaceable:
- all photographic supports.

Composed:
- title;
- Q-label shapes;
- frames;
- decorative motifs.

## FORBIDDEN
- six identical rounded cards;
- rasterized answers;
- one saturated color per question with no system;
- equal spacing/widths making a software settings grid;
- invented Q&A content;
- decoration covering answer reading zones.

## QA
Pass when all Q anchors can be found in 2–3 seconds, repeated semantics are clear without cloned card grammar, answer copy is comfortable at A5 actual size, and at least one intentional interruption gives the page editorial personality.

# P03 — PROFILE

Status: `CANONICAL_PAGE_MANUAL`
Role: `PROFILE + MINI STORY`
Editorial metaphor: `two travel protagonists introduced by a magazine editor`

## PAGE JOB
Let guests understand Shogo and Shiori quickly, then discover small personality details and a compact relationship-story strip.

## READING PATH
1. FIRST READ — `ふたりのプロフィール` title.
2. SECOND READ — Shogo/Shiori portrait pair.
3. THIRD READ — names + core profile facts.
4. USEFUL INFO — short mini-story/timeline and favorite things.
5. MICRO DISCOVERY — labels, small icons, arrows and one playful feature.

## ZONE MAP
- top 0–18%: title + subtitle.
- upper-middle 15–55%: portrait pair.
- center-lower 50–76%: compact profile facts.
- right or side 28–76%: mini-story strip/timeline if it does not force symmetry.
- lower 75–98%: `好きなもの Best 5` or equivalent small discovery feature.

## PHOTO PLAN
- `P03_PROFILE_SHOGO`: replaceable portrait, polaroid/photo-print treatment.
- `P03_PROFILE_SHIORI`: replaceable portrait, related but not cloned treatment.
- `P03_FAVORITE_01..05`: optional small images/illustrations for favorites only when content is confirmed.

The two portraits have equal editorial respect but do not need equal size/angle.

## TITLE / TYPOGRAPHY
- bold rounded Japanese title with pink emphasis is appropriate;
- small English subtitle may support but not dominate;
- names can use a more expressive script/display treatment;
- facts remain dark native text on cream/light field.

## PARTS
- page title plate;
- portrait frames ×2;
- name labels ×2;
- compact fact-label rows;
- mini-story date markers ×3–5 maximum;
- favorite/BEST feature label;
- tropical/photo-junction cluster ×1–2;
- small personal/travel icons only when meaningful.

## COLOR JOBS
- dominant: warm cream/paper;
- support: pink + cyan;
- burst: yellow/coral;
- body: dark ink.

## OVERLAP
- names may cross lower portrait-frame edges;
- one flower/leaf cluster may bridge portrait and title/fact zone;
- bottom mini-feature may slightly overlap the factual-zone boundary;
- mini-story strip must not visually become a corporate stepper.

## EDITABILITY
Native:
- names when practical;
- birthday/home/job/hobbies/favorites;
- relationship dates/stories;
- labels.

Replaceable:
- portraits and favorite-item photos.

Composed:
- title art;
- portrait frames;
- decorative labels/icons.

## FORBIDDEN
- employee-profile cards;
- perfect mirrored 50/50 layout;
- blue/pink as the only person identifier;
- rasterized factual profile text;
- mini timeline dominating the portraits;
- inventing personal facts.

## QA
Pass when faces/names are immediately identifiable, both people feel equally important without cloned cards, facts remain editable, and the lower/side mini-feature adds magazine browse value without overpowering the profile.

# NRSL — Family-scale template repetition can survive item-level PASS

Date: 2026-08-21
Source scope: non-Rurubu
State: `VERIFIED_CROSS_ITEM`
Failure fingerprint: `FAMILY_SCALE_TEMPLATE_REPETITION`

## Visible problem

Individually healthy items can pass thumbnail/read/detail, long-copy and structure QA yet still become visibly AI/system-template-like when viewed as a suite. In ADD-16 and ADD-17, repeated deep navy + warm cream + coral/mint/yellow and repeated oversized circles/capsule sweeps made unrelated physical artifacts look as though one component theme had been reskinned.

## Evidence / root cause

ADD-16 independently reopened after a family-scale audit and moved from the repeated sunrise/capsule vocabulary to a plum/apricot/sky physical `RETURN LETTER` object. That change passed three-scale, long-copy, writing-surface and structure QA and scored 92/100.

ADD-17 then reproduced the same benefit independently. Its structurally healthy WINDOW SEAT direction repeated the same suite vocabulary. Three new blank-frame directions were created from non-visual requirements only. `FIELD NOTE` replaced airline/window/circle language with a cobalt-bound tactile notebook, terracotta paper header, moss prompt strip and warm physical drawing/writing sheets. The result passed three-scale and realistic stress QA, fixed a real long-copy writing-lane collision, preserved all semantic editability and scored 92/100.

Root-cause model: item-level art direction can be locally coherent while the suite accumulates a repeated palette + shape grammar because each item optimizes against itself rather than against a family-diversity view.

## Bounded tests

- ADD-16: new clean-room physical-letter grammar; old design retained and only compared after new candidate matured.
- ADD-17: three new clean-room physical-activity-object directions; old WINDOW SEAT retained and only used for final mature comparison.

No generated people or generic travel imagery were introduced. The defect was not missing imagery.

## Result

`VERIFIED_CROSS_ITEM`.

Family-scale QA is now evidence-backed as a separate visual gate after individual item maturity. It should look for repeated palette dominance, repeated large-shape vocabulary, repeated hero proportions, repeated panel systems and repeated decorative motion across functionally unrelated artifacts.

This is not a rule that every item must use different colors. Family resemblance remains desirable. The test is whether unrelated objects still feel separately art-directed rather than template variants.

## Regression risk

Over-correcting diversity can destroy suite coherence. The receiving item must retain at least one shared conceptual thread—travel, hospitality, celebration, Japanese editorial voice, or material behavior—without copying exact palette/geometry.

## Three-scale evidence

ADD-16 and ADD-17 both passed whole-item, reading and native/actual-size review after the family-diversity rebuilds. ADD-17 realistic stress additionally exposed a headline/prompt → writing-surface collision and was corrected by moving the physical writing lane rather than shrinking type.

## Item-specific — do not transfer

Do not transfer ADD-16 plum/apricot letter geometry or ADD-17 cobalt/terracotta field-notebook layout, colors, dimensions or copy into other items.

## Cross-item applicability

Receiving items that are already individually `SELLABLE_VISUAL_QA_PASS` may still be audited at family thumbnail scale. If one repeats both palette and dominant shape grammar already used by unrelated items, reopen only the family-fit/art-direction ceiling, preserve prior structural evidence, and create a materially different blank-frame direction from the receiving item's own physical role.

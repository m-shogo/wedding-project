## NRSL — DESTINATION FAMILY DIVERSITY REQUIRES SKELETON AND ENERGY VARIATION

State: `TESTED_LOCAL → VERIFIED_LOCAL`
Source: ADD-02 11卓の国別テーブルサイン

### Visible problem

HAWAII / JAPAN / ITALY V3 proved that different material and atmosphere vocabularies can improve destination specificity, but a same-scale family board still showed layout convergence: multiple anchors reused a strong upper destination title plus edge-field/pale-main-field mass distribution. The V3 row was also calmer than current production, reducing wedding/pop excitement.

### Root-cause hypothesis

A family can remain templated even when colors, textures and motifs differ. The underlying composition skeleton—where the main mass sits, where the title begins, how information lanes divide, and where fixed-art weight accumulates—can repeat strongly enough to preserve an AI/system-generated feel. Destination specificity is also not a promotion signal if emotional energy declines.

### Bounded method switch

A fourth destination was authored from a blank frame with both a new place/material role and a new mass distribution:

- SPAIN `153:2 / CERAMIC COURTYARD RHYTHM`
- realistic long-copy stress `153:18`

The treatment used a full-width celebratory top field, open central native-copy lane, offset cobalt/saffron light break, and irregular lower courtyard/tile-color rhythm. It did not reuse the HAWAII coastal side field, JAPAN paper-edge skeleton, ITALY stone-spine/portico skeleton, or current production's large circle/capsule composition.

### Result

The Spain experiment verified the method switch locally:

- the V3 family became visibly less dependent on one skeleton;
- Spain recovered stronger celebration energy while remaining cleaner and more editorial than its current abstract-sweep/capsule treatment;
- HAWAII/JAPAN/ITALY are still not uniformly stronger than current production, so no family rollout or production promotion occurred.

### Transferable principle

For a multi-item family that still looks templated after palette/material diversification, audit four separate layers:

1. semantic/content role;
2. material/atmosphere role;
3. layout skeleton / mass distribution;
4. emotional energy at family thumbnail scale.

If layer 3 repeats or layer 4 regresses, changing only texture/color is insufficient. Method-switch the composition skeleton before rollout.

### Regression risk

Maximum skeleton diversity can fragment a family into unrelated brands. Preserve a coherent typographic/print-quality world; change skeleton only when the item/place role justifies it.

### What must remain item-specific

Do not transfer Spain's red top field, courtyard-color blocks, cobalt/saffron geometry, exact headline placement, palette or table-sign dimensions.

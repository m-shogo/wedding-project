## Observed problem

V8 F initially contained two related professional-authenticity failures:

1. several visible strings read like design notes or schema labels rather than publication copy (`replaceable role`, `hero`, `route diagram`, instructions about density/structure);
2. Cafe/Table carried a large neutral block whose only real purpose was to say “a second photo will go here later.”

Neither failure caused a structural collision, but both were strong AI/template signals at reading scale.

## New professional hypothesis

Photobook/editorial research reinforced two ideas:

- sequence and page-turn rhythm are publication-level decisions; each spread does not need identical image density;
- a designer contributes to picture edit, sequence, reproduction and physical-book pacing rather than mechanically filling predetermined boxes.

Therefore a quiet facing page can be more professional than a fake photo module when the quiet page has a clear reading/pacing role. Separately, production vocabulary should remain in layer names/evidence, not reader-facing copy.

## Failure fingerprint

`F-RSL-V8-INTERNAL-PROCESS-COPY-AND-FAKE-IMAGE-SLOT`

Trigger:

- visible copy describes how the design should work instead of speaking to the reader; or
- a large visual block has no semantic content beyond reserving space for a hypothetical future image.

Preferred correction order:

1. move process information to layer naming/evidence;
2. rewrite visible copy as reader-facing editorial language;
3. ask whether the sequence truly needs another image at that page turn;
4. if no legitimate asset exists, test a purposeful text-led counter-spread;
5. if the publication purpose genuinely requires imagery, create a role-specific asset instead of borrowing a semantically wrong image.

## Transferable candidate principle

A publication family does **not** need equal image density on every spread to feel coherent. Theme + variation can be more authentic than repeated modules when the variation serves reading order, page-turn rhythm or emotional pacing.

At the same time, “quiet” must not become empty luxury styling. Every quiet region needs a sequencing, reading or physical-book purpose.

## Regression risk

Do not turn this into:

- “remove images for professionalism”;
- “more whitespace is better”;
- “all internal terminology is forbidden from Figma.”

Internal terminology is useful in semantic layer names, QA and provenance evidence. The restriction is specifically on **reader-facing publication copy**.

Likewise, image-led pages remain essential when imagery carries destination, person, food or atmosphere. The lesson is to reject fake slots, not photography.

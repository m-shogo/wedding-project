## Observation

V8 AW7 `2439:2` had already removed unsupported section/chapter numbering and truth-gated unresolved personal answers, but its two person labels still read `SHOGO / 01` and `SHI-CHAN / 02`.

The names performed the identity job. The `01 / 02` suffixes did not currently map to a verified sequence, navigation/finding system, quantity, time, issue, or cross-reference. At reading scale they therefore acted mainly as publication-looking furniture.

## New/deeper professional input

This run deliberately rotated away from recent section-marker and photo-source-truth research toward current interview/editorial practice. Society of Publication Designers examples and Pentagram editorial work reinforce that interview pages can vary their structure according to the article/content role; systematic numbering is not itself the editorial idea.

Only the decision principle was transferred. No external layout, typography, palette or composition was copied.

## Verified principle

Do not remove numbers categorically. Require a defensible reader-facing referent. In a profile/interview, a person label may simply identify the person; adding an ordinal for visual systemization is not automatically stronger design.

A real sequence, index, finding aid, issue/section reference, quantity or other authoritative job can still justify numbering.

## What must not transfer

Do not transfer V8's cream/navy system, exact person-name position, type size, left-page geometry, Q&A layout, or book-like restraint. Only the semantic-numbering test and rollback/three-scale QA method are transferable.

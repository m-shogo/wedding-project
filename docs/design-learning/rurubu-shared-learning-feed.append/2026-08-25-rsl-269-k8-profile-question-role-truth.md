## Visible problem

K7 correctly used two small verified real-couple Hawaii snapshots, but the Q&A page still depended on two large generic structural dummy photographs. The page therefore mixed truthful couple evidence with unrelated editorial art, while the same generic dummy hashes were repeated across multiple V7 roles.

## Root-cause hypothesis

Once verified human evidence is available, generic imagery should not continue occupying dominant person-profile/Q&A roles merely to preserve visual density. In a profile/interview, photography must either provide truthful subject evidence or perform another clearly defensible editorial function. Otherwise it increases template repetition and can imply false subject/context authority.

## Bounded experiment

K8 `2546:2`:

- removed the two generic Q&A dummies;
- retained verified Hawaii `004.jpg` and `036.jpg` only as shared-couple evidence, never as groom-only/bride-only or answer-specific proof;
- organized unanswered Q1–Q6 into `ふたりのこと` and `これからのこと` semantic groups;
- preserved unanswered values, factual truth boundaries, typography system and V7 palette;
- no cards, pills, fake answers, gradients or decorative filler added.

## Professional learning

This strengthens RSL-269 beyond the earlier micro-snapshot test. The transferable principle is not “put couple photos on a profile” and not “remove all dummy photos.” It is:

> In a real-person profile/interview, after verified subject imagery exists, re-audit remaining dominant placeholder imagery. Keep a placeholder only if it performs a defensible non-identity editorial job. Otherwise prefer verified subject evidence or a text-led structure rather than density for density's sake.

## Regression risk

- Do not make a shared-couple photo imply a one-person identity.
- Do not enlarge low-resolution derivatives into final-print authority.
- Do not remove photography where it is genuinely needed for publication rhythm; compare the resulting spread at whole-item scale.
- Do not transfer V7's coral/cream/navy composition, exact image positions or question grouping to other items.

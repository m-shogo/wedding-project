## Visible problem

## New/deeper professional observation

ICP picture-story guidance distinguishes contextual, action, sequence and emotional-detail photographs as different narrative jobs. ICP faculty guidance also emphasizes that individual good images do not automatically make a coherent story; editing and sequencing create the narrative. Magnum contact sheets similarly expose selection as an editorial act across adjacent frames rather than isolated image quality.

## Root-cause hypothesis

`Verified person/place` and `verified dated event` are different levels of photographic authority. A chronology or timeline increases the risk that any nearby image will be interpreted as evidence for the adjacent milestone, even when the image is only subject-level or illustrative.

## Transferable principle

When photographs sit beside dates, milestones or timeline events, verify the **event-level** relationship, not merely that the person/place in the image is real. If event authority is unavailable, separate subject-level images from the event rail, use explicit non-event-specific roles, or let the chronology remain type-led.

## What must NOT transfer

Do not transfer V7 colors, dimensions, couple photography, chronology geometry, type scale, fixed display treatment or exact image placement.

## Receiving-item hypothesis

A materially different artifact with event/date/milestone presentation can independently test whether a nearby real-but-non-event-specific image falsely upgrades its documentary meaning. Preserve legitimate photos that truly bind to dated events; this is not a blanket photo-removal rule.

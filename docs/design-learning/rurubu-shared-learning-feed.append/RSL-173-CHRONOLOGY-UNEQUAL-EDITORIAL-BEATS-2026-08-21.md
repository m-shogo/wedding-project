# RSL-173 — Redundant chronology rails can be replaced by unequal editorial beats

Source scope/item: Rurubu WEDDING / V6 Story + Chronology
Date: 2026-08-21
State: `VERIFIED_LOCAL → CROSS_ITEM_CANDIDATE`

## Visible problem

The Story / Chronology spread had a strong photo-led left page, but the Chronology right page still depended on a vertical rail, node dots, and repeated event rows. At whole-item scale that rail/list grammar weakened the spread and made unequal life moments feel visually uniform.

## Root-cause hypothesis

The ordering mechanism had become redundant. Event numbers already communicated sequence, while the rail forced the page to organize around a list rather than around editorial importance. Existing event photography was adequate; the missing capability was stronger macro-hierarchy, not another generated asset.

## Principle / capability tested

When ordered information already has a reliable sequence cue such as numbers or dates, test whether a visible rail/list scaffold is still necessary. A rollback-safe alternative can preserve order while grouping content into deliberately unequal editorial beats and promoting a semantically meaningful photograph into a structural chapter.

The neutral cross-scope lesson consumed was method-only: a retained line/field should prove a binding function at whole-item scale. No literal layout, asset, brand treatment, dimensions, or opposite-item current-state conclusion was transferred.

## Expected improvement

A clearer `opening → destination → terminal` eye path, stronger photo/type hierarchy, less timeline-UI character, and better continuity between the two pages of the spread without sacrificing chronological comprehension.

## Regression risk

- rail removal can obscure sequence when numbers/dates are weak;
- unequal grouping can accidentally imply false importance;
- side notes can become too narrow for Japanese text;
- text-over-photo can fail contrast;
- large terminal numerals can collide with dates/titles;
- a retained separator can become decorative noise if its binding function is not evident.

## Strengthened principle

Source-truth risk is broader than literal caption placement. A nearby place-specific headline, deck, close, label or other grounded copy can also authenticate an unverified photograph by composition.

When that happens, do not apply a universal layout fix. Choose among:

1. verify or replace the photograph with legitimate role-specific source evidence;
2. separate the grounded copy from image ownership;
3. if the line is editorial rather than factual, rewrite only that line to the semantic role actually visible without inventing location evidence;
4. reject simple image subtraction when whole-item pacing or reader value materially regresses.

The V8 Outer AV4A/AV4B typography-only studies were rejected because removing the unverified photo without a legitimate replacement reduced travel desire and created an empty-field cover. This confirms that source truth and publication impact must both pass; truth-safe subtraction is not automatically promotion-worthy.

## What must not transfer

Do not transfer `夜の食卓` wording, V7/V8 layout, palette, image geometry, typography scale, or the decision to remove place names from legitimate verified photography. Exact place language is desirable when the image/source authority is real.

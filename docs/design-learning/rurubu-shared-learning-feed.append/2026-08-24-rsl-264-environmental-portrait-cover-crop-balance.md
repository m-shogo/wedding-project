## Visible problem

The legitimate real Hawaii `007.jpg` had already improved truth and travel desire, but the preferred C10 screen crop was optimized strongly toward enlarging the couple. Once a more environmental comparison was tested, it became clear that a cover environmental portrait has two simultaneous jobs: identify the wedding subjects and preserve enough place context to make the destination desirable.

## Root-cause hypothesis

A crop can be technically valid and emotionally stronger in one dimension while weakening the second editorial job. `larger people` is not automatically the best wedding-travel cover, and `more scenery` is not automatically the best travel cover when the publication is specifically about the couple.

## Bounded experiment

Same photo, same C8-derived page system, no typography/copy/color/layout changes:

- C10 `2483:2`: tight people-first crop, transform `0.72 / 0.20` → subject strong, environment reduced.
- C11 `2486:2`: environment-heavy crop, transform `0.84 / 0.08–0.10` → destination strong, couple too small.
- C12 `2486:33`: intermediate people+place crop, transform `0.78 / 0.14` → preferred screen balance.

## What changed in the learning

Planning-source truth is not a visual style. The same identified source can improve two materially different Rurubu systems while each preserves its publication personality:

- V7: dense travel-information utility
- V8: restrained book transcript / temporal cadence

Strengthened transferable principle:

> When a legitimate planning source exists, do not retain generic model-course language merely because it is easy to lay out. Represent the source state honestly, let actual content determine sequence/hierarchy, preserve update-sensitive caveats, and keep unverified imagery from implying documentary proof.

The receiving system must still decide its own visual grammar from the content role.

## Professional research contribution

Fresh observation: MoMA's record of Ed Ruscha's *Every Building on the Sunset Strip* and Getty's production archive show a book whose format/sequence follows a systematic record rather than a decorative modular scheme. This changed the V8 experiment from `copy C6E modules` to `translate the plan as a book transcript`.

Research observation is not promoted as a project rule and no Ruscha styling is copied.

## Visible problem

V7 Profile K4 was safely free of invented people, but all image roles were object/food/street structural dummies. The page therefore described the real couple while withholding any actual human evidence, even though verified real-couple Hawaii photography already existed.

## Root-cause hypothesis

Identity-safety can be over-applied. Avoiding false groom/bride portrait mapping does not require a Profile to remain personless when real couple photography is available. The safer alternative is to keep individual identity in native text while using explicitly couple-owned photography only in roles that do not imply a one-person mapping.

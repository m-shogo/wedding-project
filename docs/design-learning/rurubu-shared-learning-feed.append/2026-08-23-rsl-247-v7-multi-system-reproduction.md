This run deliberately rotated research toward chronology and publication sequence rather than repeating recent photo, grid, cover or food references.

Observed professional viewpoints:

- a complex visual chronology may use a deliberately strict system to keep dates, images and text legible;
- a book is experienced as an ordered sequence of spaces and moments, not merely a pile of independent pages;

## Root-cause hypothesis

The defect is not the typographic styling of the folio. It is the use of a production/navigation datum before the production sequence exists.

A visually convincing magazine can therefore become **less** professionally credible when decorative authenticity simulates production certainty that has not been established.

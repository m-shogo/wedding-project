# RSL-186 — Book-design restraint can remove AI/template signals, but must preserve travel desire

Date: 2026-08-21
Source: Rurubu WEDDING V8 clean-room six-role study
State: `VERIFIED_LOCAL → CROSS_ITEM_CANDIDATE`

## Observed problem

V7 proved that a high-energy clean-room system can still look synthetic when its generated visual craft is weak. The opposite correction—making everything quiet, spacious and typographic—risks replacing AI-template energy with empty art-book restraint.

## New professional hypothesis

Use book-design pacing as a real sequence tool:

- some spreads may be quiet;
- serif-led reading and folios/indexes can create publication identity;
- not every spread needs a hero module;
- density can change page by page while the system stays coherent.

But a travel publication still needs desire, place and sensory evidence. Restraint is not an excuse to remove destination specificity.

## Bounded test

V8 was created from blank frames with no V7 composition/image reuse across six roles:

- Outer `2158:62`
- Profile/Q&A `2158:86`
- Story/Chronology `2159:54`
- Memory/Guide `2159:90`
- Cafe/Food `2159:119`
- 1DAY `2159:144`

All six final roots have `0` visible native-text intersections and `0` 18 px text safe-area risks.

The set uses a muted serif/sans editorial language, folios/indexing, long-form text rhythm, and non-card layouts. New generated rasters are abstract/semantic book-image roles rather than V7's high-energy faux photography.

## Three-scale evidence

- all six V8 spreads inspected at 500 px family scale;
- Story/Chronology inspected at 1400 px reading scale;
- actual-size representative pages inspected for Outer, Q&A, Story, Memory, Cafe and 1DAY.

At actual size, typography and pacing feel materially more intentional and less template/AI-like than V7. However several roles—especially Story, Memory and 1DAY—lack enough destination-specific evidence to create travel desire.

## Fresh comparison against V6/V7

V6 preferred six spreads were re-read at 500 px after V8 completion.

Observed locally:

- V6 still wins destination desire and photographic atmosphere;
- V8 wins book pacing, typography and restraint;
- V7 currently loses overall because its image craft is weakest.

No global winner was promoted.

## Transferable candidate principle

When a publication looks AI/template-like, introducing quieter book-design pacing can improve authenticity **only if** the sequence still contains enough subject-specific visual evidence to sustain the publication's purpose.

Operational test:

1. remove repeated module grammar;
2. establish sequence/pacing, typographic voice, folios and indexing;
3. inspect the whole publication, not one spread;
4. then ask whether the subject still creates desire/interest at thumbnail and reading scale;
5. if it feels tasteful but generic, add stronger subject-specific photography/illustration rather than more decoration.

## Regression risk

Do not promote “more whitespace” or “serif = professional” as a rule. V8 demonstrates that restraint can itself become generic or empty. The useful principle is publication-level pacing plus subject specificity.

## Transfer boundary

Potentially transferable:

- sequence/pacing test;
- anti-template diagnostic;
- requirement to retain subject-specific desire after subtraction;
- whole-publication rather than single-page evaluation.

Rurubu-specific and non-transferable:

- V8 palette/type pairing;
- exact folio/index treatment;
- Hawaii/travel wording;
- Figma node IDs and generated asset hashes;
- V6/V7/V8 winner status.

# RSL-056 — Bounded texture + event rhythm, not texture alone

Date: 2026-08-17
Source scope: Rurubu WEDDING V6
State: `OBSERVED → ROOT_CAUSE_HYPOTHESIS → TESTED_LOCAL → VERIFIED_LOCAL → CROSS_ITEM_CANDIDATE`

## Source problem
Story/chronology BY already had strong photography, native event copy and major/minor milestone hierarchy, but the chronology middle field still read as disconnected cream-space annotations. `02` and `04` were structurally valid yet visually isolated between the photo-led `01 / 03 / 05` beats.

## Root-cause hypothesis
When an editorial sequence is semantically correct but its support beats do not visually belong to the same journey, simply adding more cards or making every milestone photographic risks recreating a timeline UI. A bounded, subordinate composed texture can provide continuity **only if the event rhythm is tightened at the same time**.

Texture alone is not the treatment. The treatment is:

`existing semantic hierarchy + tighter major/minor vertical rhythm + one low-opacity bounded continuity field`.

## Photo-diversity side finding
A same-scope audit confirmed repeated preferred photo hashes. Low-reuse Rurubu V5 alternatives were inspected before substitution:
- two contained recognizable people and were rejected because they could imply the real bride/groom;
- one non-person beach photo was only `270×192` and was rejected for hero use.

Do not lower provenance/identity/resolution quality merely to reduce repetition. Preserve the replaceable photo contract and wait for a legitimate final-photo replacement.

## Finding
A composed texture may help a magazine sequence only when it is:
- already verified or independently QA'd;
- bounded to the weak continuity field;
- subordinate in opacity/contrast;
- paired with a real hierarchy/rhythm correction;
- kept separate from variable native text and replaceable photography.

If adding the texture alone is the only change, treat that as insufficient evidence of improvement.

## What must remain Rurubu-specific
Do not transfer the exact texture, opacity, colors, chronology geometry, Japanese copy, photo subjects, crop, coordinates or Rurubu editorial grammar.

## Cross-item applicability hypothesis
On a materially different wedding item, if semantic content is correct but repeated support beats feel visually disconnected, test a rollback-safe **bounded continuity field plus rhythm adjustment** before adding a new card system. The continuity field must not bake variable copy or replaceable imagery into one raster.

This is not `VERIFIED_CROSS_ITEM` and not a project-wide visual rule.

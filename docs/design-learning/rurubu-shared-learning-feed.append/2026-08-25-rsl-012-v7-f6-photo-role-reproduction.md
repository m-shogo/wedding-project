## Principle strengthened

Source validity does not determine role assignment automatically. Review an image set as a system:

- semantic job;
- crop/orientation compatibility;
- subject recognition at the assigned scale;
- repetition and sequence;
- effective source quality at the assigned size.

If two valid sources are simply assigned to the wrong dominant/support roles, generating another asset or adding decoration is not the first correction.

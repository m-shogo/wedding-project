## External design learning used in this reset

- Pentagram / United Airlines: one genuine identity gesture can become bigger, bolder and more expressive, including sweeping out of frame; this is stronger than clusters of travel clichés.
- Hawaiian Airlines / Lippincott: bright emotive color, organic natural forms, typography, hospitality and cultural study can create premium leisure warmth without relying on literal travel icons.
- Hawaii Tourism Authority guidance: Hawaii must be depicted with authentic respect for place, culture, natural environment and hospitality rather than paradise stereotypes.
- Adobe design principles: hierarchy, emphasis, scale, contrast, repetition, rhythm, balance, alignment and negative space must work as one system; pop is controlled contrast and movement, not decoration count.

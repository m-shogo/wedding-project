This file is intentionally more explicit than `RURUBU-V10-PAGE-RECIPES.md`. It exists to reduce Figma improvisation. Measurements are compositional guidance, not printer-template law; live content and physical proof may require adjustment.

General coordinate convention:
- x = distance from left trim edge;
- y = distance from top trim edge;
- critical copy normally remains inside ~6 mm trim-safe zone;
- decorative art may enter bleed;
- measurements describe target visual territory, not rigid component rectangles.

# P01 — COVER

## PAGE_JOB
Within 3 seconds, communicate: `This is a joyful travel-magazine wedding special about Shogo & Shiori, dated 2026.10.24, and it looks worth opening.`

## EDITORIAL_CONCEPT
`Japanese travel-magazine special issue cover`.

Not a luxury wedding invitation. Not a landing-page hero.

## READING ORDER
1. `WEDDING` / Rurubu-Wedding identity.
2. Couple hero image.
3. Shogo & Shiori / date / wedding-special identity.
4. 3–5 editorial coverline hooks.
5. Small travel-photo discoveries and travel objects.

## DENSITY
`VERY_HIGH_IMPACT`, with a readable hero silhouette.

Density peak:
- upper-middle title zone;
- lower-left/lower-right editorial clusters.

Calm zone:
- selected sky/background/photo negative space around the couple;
- tiny factual text must not sit over chaotic florals.

## GEOMETRY
Suggested territories:
- identity mark: x 6–28 mm / y 6–32 mm;
- hero title: x 24–144 mm / y 18–72 mm;
- couple hero: x 34–140 mm / y 60–180 mm;
- stacked coverlines: x 4–48 mm / y 74–154 mm;
- support-photo cluster: x 4–78 mm / y 154–207 mm;
- date/name/CTA accent: x 72–146 mm / y 156–202 mm.

Do not interpret these as equal boxes. Objects may overlap and cross these territories.

## HERO PHOTO
- couple photo/cutout;
- target visual area roughly 45–60% of page;
- may overlap title territory;
- faces must remain clear;
- current proxy may be lower-res, but mask must remain replaceable.

## SUPPORT PHOTOS
3–5 total.

Use a mix such as:
- 1 landscape polaroid;
- 1 smaller tilted print;
- 1 destination/venue photo;
- optional 1–2 tiny travel snapshots.

Never make the support photos equal width and equally spaced.

## EDITORIAL_PARTS
Required or strongly preferred:
- `TITLE / WEDDING HERO`;
- `IDENTITY / ORIGINAL RURUBU-WEDDING MARK`;
- `NAME RIBBON / Shogo & Shiori`;
- `BADGE / DATE OR COUNTDOWN`;
- 3–5 coverline labels;
- 2–4 floral/travel clusters;
- optional airplane/route gesture;
- optional lower photo-junction flowers;
- one short welcoming editorial phrase.

## OVERLAP_PLAN
- couple may overlap lower edge of title;
- flowers may bridge photo frame + page edge;
- one lower support photo may overlap another by ~3–8 mm;
- one ribbon/badge may cross a photo edge;
- coverline stack may approach left trim.

## ROTATION_PLAN
- max 2–3 support frames rotated;
- typical ±1.5–4°;
- hero couple and hero title remain stable unless the specific title art already contains motion.

## NATIVE_TEXT
Keep editable:
- `Shogo & Shiori` if not part of approved hero art;
- `2026.10.24`;
- venue name;
- coverline factual copy;
- any destination/feature names.

## RASTER_ART
May be raster/composed:
- hero display title;
- original identity mark;
- badges/stamps;
- floral/travel decorative clusters;
- decorative handwritten microcopy.

## Z_ORDER
background
→ venue/scenery background
→ couple/photo masks
→ photo frames
→ hero title
→ coverlines/native facts
→ badges/florals/travel micro-decor.

## FORBIDDEN
- one centered title + one centered photo + large whitespace;
- bottom row of three identical cards;
- coverlines rendered as software navigation pills;
- every object centered;
- factual date/name trapped inside a non-editable generated page image.

## QA
Pass when:
- thumbnail instantly reads `WEDDING travel magazine`;
- title and couple are first/second read, not six equal elements;
- left/right supporting clusters add energy without hiding faces;
- factual text remains editable;
- support-photo scale visibly varies;
- edge tension exists in at least two zones;
- no generic UI-card silhouette dominates.

# P02 — PROFILE

## PAGE_JOB
Let a guest understand both people in under 30 seconds, then reward closer reading with personality details.

## EDITORIAL_CONCEPT
`Two travel protagonists introduced by a magazine editor`.

## READING ORDER
1. page title `ふたりのプロフィール` / equivalent;
2. Shogo and Shiori portrait pair;
3. names + core facts;
4. personality/hobby microfacts;
5. one playful comparison or mini-story discovery.

## DENSITY
`MEDIUM_HIGH`.

Calm zone must exist around factual profile text.

## GEOMETRY
Suggested territories:
- page title: x 18–140 / y 6–31 mm;
- Shogo portrait: x 10–69 / y 35–104 mm;
- Shiori portrait: x 76–138 / y 31–102 mm;
- core facts: x 8–139 / y 102–150 mm;
- comparison/microfeature: x 8–140 / y 150–204 mm.

Asymmetry rule:
- portraits may differ by ~5–15% scale and slight angle;
- facts can share a reading baseline but should not become two corporate cards.

## HERO_PHOTO
No single hero. Use a `DUAL_EDITORIAL_HERO` portrait pair with equal editorial respect.

## SUPPORT PHOTOS
0–3 small supporting images, only if they add real personality/story value.

## EDITORIAL_PARTS
- page-title art;
- two portrait frame treatments;
- name scripts/labels;
- compact fact labels;
- 2–4 arrows/microcomments;
- 1 comparison feature such as `編集部が勝手に比較` or `BEST 5`;
- 1–2 travel/floral support clusters.

## OVERLAP_PLAN
- name labels may overlap portrait frame lower edge;
- small icon/flower may bridge portrait and fact area;
- microcomment arrows may point toward specific photo details;
- bottom comparison feature can slightly overlap the fact area boundary.

## ROTATION_PLAN
- one portrait frame may be ~+2° and the other ~-1°;
- avoid mirrored symmetry.

## NATIVE_TEXT
All factual profile information stays native:
- birthday;
- hometown;
- job;
- hobbies;
- favorites;
- labels;
- comparison values.

## RASTER_ART
- page title plate;
- portrait frames;
- small icons;
- handwritten callout graphics.

## FORBIDDEN
- employee-profile UI;
- two identical rounded cards;
- pink/blue color as the only person identifier;
- rasterized profile facts;
- perfectly mirrored left/right composition.

## QA
Pass when:
- names and faces can be found instantly;
- each person feels equally important but not cloned;
- facts can be edited without touching frames;
- bottom microfeature gives one extra reason to browse;
- there are at least two visible differences in portrait/frame treatment.

# P03 — Q&A

## PAGE_JOB
Make the couple feel playful and human while 4–6 questions remain immediately scannable and answers comfortable to read.

## EDITORIAL_CONCEPT
`Magazine interview / playful investigation`.

## READING ORDER
1. Q&A title;
2. numbered question anchors;
3. answer text;
4. small supporting photo/object jokes.

## DENSITY
`MEDIUM_HIGH / PLAYFUL`.

The answer fields are calm; the question labels/edges carry the energy.

## GEOMETRY
Use a staggered 2-column field, not a strict grid.

Suggested anchors:
- title: y 6–29 mm;
- Q1/Q2 zone: y 33–82 mm;
- Q3/Q4 zone: y 82–139 mm;
- Q5/Q6 zone: y 139–201 mm.

Within each row, vary x/width by roughly 4–12 mm so the page does not read as six cloned modules.

## QUESTION SYSTEM
Each question shares semantic consistency:
- visible `Q1–Q6` label;
- question title;
- answer field;
- optional associated motif.

But visual wrappers can vary among:
- burst label;
- tape/brush label;
- small flag;
- speech bubble edge;
- no container + strong number;
- photo-adjacent caption.

## SUPPORT VISUALS
1–3 total:
- one couple cutout/photo;
- one travel/food/photo detail;
- small corgi/house/camera/object illustration if appropriate.

## OVERLAP_PLAN
- question number can cross answer-field edge;
- small motif may sit partly inside/partly outside an answer field;
- one support visual may interrupt the repeated rhythm.

## ROTATION_PLAN
- question labels may vary slightly;
- answer fields themselves should remain stable enough for reading.

## NATIVE_TEXT
All questions and answers remain native and editable.

## RASTER_ART
- title plate;
- Q-number bursts;
- decorative motifs;
- speech-bubble outlines if desired.

## FORBIDDEN
- six identical rounded cards;
- answer copy rasterized into decoration;
- every question using a different saturated color with no hierarchy;
- tiny text to preserve oversized decoration;
- symmetrical 3×2 software settings grid.

## QA
Pass when:
- all question numbers can be found in 2–3 seconds;
- repeated structure is clear but not cloned;
- answers remain readable at A5 actual size;
- at least one interruption breaks the grid rhythm;
- support visuals do not reduce answer readability.

# P04 — STORY

## PAGE_JOB
Create an emotional pause where guests read the relationship as a story, not as a chronology database.

## EDITORIAL_CONCEPT
`Opening feature / short travel essay about two people`.

## READING ORDER
1. emotional story title;
2. one hero photo or key sentence;
3. 2–3 narrative blocks;
4. 1–2 supporting photos;
5. small editorial labels such as `FIRST DATE`, `MEMORABLE DAY`.

## DENSITY
`MEDIUM`.

This page intentionally breathes more than P01/P03/P05.

## GEOMETRY
Suggested:
- title/hero intro: y 6–37 mm;
- hero photo: x 8–140 / y 34–106 mm;
- narrative field: x 10–139 / y 104–190 mm;
- supporting photos/callouts: use edges of narrative field, not its central reading line.

Alternative if current live story structure is stronger:
- hero on left ~60–72% width;
- text calm column on right ~42–52 mm;
- small supporting photo crosses lower boundary.

## HERO_PHOTO
1 emotional anchor, roughly 55–80% page width or equivalent visual mass.

## SUPPORT PHOTOS
1–2.

Use different geometry from hero:
- small portrait print;
- circular crop;
- tilted detail.

## EDITORIAL_PARTS
- story title;
- one emotional deck/subtitle;
- 2–3 small editorial labels;
- 1–2 photo captions;
- 1–3 restrained decorative accents.

## OVERLAP_PLAN
- title may touch/overlap hero photo edge;
- one small photo may intrude into text-zone margin but not line measure;
- label may sit across a frame corner.

## NATIVE_TEXT
All story paragraphs, dates and labels remain native.

## RASTER_ART
Only title/decorative labels/frames.

## FORBIDDEN
- another timeline stepper;
- three equal story cards;
- overdecorating the calm reading field;
- body text directly on busy photography;
- chronological dates dominating emotional story.

## QA
Pass when:
- page is visibly calmer than P03/P05;
- the emotional anchor can be identified at thumbnail scale;
- narrative can be read top-to-bottom without hunting;
- decorative travel objects stay subordinate to the relationship story.

# P05 — TIMELINE + MEMORY

## PAGE_JOB
Make the relationship chronology unmistakable while keeping the page tactile, personal and travel-editorial rather than corporate.

## EDITORIAL_CONCEPT
`Travel route through the relationship history`.

## READING ORDER
1. timeline/history title;
2. chronological route/spine;
3. dates;
4. milestone descriptions;
5. selected memory-photo interruptions;
6. wedding endpoint.

## DENSITY
`HIGH / SEQUENTIAL`.

## GEOMETRY
Avoid a perfect centered vertical roadmap.

Preferred behavior:
- route enters upper-left or upper-center;
- bends through 4–7 milestones;
- alternates left/right text/photo anchors;
- final wedding milestone gains extra scale/contrast near lower-right/lower-center.

Suggested chronological corridor:
- x 18–128 mm / y 35–196 mm;
- route itself may snake within a ~40–70 mm-wide central corridor;
- photo clusters can sit outside it.

## ROUTE SYSTEM
The route must be semantically chronological.

Allowed:
- dotted travel path;
- illustrated route;
- subtle airplane/heart/pin nodes;
- numbered/date milestones.

Do not make a decorative route that contradicts reading order.

## SUPPORT PHOTOS
2–4.

Roles:
- one early-memory photo;
- one relationship/travel highlight;
- one pre-wedding highlight;
- optional wedding/venue endpoint visual.

At least one photo should interrupt the route, not sit in a separate gallery row.

## EDITORIAL_PARTS
- timeline title;
- 4–7 date labels;
- milestone captions;
- route/pins;
- 2–4 frames;
- 1–3 stamps/flowers/camera accents;
- one emphasized final milestone.

## OVERLAP_PLAN
- photo may cross route line;
- route can pass visually behind a photo frame;
- flowers/stamp may anchor a major milestone junction;
- final milestone badge can overlap endpoint photo.

## NATIVE_TEXT
All dates and milestone facts stay native.

## RASTER_ART
- route art;
- pins/stamps;
- title;
- frame art.

## FORBIDDEN
- SaaS roadmap;
- identical milestone nodes;
- one straight line with equal spacing and equal cards;
- color-coded milestones with no date hierarchy;
- route purely ornamental/unrelated to chronology.

## QA
Pass when:
- order is understandable without reading every description;
- dates visually outrank long copy;
- one milestone is intentionally strongest;
- photos add memory/emotion without blocking sequence;
- the page would not be mistaken for a project-management timeline.

# P06 — MEMORY SPOTS + GALLERY

## PAGE_JOB
Feel most like an actual travel-feature page: guests should browse memorable places first and then enjoy the photos/stories.

## EDITORIAL_CONCEPT
`Rurubu-style destination feature starring the couple's memories`.

## READING ORDER
1. memory-spots title;
2. one dominant destination/hero image;
3. 2–4 satellite destinations/photos;
4. destination labels + microstories;
5. optional small map/number route;
6. `BEST SHOT` style discovery.

## DENSITY
`HIGH / PHOTO_LED`.

Photography carries more visual weight than decoration.

## GEOMETRY
Suggested:
- title: y 6–29 mm;
- hero photo: x 9–88 / y 36–112 mm OR x 8–140 / y 38–105 mm depending on source;
- satellites: occupy right-middle + lower half in intentionally mixed sizes;
- optional map strip/mini-map: lower-left or lower-right, ~35–60 mm region;
- captions stay attached to respective photo.

Hero should be roughly 1.6–2.5× the visual area of the smallest satellite photo.

## SUPPORT PHOTOS
3–6 total including hero.

Use at least three different photo behaviors:
- large landscape hero;
- tilted polaroid;
- portrait/square;
- circular/organic crop;
- stacked mini prints.

## EDITORIAL_PARTS
- memory-spots title;
- one `BEST SHOT`/editor pick badge maximum;
- destination labels;
- 1–4 numbered pins if a map is used;
- 1 map/route max;
- 2–4 floral/travel/photo-junction clusters;
- camera/suitcase/plane only where semantically helpful.

## OVERLAP_PLAN
- two support frames may overlap;
- one floral cluster may bridge frame junction;
- map/pin can sit beneath photo cluster;
- `BEST SHOT` badge may partially cross a selected photo.

## ROTATION_PLAN
- max ~2–3 support photos rotated ±2–4°;
- hero remains mostly stable.

## NATIVE_TEXT
- destination names;
- captions;
- personal memory snippets;
- ranking/number labels where factual;
- map labels.

## RASTER_ART
- title plate;
- frames;
- decorative map base;
- non-authoritative badge art;
- flowers/travel icons.

## FORBIDDEN
- four equal destination cards;
- same border on every image;
- detached captions;
- map used only as wallpaper;
- stickers more prominent than memories;
- full-page generated image as sole production master.

## QA
Pass when:
- one photo clearly dominates;
- supporting images visibly vary in shape/scale;
- page reads as a travel feature at thumbnail scale;
- destination labels are attached correctly;
- images remain replaceable;
- there is at least one micro-discovery and at least one calm caption field.

# P07 — 1DAY + CAFE TABLE

## PAGE_JOB
Make the wedding-day sequence easy to follow while maintaining travel-magazine energy and integrating food/table/cafe visuals as editorial rewards.

## EDITORIAL_CONCEPT
`One-day model course / itinerary feature`.

## READING ORDER
1. `1DAY` page title;
2. chronological times/stops;
3. key ceremony/reception moments;
4. strongest food/table/dress/venue visual;
5. practical side information / special thanks.

## DENSITY
`HIGH / UTILITY`.

## GEOMETRY
Preferred two-system composition, not two generic columns:

- itinerary spine occupies roughly x 10–86 mm, y 36–196 mm;
- visual reward/utility cluster occupies roughly x 88–141 mm, y 45–193 mm;
- title spans/bridges upper width;
- one image or callout may interrupt the spine to avoid table-like monotony.

## ITINERARY SYSTEM
Each stop contains:
- time;
- stop/event name;
- optional icon;
- optional microcaption.

Time labels may repeat visual structure because chronology is a real semantic repetition.

Vary at least one or two stop treatments for special events (ceremony, cake, closing) using scale/badge/visual, not arbitrary colors.

## SUPPORT VISUALS
2–5 total:
- food/table hero;
- venue/detail;
- rings/cake/dress code;
- travel/wedding small image.

## EDITORIAL_PARTS
- 1DAY title;
- route/spine;
- 4–8 time labels according to confirmed schedule;
- small icons;
- one secondary utility feature e.g. dress code / special thanks / food highlight;
- 1–3 edge decorations.

## OVERLAP_PLAN
- selected photo may break into itinerary corridor;
- icon may sit on/over route node;
- title decorations may span both systems;
- lower note may overlap a small image edge.

## NATIVE_TEXT
All confirmed times, event names, venue/useful notes and thanks remain native.

## RASTER_ART
- title;
- route art;
- icons;
- dress/food illustrations when not factual photography;
- decorative frames.

## FORBIDDEN
- fake subway-map complexity;
- route dots with no actual sequence meaning;
- itinerary as spreadsheet/table UI;
- tiny times caused by oversized decoration;
- right half as disconnected dashboard card.

## QA
Pass when:
- guest can follow sequence in under 10 seconds;
- time labels are visually consistent;
- page still has one or two memorable visual moments;
- all times remain editable;
- utility cluster feels integrated, not bolted on.

# P08 — BACK COVER

## PAGE_JOB
Close the booklet warmly, clearly and with enough identity that the publication feels finished rather than abruptly stopped.

## EDITORIAL_CONCEPT
`Travel-journal closing page / postcard home`.

## READING ORDER
1. closing message or image;
2. short thank-you / journey line;
3. date/name/venue reprise;
4. small utility/social/photo-contest element if still desired;
5. final travel-object cluster.

## DENSITY
`MEDIUM / QUIET`.

Density may live at perimeter/lower edge while the message field remains calm.

## GEOMETRY
Suggested:
- closing title/message: y 12–50 mm;
- calm message/photo field: y 48–145 mm;
- lower utility/CTA: y 145–202 mm;
- decorative clusters at corners and lower-right/lower-left.

Alternative strong behavior from approved reference:
- large calm lined/postcard-like message field occupying upper ~60%;
- lower photo-contest/social/travel cluster.

## SUPPORT VISUALS
1–3.

Prefer:
- one closing photo or postcard field;
- one camera/passport/ticket/suitcase still-life cluster;
- optional small support snapshot.

## EDITORIAL_PARTS
- closing title or handwritten message;
- date/name reprise;
- postcard/postmark/date stamp;
- optional photo contest/hashtag/guest CTA;
- 1–2 floral clusters;
- travel-object cluster.

## OVERLAP_PLAN
- lower travel objects may overlap each other;
- stamp can partially enter message field edge;
- flowers frame perimeter but do not invade central message copy.

## NATIVE_TEXT
- thank-you message;
- names/date;
- CTA;
- hashtag;
- venue/factual data.

## RASTER_ART
- postcard/stamp decoration;
- frame/line art;
- floral/travel object cluster;
- expressive closing title if verified.

## FORBIDDEN
- leftover-asset junk drawer;
- same density as P01/P07;
- giant title leaving no room for message;
- low-contrast sentimental copy;
- rasterized final guest message that cannot be corrected.

## QA
Pass when:
- page visibly lowers energy from P07;
- message can be read comfortably;
- book identity remains present through edge/closing motifs;
- no leftover section is forced in without a role;
- thumbnail looks like a deliberate back cover.

# Whole-book adjacency contract

The page pair rhythm matters as much as individual pages.

## P01 → P02
Move from maximal impact into structured introduction.
Do not repeat the giant cover hero geometry on P02.

## P02 → P03
Move from profile facts into playful interaction.
P03 should feel more irregular and question-led than P02.

## P03 → P04
Reduce noise. P04 is the emotional reading pause.

## P04 → P05
Move from emotional narrative into explicit chronology.
Do not let P04 accidentally preview the exact same route system as P05.

## P05 → P06
Move from sequence-led information into photo-led travel browsing.
P06 should feel more image-dominant.

## P06 → P07
Move from memories/destinations into practical day flow.
Route semantics may appear on both, but their jobs must differ: destination linking vs time sequence.

## P07 → P08
Reduce density and close.

# Global Figma placement rules compiled from these specs

1. Do not ask Figma to decide page concept from scratch.
2. Before write, state page job and reading order.
3. Establish hero geometry first with 3–6 large semantic objects.
4. Screenshot before adding micro-decoration.
5. If the page looks like equal cards, change geometry rather than adding stickers.
6. Use absolute positioning freely for editorial art/photos; use Auto Layout only where the repeated information truly benefits from it.
7. Keep native copy, masks and frames separate.
8. Preserve replaceability for all provisional photos.
9. Limit rotation to support roles; never rotate everything.
10. Treat decorative edge objects as compositional tools, not filler.
11. Use different photo behavior across adjacent pages.
12. No whole-page flattening.

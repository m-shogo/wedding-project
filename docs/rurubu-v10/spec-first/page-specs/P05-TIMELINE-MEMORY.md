# P05 — TIMELINE + MEMORY Page Manual

## PAGE JOB
Make the relationship chronology unmistakable while keeping it emotional and magazine-like rather than looking like a product roadmap.

## READING PATH
1. FIRST READ: timeline/journey title.
2. SECOND READ: route-like chronological spine.
3. THIRD READ: enlarged milestone/date.
4. USEFUL INFO: short milestone descriptions and memory photos.
5. MICRO DISCOVERY: date stamps, pins, tiny comments, travel-route cues.

## ZONE MAP
- top 0–15%: timeline title.
- body 12–88%: winding/stepped chronological route crossing left/right regions.
- 20–78%: 4–6 milestone clusters attached to the route, unequal in scale.
- lower 68–94%: emotional peak / wedding milestone / larger final photo.

## PHOTO PLAN
- MEMORY_HIGHLIGHT: 1 larger photo tied to the emotional peak.
- MEMORY_SUPPORT: 2–4 smaller photos, mixed postcard/circle/landscape forms.
- not every milestone needs a photo.

## TIMELINE PLAN
Use a clear actual sequence. Route may curve, bend or alternate but must remain easy to follow.
Dates should be stronger than descriptions. One milestone should be deliberately larger than the rest.

## TITLE / TYPOGRAPHY
- journey/timeline title: expressive display.
- dates: bold native editable text.
- milestone copy: short BODY text.
- small notes/captions remain close to corresponding photo/node.

## PARTS INVENTORY
- timeline title;
- semantic route line ×1;
- date markers ×4–6;
- mixed photo frames ×3–5;
- airplane/map pin ×1–2;
- date stamp ×1;
- small camera/heart/travel doodles ×2–4;
- floral edge cluster ×1–2.

## COLOR JOBS
Dominant: paper/cream.
Sequence: cobalt or strong pink.
Highlight: yellow with INK.
Secondary: cyan/mint sparingly.

## OVERLAP / Z-ORDER
paper → route line → photo sources → frames → milestone/date markers → native descriptions → title → foreground travel/doodle accents.

Some photos may visually interrupt the route, but the route must reappear clearly after them.

## DENSITY MAP
High: milestone clusters around route.
Peak: final/emotional milestone.
Calm: short text areas beside dates.

## EDGE TENSION
Route and decorative travel objects may approach outer edges. Photos may tilt toward edges. Important dates/copy stay safe.

## EDITABILITY CONTRACT
NATIVE_EDITABLE: every date and milestone description.
REPLACEABLE_IMAGE: all memory photos.
COMPOSED_DISPLAY_ASSET: title, route style, decorative stamps/objects/frames.

## ANTI-PATTERNS
- five identical timeline nodes;
- corporate vertical stepper;
- route line unrelated to chronology;
- every milestone same size/color;
- photos obscuring sequence;
- dense paragraphs.

## FIGMA EXECUTION
Draw/position chronological spine first and prove order without decoration. Add milestone dates, then photo interruptions, then title and travel accents. Enlarge one emotional peak deliberately.

## QA
- chronology understandable in under 5 seconds;
- route is semantic, not ornamental;
- one milestone is clearly emphasized;
- node geometry varies;
- memory photos support rather than hide sequence;
- dates and copy editable.

# P01 — COVER Page Manual

## PAGE JOB
Within 3 seconds, communicate: `Rurubu WEDDING / Shogo & Shiori / joyful travel wedding / worth opening`.

## READING PATH
1. FIRST READ: `RURUBU WEDDING` hero title.
2. SECOND READ: couple hero photo.
3. THIRD READ: date + names.
4. USEFUL INFO: 3–5 coverline hooks.
5. MICRO DISCOVERY: travel objects, flowers, stamps, small snapshots.

## ZONE MAP
- top 0–28%: oversized title silhouette, slightly left-biased or spanning width; not a centered web hero.
- center 20–78%: couple hero image; allow title/photo overlap.
- left/side 28–72%: stacked coverline labels, irregular lengths.
- bottom 70–96%: 2–3 small travel snapshots / ticket / date cluster.
- corners/bleed: tropical foliage/flowers entering from 2–3 edges.

## PHOTO PLAN
- HERO_COUPLE: 1, ~58–78% page width equivalent, large cutout or irregular crop; source remains replaceable.
- SUPPORT_TRAVEL: 2–3, small mixed horizontal/square postcard treatments; not equal row cards.
- optional CUTOUT_OBJECT/PLACE: 1.

Use different frame treatments. One small photo may tilt -3° to -6°, another +2° to +5°.

## TITLE / TYPOGRAPHY
- hero title may be composed display art inspired by the canonical reference: hot pink + white keyline + yellow/blue support.
- `Shogo & Shiori`, date and factual lines stay native/editable.
- coverlines use short bold Japanese phrases, not tiny decorative English.

## PARTS INVENTORY
- hero title lockup;
- 3–5 coverline ribbons/labels;
- date stamp/badge;
- flower/leaf corner clusters ×2–3;
- airplane ×1;
- camera/suitcase/ticket cluster ×1–2;
- small heart/arrow accents ×2–4;
- mixed postcard/polaroid frames ×2–3.

## COLOR JOBS
Dominant: saturated cyan/travel blue.
Support: hot pink.
Surprise: sunny yellow.
Body/factual text: INK on light plate or sufficiently strong dark field.

## OVERLAP / Z-ORDER
blue/sky field → edge foliage → hero photo → photo frame/cutout keyline → title → native date/name → coverline ribbons → foreground flowers/travel objects.

Allow the title to overlap the hero silhouette and one coverline cluster to touch the photo edge.

## DENSITY MAP
Highest: upper-left/title + side coverlines.
Medium: hero center.
High-small-detail: bottom snapshot/travel cluster.
Calm: a small factual date/name zone.

## EDGE TENSION
Flowers/leaves can crop into bleed at top-left, top-right, bottom-left/right. One bottom travel object may touch trim. Critical text stays safe.

## EDITABILITY CONTRACT
NATIVE_EDITABLE: names, date, coverline copy, factual venue line.
REPLACEABLE_IMAGE: couple hero, support photos.
COMPOSED_DISPLAY_ASSET: hero title, decorative stamp/flower/travel objects.

## ANTI-PATTERNS
- centered title + centered photo + centered CTA-like badge;
- three equal thumbnails along bottom;
- generic rounded cards;
- excessive whitespace;
- every element inside safe centered rectangle;
- hero title and date badge at equal visual weight.

## FIGMA EXECUTION
Construct hero/photo/title skeleton first. Then side coverline cluster. Then bottom snapshot cluster. Add only enough floral/travel parts to create edge framing and micro-discovery. Do not use global Auto Layout for the whole cover.

## QA
- cover purpose readable at thumbnail;
- title is unmistakably first;
- hero photo clearly second;
- at least 3 scale levels visible;
- title/photo overlap intentional;
- small photos not equal cards;
- names/date editable;
- no critical copy in bleed.

# P04 — STORY Page Manual

## PAGE JOB
Create the emotional pause in the booklet: guests should feel the relationship story before they encounter the more structured chronology on P05.

## READING PATH
1. FIRST READ: emotional story title / key sentence.
2. SECOND READ: one dominant relationship photo.
3. THIRD READ: short narrative block.
4. USEFUL INFO: 2–3 episode labels or mini facts.
5. MICRO DISCOVERY: date stamp, heart, camera, tiny travel note.

## ZONE MAP
- top 0–18%: story title, more restrained than cover but still authored.
- upper/center 14–58%: hero photo or overlapping photo pair.
- center/lower 46–86%: calm narrative field with one inset snapshot.
- bottom/edges 78–96%: 1–2 short episode labels / stamp / decorative travel cue.

## PHOTO PLAN
- STORY_HERO: 1, ~55–70% width equivalent, emotional anchor.
- STORY_SUPPORT: 1–2, smaller, different aspect ratio/frame.
- allow one support photo to overlap hero frame edge by a small amount.

Do not turn this page into a chronology stepper. Dates are supporting facts, not the primary structure.

## TITLE / TYPOGRAPHY
- expressive Japanese story title with short English subtitle optional.
- one short pull-quote may be visually enlarged.
- narrative copy stays native and uses a calm cream/paper field.
- use comfortable leading; this page should breathe more than P01/P03.

## PARTS INVENTORY
- story title;
- 2–3 mixed photo frames;
- pull-quote marker/ribbon ×1;
- date/travel stamp ×1;
- hand-drawn heart/underline ×1–3;
- camera/postcard object ×1;
- small floral cluster ×1–2.

## COLOR JOBS
Dominant: warm cream/paper.
Support: coral/pink.
Travel secondary: cyan/mint.
Body: INK.

## OVERLAP / Z-ORDER
paper → subtle edge floral → hero photo source → hero frame → support photo/frame → title → native narrative copy → pull quote/stamp → micro accents.

## DENSITY MAP
High: title + hero photo.
Medium: support photo/episode zone.
Calm: narrative body field.
Lower density overall than P01/P05/P06.

## EDGE TENSION
A hero frame or floral piece may approach/enter outer edge; narrative field must remain safely inset.

## EDITABILITY CONTRACT
NATIVE_EDITABLE: story text, episode labels, dates, pull quote if factual/changeable.
REPLACEABLE_IMAGE: all couple/story photos.
COMPOSED_DISPLAY_ASSET: title, decorative stamp, frames, floral/camera parts.

## ANTI-PATTERNS
- corporate timeline;
- multiple identical story cards;
- every paragraph inside a box;
- too many travel stickers competing with the people;
- text over busy photo;
- density equal to every other page.

## FIGMA EXECUTION
Build title + hero photo first. Establish one generous calm body-copy field. Add only one or two support snapshots and one small episode cluster. Introduce overlap to connect photo/title, not to clutter the reading field.

## QA
- page feels emotional before informational;
- hero photo clearly dominates;
- body copy comfortable at actual A5 size;
- chronology is not the main visual grammar;
- adjacent P03/P05 rhythm is visibly different;
- photos remain replaceable.
